## The 8 tool descriptions

### `kernel_declare`

Law cited: `c7_pin_well_founded`

> Mint an immutable value through the certifier (license: c7_pin_well_founded — references resolve only to already-admitted digests). The value's canonical bytes become its digest, its name forever. There is no update: revision is a successor declaration pinning its predecessor.

### `kernel_resolve`

Law cited: `content addressing`, `verify-on-read`

> Digest to value, verify-on-read (license: content addressing — the decode re-derives the digest of what it fetched and refuses on mismatch). No anchor parameter exists: a digest names one value forever, so nothing an anchor could change. Head-relative reads are kernel_fold.

### `kernel_emit`

Law cited: `f2_trace_invariant`

> Attributed evidence onto a lane (license: f2_trace_invariant — duplication and reordering of deliveries cannot move the folded state). Not a send: no point-to-point primitive exists; lanes mean, subjects route.

### `kernel_join`

Law cited: `f1_cell_merge_aci`, `join-semilattice`
Refusal reasons named: `last-writer-wins`

> Merge a contribution into a lattice cell (license: f1_cell_merge_aci and the join-semilattice package — associative, commutative, idempotent, so arrival order and duplication cannot matter). No ordering parameter and no conflict strategy exist; idempotent join leaves nothing to choose (refusal: last-writer-wins, machine-applicable repair).

### `kernel_fold`

Law cited: `f11_query_deterministic`, `f3_resume_exact`

> Read reduced state at a checkpoint (license: f3_resume_exact for resumption; f11_query_deterministic — the answer is a function of support and query alone). Every head-relative read is this tool: search, views, rosters, audits. The anchor names the position the answer is true at; it is never wrong later, only earlier.

### `kernel_decide`

Law cited: `at_most_one_landed_commit`

> The fenced one-winner commit (license: at_most_one_landed_commit over monotone tokens — at most one outcome lands per register). The one priced act. At most one landed outcome is not at most one external side effect.

### `kernel_trigger`

Law cited: `f10_stability`
Refusal reasons named: `absence-trigger`

> Deposit a standing monotone reaction (license: f10_stability — a predicate that holds at a state holds at every grown state; an enabled firing never un-fires). Five productions exist; absence, negation, and deadline are unspellable (refusal: absence-trigger).

### `kernel_spawn`

Law cited: `f9_policy_meet_semilattice`, `f9_tree_attenuation`

> Derive a narrower writ (license: f9_policy_meet_semilattice and f9_tree_attenuation — the child is the meet of parent and request, and every descendant stays under the root grant). An escalating request is clamped, not refused.

## The 3 `$comment` paragraphs

### `/`

> The kernel language projected as MCP tool input schemas. EXPLORATORY, hand-derived from the Lean model in verify/kernel at this commit; the design mandates projections be GENERATED from the one grammar and walled served-equals-derived — this file is the reference sketch that generation owes. Shapes follow the survey leans: eight flat tools, no oneOf, compound self-descriptive field names (a field is named for the value it carries), digests as prefixed opaque strings with pattern, integers only (I-JSON safe range; no floats exist in the canonical grammar). Constraints beyond type/enum may not be sampler-enforced by every provider; the admission door enforces them all, and a violation returns the taught refusal (see refusal_result).

### `/refusal_result`

> Every tool returns either its admitted result or this refusal shape. Refusal parity: the door never refuses without the law it defends and the taught repair; applicability marks whether the repair is a mechanical rewrite of the refused call (machine-applicable) or needs information the call does not carry (advisory).
