# Extended Solution

## Step 1: Set up coordinate system
Place $B$ at the origin and $A$ at distance 1:
- $B = (0, 0)$
- $A = (1, 0)$

## Step 2: Find $A'$ after first rotation
Rotating point $A = (1, 0)$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B = (0, 0)$:
$$A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 3: Verify distance $A'B$
By the property of rotation (distance preservation):
$$|A'B| = |AB| = 1$$ ✓

## Step 4: Find $B'$ after second rotation
Rotate $B = (0, 0)$ **clockwise** by angle $\theta$ around $A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$.

**Translation to origin:** $B - A' = (-\cos\theta, -\sin\theta)$

**Apply clockwise rotation matrix:**
$$R_{-\theta} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ -\sin\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta \\ \sin\theta\cos\theta - \sin\theta\cos\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

**Translate back:**
$$B' = A' + \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 5: Apply the distance constraint $AB' = \frac{4}{3}$

Calculate $|AB'|^2$ where $A = (1, 0)$ and $B' = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$:

$$|AB'|^2 = [1 - (\cos\theta - 1)]^2 + (0 - \sin\theta)^2$$
$$= (2 - \cos\theta)^2 + \sin^2\theta$$
$$= 4 - 4\cos\theta + \cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta$$

Using $\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$:
$$|AB'|^2 = 4 - 4\cos\theta + 1 = 5 - 4\cos\theta$$

Setting this equal to $\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{16}{9}$:
$$5 - 4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9}$$

## Step 6: Solve for $\cos\theta$

$$4\cos\theta = 5 - \frac{16}{9}$$
$$4\cos\theta = \frac{45 - 16}{9}$$
$$4\cos\theta = \frac{29}{9}$$
$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$

**Verification:** $\frac{29}{36} \approx 0.806$, which is valid for an acute angle ✓

## Step 7: Express in required form

$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$

Check $\gcd(29, 36)$:
- $29$ is prime
- $36 = 2^2 \times 3^2$
- Therefore $\gcd(29, 36) = 1$ ✓

Thus $m = 29$ and $n = 36$.

$$m + n = 29 + 36 = 65$$

**65**
