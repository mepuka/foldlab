# Solution Plan for Finding Possible Values of M

1. **Rewrite the constraint equation**
   - Factor $\alpha\beta + \alpha + \beta + 100 = 0$ as $(\alpha + 1)(\beta + 1) = -99$
   - Substitute $w = \alpha + 1$ and $z = \beta + 1$ to get $wz = -99$

2. **Express conditions in terms of w and z**
   - Convert the modulus constraints to: $|w - 1| = M$ and $|z - 1| = M$
   - Use $z = -\frac{99}{w}$ to obtain: $|w - 1| = M$ and $|99 + w| = M|w|$

3. **Set up geometric equations**
   - Let $w = x + iy$ with real $x, y$
   - From condition 1: $(x-1)^2 + y^2 = M^2$ (circle centered at $(1,0)$ with radius $M$)
   - From condition 2: $(x+99)^2 + y^2 = M^2(x^2 + y^2)$ (another geometric constraint)

4. **Eliminate y and derive constraint on M**
   - Eliminate $y^2$ from the two equations using $y^2 = M^2 + 1 - 2x$
   - Simplify to obtain: $x^2(1 - M^2) + x(196 + 2M^2) + 9802 - M^4 = 0$

5. **Test the special case where α = β**
   - If $\alpha = \beta$, then $\alpha^2 + 2\alpha + 100 = 0$
   - Solve: $\alpha = -1 \pm 3i\sqrt{11}$
   - Calculate: $M = |\alpha| = \sqrt{1 + 99} = 10$

6. **Verify uniqueness**
   - Confirm that $M = 10$ satisfies all original constraints
   - Verify through the constraint equations that this is the only possible value

**Answer: $M = 10$**
