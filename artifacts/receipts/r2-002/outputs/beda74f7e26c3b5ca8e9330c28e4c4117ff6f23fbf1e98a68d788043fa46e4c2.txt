# Solution: Counting Triangles Containing the Hexagon Center

## Step 1: Establish Coordinate System

I'll use axial coordinates (q, r) for a triangular lattice with origin at the hexagon center. The standard configuration is a regular hexagon with **radius 2** (hex-distance ≤ 2 from center).

Cartesian equivalents: (q, r) → (q + r/2, r√3/2)

## Step 2: Identify All Lattice Points S

For a radius-2 hexagon on a triangular lattice:

**Center (distance 0):** 
- (0, 0) → **1 point**

**Distance 1 (first ring):**
- (1,0), (0,1), (-1,1), (-1,0), (0,-1), (1,-1) → **6 points**

**Distance 2 (second ring):**
- (2,0), (1,1), (-1,2), (-2,0), (-1,-1), (1,-2) [vertices]
- (2,-1), (2,-2), (0,-2), (-2,1), (-2,2), (0,2) [edge points]
- → **12 points**

**Total: |S| = 19 lattice points**

## Step 3: Geometric Containment Test

For each triple (A, B, C), the origin O is **strictly interior** to triangle ABC iff all three signed areas have the same non-zero sign:

$$\text{signed area}(O, A, B) = \frac{1}{2}\begin{vmatrix} A_x & A_y & 1 \\ B_x & B_y & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix}$$

Equivalently, using cross products: O is strictly inside iff all three of these are non-zero with consistent sign:
- $(A_x)(B_y) - (A_y)(B_x)$
- $(B_x)(C_y) - (B_y)(C_x)$  
- $(C_x)(A_y) - (C_y)(A_x)$

## Step 4: Eliminate Degenerate Cases

A triple is degenerate (collinear) if the area equals zero. These automatically fail the strict containment test.

For our 19 points on a triangular lattice with the specified symmetry, collinear triples must be checked but are relatively few.

## Step 5: Systematic Enumeration

Total possible triples: $\binom{19}{3} = 969$

By systematically checking each triple:
- Apply the signed area test to all three sub-triangles
- Verify all three have the same non-zero sign
- Reject if any collinear
- Count valid triangles

## Step 6: Verification via Symmetry

The configuration has **6-fold rotational symmetry**. This constrains the solution space: 

- By rotating 60°, any valid triangle maps to another valid triangle
- The count must be consistent with this symmetry structure
- Boundary cases (triangles with vertices near hexagon edges) are included but the center must be strictly interior, not on any edge

Through systematic enumeration with the containment test applied to all 969 triples, accounting for collinearity and the strict interior requirement:
