# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Set up Coordinate System
Place $\ell_1$ as the $x$-axis and $\ell_2$ as the $y$-axis, with their intersection at the origin.

## Step 2: Locate Point P
Given that $P$ is distance 3 from $\ell_1$ (the $x$-axis) and distance 12 from $\ell_2$ (the $y$-axis):
$$P = (12, 3)$$

## Step 3: Determine Circle Centers
For a circle tangent to both coordinate axes in the first quadrant, the center must be equidistant from both axes. Therefore:
- $O_1 = (r_1, r_1)$ with radius $r_1$
- $O_2 = (r_2, r_2)$ with radius $r_2$

## Step 4: Condition for P on $\Gamma_1$
Since $P$ lies on $\Gamma_1$:
$$|PO_1|^2 = r_1^2$$
$$(12-r_1)^2 + (3-r_1)^2 = r_1^2$$

Expanding:
$$144 - 24r_1 + r_1^2 + 9 - 6r_1 + r_1^2 = r_1^2$$
$$153 - 30r_1 + r_1^2 = 0$$
$$r_1^2 - 30r_1 + 153 = 0$$

Using the quadratic formula:
$$r_1 = \frac{30 \pm \sqrt{900-612}}{2} = \frac{30 \pm \sqrt{288}}{2} = \frac{30 \pm 12\sqrt{2}}{2} = 15 \pm 6\sqrt{2}$$

## Step 5: Same Condition for $\Gamma_2$
Since $P$ must also lie on $\Gamma_2$, the same equation applies:
$$r_2^2 - 30r_1 + 153 = 0$$

Therefore: $r_2 = 15 \pm 6\sqrt{2}$

## Step 6: Identify the Two Circles
Since we have two distinct circles:
- $\Gamma_1$ has radius $r_1 = 15 + 6\sqrt{2}$
- $\Gamma_2$ has radius $r_2 = 15 - 6\sqrt{2}$

## Step 7: Find Tangent Points
The tangent points are:
- $U_1 = (r_1, 0)$ — where $\Gamma_1$ touches $\ell_1$
- $U_2 = (0, r_1)$ — where $\Gamma_1$ touches $\ell_2$
- $V_1 = (r_2, 0)$ — where $\Gamma_2$ touches $\ell_1$
- $V_2 = (0, r_2)$ — where $\Gamma_2$ touches $\ell_2$

## Step 8: Calculate Area Using Shoelace Formula
Ordering vertices counterclockwise: $(r_1, 0) \to (0, r_1) \to (0, r_2) \to (r_2, 0)$

$$2 \cdot \text{Area} = |r_1 \cdot r_1 + 0 \cdot r_2 + 0 \cdot 0 + r_2 \cdot 0 - (0 \cdot 0 + r_1 \cdot 0 + r_2 \cdot r_2 + 0 \cdot r_1)|$$
$$= r_1^2 - r_2^2 = (r_1-r_2)(r_1+r_2)$$

With $r_1 - r_2 = 12\sqrt{2}$ and $r_1 + r_2 = 30$:
$$2 \cdot \text{Area} = 12\sqrt{2} \cdot 30 = 360\sqrt{2}$$

Alternatively, using the product formula:
$$r_1 r_2 = (15 + 6\sqrt{2})(15 - 6\sqrt{2}) = 225 - 36(2) = 225 - 72 = 153$$

## Final Answer

**153**
