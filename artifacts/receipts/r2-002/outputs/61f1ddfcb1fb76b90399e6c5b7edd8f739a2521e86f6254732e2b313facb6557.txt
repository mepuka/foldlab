# Solution: Maximizing Sum of Adjacent Differences in 8×8 Grid

## Step 1: Understand the Objective
We need to arrange numbers 1-64 in an 8×8 grid to maximize the sum of absolute differences between all adjacent pairs (horizontally and vertically adjacent cells).

## Step 2: Count Total Adjacencies
- **Horizontal adjacencies:** 8 rows × 7 adjacencies per row = 56
- **Vertical adjacencies:** 7 rows of adjacencies × 8 columns = 56
- **Total adjacencies:** 56 + 56 = 112

## Step 3: Graph Theory Interpretation
The grid is a graph where:
- Vertices = 64 cells
- Edges = 112 adjacencies (horizontal + vertical)
- Goal: Assign values 1-64 to maximize sum of |value differences| on edges

## Step 4: Optimal Strategy - Checkerboard Pattern
To maximize differences, place numbers so that adjacent cells contain values as far apart as possible.

**Key insight:** Use a checkerboard coloring (like a chess board with black and white squares).
- **Black squares (32 cells):** Place numbers {1, 2, ..., 32}
- **White squares (32 cells):** Place numbers {33, 34, ..., 64}

This ensures every edge connects a number from {1-32} with a number from {33-64}.

## Step 5: Calculate Minimum Difference
With the checkerboard strategy:
- Minimum possible difference on an edge: 33 - 32 = 1
- Maximum possible difference on an edge: 64 - 1 = 63

## Step 6: Optimal Pairing Within Strategy
To maximize the total, pair numbers optimally:
- Pair 1 with 64 (difference = 63)
- Pair 2 with 63 (difference = 61)
- Pair 3 with 62 (difference = 59)
- ...
- Pair 32 with 33 (difference = 1)

**Average difference:** $\frac{63 + 61 + 59 + ... + 1}{32} = \frac{1024}{32} = 32$

## Step 7: Calculate Maximum Sum
Each of the 112 edges connects cells of opposite checkerboard colors.

If we can achieve an average difference of 32 per edge:
$$M_{\max} = 112 \times 32 = 3584$$

This is achievable by carefully arranging the 32 small numbers on one color and 32 large numbers on the other color, with optimal internal pairings.

## Step 8: Final Answer
$$M_{\max} \pmod{1000} = 3584 \pmod{1000} = \boxed{584}$$
