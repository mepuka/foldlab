# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

1. **Grid structure**: A row of 10 squares arranged consecutively
2. **Diagonals**: Each square has a diagonal connecting its lower left vertex to its upper right vertex
3. **Starting point (A)**: Lower left corner at position (0, 0)
4. **Ending point (B)**: Upper right corner at position (10, 1)
5. **Movement constraints**:
   - The bug travels along line segments from vertex to vertex
   - Each segment can be traversed **at most once** (never twice)
   - **Never moves from right to left** along horizontal or diagonal segments
6. **Example**: One valid path is shown in the figure

## What is Being Asked:

Find $\sqrt{N}$, where $N$ is the total number of distinct valid paths the bug can take from $A$ to $B$.
