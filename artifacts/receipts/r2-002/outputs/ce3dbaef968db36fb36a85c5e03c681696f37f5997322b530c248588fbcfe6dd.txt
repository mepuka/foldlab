# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

1. **Starting shape:** An 8 × 8 square (total area = 64 square units)
2. **Number of rectangles:** Exactly 3 rectangles
3. **Constraint on rectangles:** Each rectangle must have positive integer side lengths
4. **Constraint on division:** The three rectangles must completely partition the square (cover it without overlap)

## What is Being Asked:

Count the total number of distinct ways to partition an 8 × 8 square into exactly 3 non-overlapping rectangles, where each rectangle has positive integer dimensions.

---

**Note:** The problem implicitly requires that:
- The three rectangles tile the entire 8 × 8 square with no gaps or overlaps
- Two divisions are considered the same if and only if they result in the same geometric partition (rectangles in different positions count as different divisions)
