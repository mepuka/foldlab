# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Divisibility condition**: $n$ is divisible by 101
2. **Digit sequence condition**: The decimal representation of $n$ contains "2026" as a consecutive sequence of digits
3. **Positivity condition**: $n$ is a positive integer

## What is Being Asked

Find the **second smallest positive integer** $n$ that satisfies both conditions above.

(In other words: identify all positive integers satisfying both conditions, order them from smallest to largest, and report the one in the second position.)
