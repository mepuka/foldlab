# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities:
- Triangle $\triangle ABC$ with side lengths:
  - $AB = 13$
  - $BC = 14$
  - $CA = 15$

## Given Conditions:
1. $\triangle A'B'C'$ is obtained by rotating $\triangle ABC$ about its circumcenter
2. After rotation, $\overline{A'C'}$ is perpendicular to $\overline{B'C'}$ (i.e., $A'C' \perp B'C'$)
3. $A'$ and $B$ are not on the same side of line $B'C'$

## What is Being Asked:
Find the integer closest to the area of hexagon $AA'CC'BB'$.

---

## Implicit Tasks:
To solve this problem, one would need to:
1. Determine the circumcenter and circumradius of $\triangle ABC$
2. Find the angle of rotation required so that $A'C' \perp B'C'$
3. Locate the positions of the rotated vertices $A'$, $B'$, $C'$ in relation to the original triangle
4. Verify the condition about $A'$ and $B$ being on opposite sides of $B'C'$
5. Calculate the area of the hexagon with vertices in order: $A, A', C', C, B, B'$
