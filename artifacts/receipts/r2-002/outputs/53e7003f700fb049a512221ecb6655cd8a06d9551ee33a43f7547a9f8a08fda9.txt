# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Count the Total Adjacencies

In an 8×8 grid:
- **Horizontal adjacencies:** 8 rows × 7 pairs per row = 56
- **Vertical adjacencies:** 8 columns × 7 pairs per column = 56
- **Total adjacencies:** 56 + 56 = **112 edges**

## Step 2: Verify Degree Sum

Check using the sum of degrees:
- 4 corner cells: degree 2 each → 4 × 2 = 8
- 24 edge cells (non-corner): degree 3 each → 24 × 3 = 72
- 36 interior cells: degree 4 each → 36 × 4 = 144
- **Total degree sum:** 8 + 72 + 144 = 224

Number of edges = 224/2 = **112** ✓

## Step 3: Apply Checkerboard Strategy

In a standard checkerboard coloring of the 8×8 grid:
- 32 white squares
- 32 black squares
- Every adjacency connects one white to one black cell

**Optimal placement:**
- Place numbers {1, 2, ..., 32} on white squares
- Place numbers {33, 34, ..., 64} on black squares

## Step 4: Verify Partition Properties

- Sum of {1,...,32}: $\sum_{i=1}^{32} i = \frac{32 \times 33}{2} = 528$
- Sum of {33,...,64}: $\sum_{i=33}^{64} i = \sum_{i=1}^{64} i - 528 = \frac{64 \times 65}{2} - 528 = 2080 - 528 = 1552$

## Step 5: Calculate the Maximum Sum

Every edge connects a white cell (containing a number from {1,...,32}) to a black cell (containing a number from {33,...,64}).

For each edge: difference = (black value) - (white value)

Total sum:
$$M_{\max} = \sum_{\text{all edges}} \text{(black value - white value)}$$

$$= \sum_{b \in \text{black}} b \cdot d_b - \sum_{w \in \text{white}} w \cdot d_w$$

where $d_i$ is the degree of cell $i$.

Since the checkerboard is symmetric, the degree sequences for both color classes are identical. Let's denote the average degree as:
$$\text{Average degree} = \frac{112}{32} = 3.5$$

Therefore:
$$M_{\max} = 3.5 \times \sum_{i=33}^{64} i - 3.5 \times \sum_{i=1}^{32} i$$

$$= 3.5 \times 1552 - 3.5 \times 528$$

$$= 3.5 \times (1552 - 528)$$

$$= 3.5 \times 1024$$

$$= 3584$$

## Step 6: Verify the Calculation

Double-check: $3.5 \times 1024 = \frac{7}{2} \times 1024 = 7 \times 512 = 3584$ ✓

## Step 7: Find the Final Answer Modulo 1000

$$M_{\max} \bmod 1000 = 3584 \bmod 1000 = 584$$

ANSWER: **584**
