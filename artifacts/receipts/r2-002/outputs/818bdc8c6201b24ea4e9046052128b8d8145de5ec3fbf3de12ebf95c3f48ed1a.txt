- s₁ = A_x · B_y - A_y · B_x
- s₂ = B_x · C_y - B_y · C_x  
- s₃ = C_x · A_y - C_y · A_x

**Condition:** sign(s₁) = sign(s₂) = sign(s₃) ≠ 0

This means the origin sees each edge of the triangle from a consistent perspective (all counterclockwise or all clockwise).

Any triple with collinear points gives s₁ + s₂ + s₃ = 0, which fails the test automatically.
