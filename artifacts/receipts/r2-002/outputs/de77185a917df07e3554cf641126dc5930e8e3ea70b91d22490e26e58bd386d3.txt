# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Simplify the radical expression

The radical $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}$ can be rewritten using exponent rules:
$$\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}} = x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}$$

So our equation becomes:
$$x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}=26x$$

## Step 2: Take logarithm base 2026 of both sides

Applying $\log_{2026}$ to both sides:
$$\log_{2026}\left(x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}\right) = \log_{2026}(26x)$$

**Left side:** Using the power rule for logarithms:
$$\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x = \frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20}$$

**Right side:** Using the product rule:
$$\log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

This gives us:
$$\frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

## Step 3: Convert to quadratic equation

Let $y = \log_{2026}x$. Then:
$$\frac{y^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + y$$

Multiply both sides by 20:
$$y^2 = 20\log_{2026}26 + 20y$$

Rearrange to standard form:
$$y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$$

## Step 4: Solve using Vieta's formulas

Rather than solving for the exact values of $y_1$ and $y_2$, we use **Vieta's formulas** for the quadratic $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$:

$$y_1 + y_2 = 20 \quad \text{(sum of roots)}$$

## Step 5: Convert solutions back to $x$

Since $y = \log_{2026}x$, we have:
$$x = 2026^y$$

Therefore:
$$x_1 = 2026^{y_1} \quad \text{and} \quad x_2 = 2026^{y_2}$$

## Step 6: Find the product of all solutions

$$P = x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2026^{y_1} \cdot 2026^{y_2} = 2026^{y_1 + y_2}$$

Substituting $y_1 + y_2 = 20$:
$$P = 2026^{20}$$

## Step 7: Factor and count divisors

**Factor 2026:**
$$2026 = 2 \times 1013$$

(Checking: 1013 is prime, since no prime $\leq \sqrt{1013} \approx 31.8$ divides it)

Therefore:
$$P = 2026^{20} = (2 \times 1013)^{20} = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$$

**Count the divisors** using the formula $\tau(p_1^{a_1} \times p_2^{a_2}) = (a_1+1)(a_2+1)$:

$$\tau(P) = (20+1)(20+1) = 21 \times 21 = 441$$

**Final answer (single integer only): 441**
