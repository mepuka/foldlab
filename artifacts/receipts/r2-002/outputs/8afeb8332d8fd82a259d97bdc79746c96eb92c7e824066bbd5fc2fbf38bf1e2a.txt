# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Express each term using geometric series

Starting with $\frac{1}{10^n - 1}$, I'll rewrite it:

$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \frac{1}{10^n(1 - 10^{-n})} = \frac{10^{-n}}{1 - 10^{-n}}$$

Using the geometric series $\frac{x}{1-x} = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} x^k$ for $|x| < 1$:

$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-kn}$$

**Verification:** For $n=1$: $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-k} = 0.1 + 0.01 + \cdots = \frac{1}{9}$ ✓

## Step 2: Rearrange the double sum

$$S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-kn}$$

Exchanging the order of summation: A term $10^{-m}$ appears each time $m = kn$ for positive integers $k, n$. The coefficient of $10^{-m}$ equals the number of divisor pairs $(n,k)$ where $nk = m$ and $n,k \geq 1$.

This is precisely $d(m)$, the divisor function:

$$S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d(m) \cdot 10^{-m}$$

## Step 3: Compute $10^{100} S$

$$10^{100} S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d(m) \cdot 10^{100-m}$$

## Step 4: Identify the integer part

For $m \leq 100$: $10^{100-m} \geq 1$ (integer contributions)
For $m > 100$: $10^{100-m} < 1$ (fractional contributions)

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor = \sum_{m=1}^{100} d(m) \cdot 10^{100-m}$$

## Step 5: Find the remainder modulo 1000

To find $\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000}$, only the last three decimal places matter, which comes from terms where $100 - m \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, i.e., $m \in \{98, 99, 100\}$:

**Calculate divisor counts:**

- **$d(98)$:** $98 = 2 \times 7^2$ → Divisors: {1, 2, 7, 14, 49, 98} → $d(98) = 6$
- **$d(99)$:** $99 = 3^2 \times 11$ → Divisors: {1, 3, 9, 11, 33, 99} → $d(99) = 6$
- **$d(100)$:** $100 = 2^2 \times 5^2$ → Divisors: {1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100} → $d(100) = 9$

**Compute contributions:**

$$\begin{align}
m = 98: & \quad d(98) \cdot 10^2 = 6 \times 100 = 600\\
m = 99: & \quad d(99) \cdot 10^1 = 6 \times 10 = 60\\
m = 100: & \quad d(100) \cdot 10^0 = 9 \times 1 = 9
\end{align}$$

**Final calculation:**

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000} = (600 + 60 + 9) \pmod{1000} = 669$$
