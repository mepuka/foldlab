# Solution: Finding Minimum n

## Step 1: Establish the Constraint

Since adjacent integers differ by exactly 1, each step is ±1. Traversing a circle and returning to the start requires:
$$\text{(number of +1 steps)} = \text{(number of −1 steps)}$$

This means **n must be even**: $n = 2u$ for some positive integer $u$.

## Step 2: Structure with Symmetry Breaking

To get a non-zero sum while maintaining the circular constraint, consider a path that goes:
- Up from 0: $0 \to 1 \to 2 \to \cdots \to m$
- Down: $m \to (m-1) \to \cdots \to 1 \to 0$
- Down further: $0 \to -1 \to -2 \to \cdots \to -k$
- Back up: $-k \to (-k+1) \to \cdots \to -1 \to 0$

This creates a circuit with $n = 2m + 2k$ positions.

## Step 3: Calculate the Sum

Computing the sum by sections:
- Up portion ($0$ to $m$): $\sum_{i=0}^{m} i = \frac{m(m+1)}{2}$
- Down to 0: $\sum_{i=1}^{m-1} i = \frac{m(m-1)}{2}$
- Down to $-k$: $\sum_{i=1}^{k} (-i) = -\frac{k(k+1)}{2}$
- Back up: $\sum_{i=1}^{k-1} (-i) = -\frac{k(k-1)}{2}$

**Total sum:**
$$S = \frac{m(m+1)}{2} + \frac{m(m-1)}{2} - \frac{k(k+1)}{2} - \frac{k(k-1)}{2} = m^2 - k^2$$

## Step 4: Solve for Minimum n

We need: $m^2 - k^2 = 24$

Factoring: $(m-k)(m+k) = 24$

Let $a = m - k$ and $b = m + k$, so $ab = 24$.

Then: $m = \frac{a+b}{2}$ and $k = \frac{b-a}{2}$

For integer solutions, $a$ and $b$ must have the same parity.

$$n = 2m + 2k = (a+b) + (b-a) = 2b$$

We minimize $n = 2b$ by finding factor pairs of 24 with the same parity:

| Factorization | Parity | $n = 2b$ |
|---|---|---|
| $1 \times 24$ | Different | — |
| $2 \times 12$ | Both even | 24 |
| $3 \times 8$ | Different | — |
| $4 \times 6$ | Both even | **12** |

**Minimum:** $a = 4, b = 6 \Rightarrow m = 5, k = 1$, giving $n = 12$

## Step 5: Verify the Solution

The sequence is: $0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0, -1$ (and back to 0)

**Adjacency check:** Each consecutive pair differs by exactly ±1 ✓

**Contains 0:** Yes ✓

**Sum calculation:**
$$S = (0+1+2+3+4+5) + (4+3+2+1+0) + (-1) = 15 + 10 - 1 = 24$$ ✓

**Forms a circle:** Position 12 ($-1$) is adjacent to position 1 ($0$), difference = 1 ✓

---

**Final Answer: 12**
