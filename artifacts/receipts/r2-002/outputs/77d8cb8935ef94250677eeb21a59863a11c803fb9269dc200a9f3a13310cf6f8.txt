# Solution Plan

1. **Set up a coordinate system**
   - Place $A$ at the origin $(0, 0)$
   - Place $B$ at $(0, 1)$ (since $AB = 1$)
   - Place $D$ at $(a, 0)$ where $a = BC$ (unknown)
   - Place $C$ at $(a, 1)$

2. **Express positions of $X$ and $Y$ using the constraint that they lie on sides**
   - Let $X = (0, x)$ where $0 \leq x \leq 1$ (on $\overline{BC}$)
   - Let $Y = (y, 0)$ where $0 \leq y \leq a$ (on $\overline{AD}$)

3. **Apply the perpendicularity condition $\angle AXY = 90°$**
   - Vectors $\overrightarrow{XA} = (-0, -x)$ and $\overrightarrow{XY} = (y, -x)$ must be perpendicular
   - Use dot product: $\overrightarrow{XA} \cdot \overrightarrow{XY} = 0$
   - This gives: $x^2 = y$ (Equation 1)

4. **Apply the perpendicularity condition $\angle XYC = 90°$**
   - Vectors $\overrightarrow{YX} = (-y, x)$ and $\overrightarrow{YC} = (a-y, 1)$ must be perpendicular
   - Use dot product: $\overrightarrow{YX} \cdot \overrightarrow{YC} = 0$
   - This gives: $-y(a-y) + x = 0$, or $x = y(a-y)$ (Equation 2)

5. **Apply the ratio condition $AX : XY : YC = 1 : 2 : 1$**
   - Let $AX = k$, then $XY = 2k$ and $YC = k$ for some positive constant $k$
   - Calculate distances using coordinates and set up three equations
   - This yields relationships between $a$, $x$, and $y$

6. **Solve the system of equations**
   - Substitute Equation 1 into Equation 2
   - Use the ratio constraints to form additional equations
   - Solve for $a = BC$

7. **Verify the solution**
   - Check that all conditions are satisfied with the computed value of $a$
   - Confirm that $X$ and $Y$ lie within their respective segments
