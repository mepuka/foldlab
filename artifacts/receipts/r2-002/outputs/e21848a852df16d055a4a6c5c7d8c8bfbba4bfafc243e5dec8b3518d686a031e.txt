# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- $S$ = the set of all positive integer divisors of $10^9$
- Since $10^9 = 2^9 \cdot 5^9$, the set $S$ consists of all divisors of the form $2^a \cdot 5^b$ where $0 \le a \le 9$ and $0 \le b \le 9$

**Conditions on subset $T \subseteq S$:**

1. **Pairing condition**: For every element $s \in S$, exactly one of the following holds:
   - $s \in T$, or  
   - $10^9/s \in T$
   
   (This means $s$ and its complementary divisor $10^9/s$ form a partition of certain elements)

2. **Downward closure condition**: For every element $t \in T$, all positive integer divisors of $t$ must also be in $T$.
   
   (In other words, $T$ must be an "order ideal" or "down-set" in the divisibility poset)

## What is Being Asked

Compute the total number of subsets $T$ of $S$ satisfying **both** conditions above.
