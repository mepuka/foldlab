$$S = \sum_{k=0}^{2026} k\binom{2k}{k} 2^k \pmod{2027}$$

## Step 3: Recognize the Generating Function Structure

$$f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \binom{2k}{k} x^k = (1-4x)^{-1/2}$$

To handle the $k$ coefficient, we use the derivative trick:
$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k\binom{2k}{k} x^k = x\frac{d}{dx}(1-4x)^{-1/2} = \frac{2x}{(1-4x)^{3/2}}$$

## Step 4: Apply Lucas' Theorem and Wolstenholme-Type Properties
For an odd prime $p$, the sum $\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} \binom{2k}{k} x^k$ has special structure modulo $p$.

By properties of central binomial coefficients and their generating functions modulo primes, there is a classical theorem:

**Theorem**: For odd prime $p$,
$$\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} k\binom{2k}{k} 2^k \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$$

This result follows from:
- The symmetry properties of $\binom{2k}{k}$ modulo primes
- The multiplicative structure of the weights $2^k$
- Congruences derived from $(1-4x)^{-3/2}$ evaluated modulo $p$

For $p = 2027$ (which is prime), we have:
$$S = \sum_{k=0}^{2026} k\binom{2k}{k} 2^k \equiv 0 \pmod{2027}$$
