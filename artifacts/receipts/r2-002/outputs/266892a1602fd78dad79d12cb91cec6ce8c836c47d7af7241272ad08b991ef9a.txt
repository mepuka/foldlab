# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- A hemisphere with radius $R = 200$
- A horizontal circular disk with radius $R = 200$
- The hemisphere and disk share the same center
- A sphere with radius $r = 42$

**Conditions:**
- The sphere is "placed on top of the disk at point $P$" — meaning the sphere rests on the disk at point $P$, so the center of the sphere is at height $42$ above the disk
- The sphere must lie **completely inside the hemisphere**
- Point $P$ is in the disk (the region where the sphere can be placed)

# Precise Question

**Find:** The ratio $\frac{\text{Area of } \mathcal{T}}{\text{Area of disk}}$ where $\mathcal{T}$ is the set of all points $P$ in the disk where a sphere of radius $42$ can be placed and remain completely inside the hemisphere.

Express this ratio as $\frac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms, and compute $p + q$.
