# Solution: Step-by-Step

## Step 1: Define Variables
- Patrick's speed = $p$ mph
- Tanya's speed = $p + 2$ mph
- Jose's speed = $p + 9$ mph

## Step 2: Set Up Equations
Let $t$ = arrival time (hours after Patrick starts)

- Patrick's distance: $d = p \cdot t$
- Tanya's distance: $d = (p+2)(t-1)$ (starts 1 hour later)
- Jose's distance: $d = (p+9)(t-2)$ (starts 2 hours later)

## Step 3: Equate Patrick's and Tanya's Distances

$$p \cdot t = (p+2)(t-1)$$

Expand the right side:
$$pt = pt - p + 2t - 2$$

Simplify:
$$0 = -p + 2t - 2$$

$$p = 2t - 2 \quad \text{...(Equation 1)}$$

## Step 4: Equate Patrick's and Jose's Distances

$$p \cdot t = (p+9)(t-2)$$

Expand the right side:
$$pt = pt - 2p + 9t - 18$$

Simplify:
$$0 = -2p + 9t - 18$$

$$2p = 9t - 18 \quad \text{...(Equation 2)}$$

## Step 5: Solve the System

From Equation 1: $p = 2t - 2$

Substitute into Equation 2:
$$2(2t - 2) = 9t - 18$$

$$4t - 4 = 9t - 18$$

$$-4 + 18 = 9t - 4t$$

$$14 = 5t$$

$$t = \frac{14}{5}$$

Now find $p$:
$$p = 2t - 2 = 2\left(\frac{14}{5}\right) - 2 = \frac{28}{5} - \frac{10}{5} = \frac{18}{5}$$

## Step 6: Calculate the Distance

$$d = p \cdot t = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$

## Step 7: Express as a Fraction in Lowest Terms

Find $\gcd(252, 25)$:
- $252 = 4 \cdot 63 = 4 \cdot 9 \cdot 7 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$
- $25 = 5^2$

Since they share no common factors: $\gcd(252, 25) = 1$

Therefore: $d = \frac{252}{25}$ where $m = 252$ and $n = 25$

## Step 8: Final Answer

$$m + n = 252 + 25 = \boxed{277}$$
