# Solution

## Step 1: Rewrite the expression in a more useful form

Given $n = a + b + ab$ where $a, b$ are distinct positive integers and $n \leq 100$.

Factor the expression:
$$n = a + b + ab = a(1+b) + b = a(1+b) + (1+b) - 1 = (a+1)(b+1) - 1$$

Therefore: $n = (a+1)(b+1) - 1$

This means we need: $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$

## Step 2: Determine the range for factors

Since $a, b \geq 1$ (positive integers):
- $(a+1) \geq 2$ and $(b+1) \geq 2$

Since $a \neq b$:
- $(a+1) \neq (b+1)$

Since $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$:
- Both factors are in range $[2, 101]$
