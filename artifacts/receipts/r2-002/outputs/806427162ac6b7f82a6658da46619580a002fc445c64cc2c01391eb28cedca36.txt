# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Circle properties:**
   - Center: $(4, 39)$
   - Radius: $r$ (unknown, to be determined)

2. **Parabola equation:** $2y = x^2 - 8x + 12$

3. **Tangency condition:** The circle is tangent to the parabola at at least one point

## What is Being Asked

Find the sum of all real numbers $r$ for which there exists at least one point where:
- The circle centered at $(4, 39)$ with radius $r$
- Is tangent to the parabola $2y = x^2 - 8x + 12$

In other words, find all valid radii $r$ such that the circle and parabola touch at exactly one point (or more generally, share a common tangent line at some point), then sum all such values of $r$.
