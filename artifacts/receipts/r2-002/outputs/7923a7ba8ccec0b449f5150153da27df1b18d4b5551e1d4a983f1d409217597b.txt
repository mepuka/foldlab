# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Sum definition**: $S = \sum_{k=0}^{2026} k \binom{2k}{k} 2^k$
   - The sum ranges from $k=0$ to $k=2026$
   - Each term is $k \binom{2k}{k} 2^k$ where $\binom{2k}{k}$ is the central binomial coefficient

2. **Special property of 2027**: It is prime

## What is Being Asked

Find the remainder when $S$ is divided by 2027, i.e., compute $S \pmod{2027}$.
