# Solution: Computing $\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000}$ where $S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{10^n - 1}$

## Step 1: Express each term using geometric series

Starting with $\frac{1}{10^n - 1}$:

$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \frac{1}{10^n(1 - 10^{-n})} = \frac{1}{10^n} \cdot \frac{1}{1 - 10^{-n}}$$

Using the geometric series formula $\frac{1}{1-x} = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} x^k$ for $|x| < 1$:

$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \frac{1}{10^n} \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (10^{-n})^k = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-nk}$$

**Verification:** $10^{-n} + 10^{-2n} + 10^{-3n} + \cdots$ ✓

## Step 2: Rearrange the double sum

$$S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-nk}$$

To exchange the order of summation, fix $m = nk$ and count divisors. For each $m$, the number of ways to write $m = nk$ equals $d_m$, the number of divisors of $m$:

$$S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d_m \cdot 10^{-m}$$

**where $d_m$ = number of positive divisors of $m$** ✓

## Step 3: Compute $10^{100} S$

$$10^{100} S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d_m \cdot 10^{100-m}$$

## Step 4: Identify the integer part

The integer part contains terms where the exponent $100 - m \geq 0$, i.e., $m \leq 100$:

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor = \sum_{m=1}^{100} d_m \cdot 10^{100-m}$$

Terms with $m > 100$ contribute only fractional parts. ✓

## Step 5: Find the remainder modulo 1000

For the last three digits, only contributions from the last three terms matter:

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \equiv d_{98} \cdot 10^2 + d_{99} \cdot 10^1 + d_{100} \cdot 10^0 \pmod{1000}$$

(Terms with $m \leq 97$ contribute multiples of $10^3 = 1000$)

## Calculate the divisor counts:

**$d_{98}$:** Factor: $98 = 2 \times 7^2$
- Divisors: $1, 2, 7, 14, 49, 98$
- $d_{98} = 6$ ✓

**$d_{99}$:** Factor: $99 = 3^2 \times 11$
- Divisors: $1, 3, 9, 11, 33, 99$
- $d_{99} = 6$ ✓

**$d_{100}$:** Factor: $100 = 2^2 \times 5^2$
- Number of divisors: $(2+1)(2+1) = 9$
- Divisors: $1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100$
- $d_{100} = 9$ ✓

## Final calculation:

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000} = (6 \cdot 100 + 6 \cdot 10 + 9) \pmod{1000}$$

$$= (600 + 60 + 9) \pmod{1000}$$

$$= 669 \pmod{1000}$$

$$= 669$$

ANSWER: **669**
