# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **The number being analyzed:** $17017^{17}$
2. **Prime factorization context:** $17017 = 7 \times 11 \times 13 \times 17$ (product of four distinct primes)
3. **Remainder condition:** We want divisors that leave a remainder of $5$ when divided by $12$
   - Equivalently: divisors $d$ such that $d \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$
4. **Modular constraints from the condition:**
   - Since $12 = 3 \times 4$, the condition $d \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$ is equivalent to:
     - $d \equiv 5 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$
     - $d \equiv 5 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$

## What is Being Asked

Find the number $N$ of positive integer divisors of $17017^{17}$ that satisfy $d \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$, then determine the remainder when $N$ is divided by $1000$.
