# Extended Verification of Solution

## Step 1-2: Setup Verification ✓
- Hemisphere: upper half of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$ with $z \geq 0$
- Sphere of radius 42 with center at $(p, 0, 42)$ resting on disk at point $(p, 0, 0)$

## Step 3-4: Containment Constraint ✓
For any point on the sphere to satisfy $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 200^2$, the farthest point from the origin must be within the hemisphere.

Distance from origin to sphere's center:
$$d_c = \sqrt{p^2 + 0^2 + 42^2} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764}$$

Farthest point from origin (along radial direction through center):
$$d_{\max} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42$$

Constraint: $d_{\max} \leq 200$

## Step 5: Solving the Inequality
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42 \leq 200$$

$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} \leq 158$$

Squaring both sides (valid since both sides are positive):
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 158^2$$

Computing $158^2$: 
$$158^2 = (160-2)^2 = 25600 - 640 + 4 = 24964$$ ✓

$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$

$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

$$p \leq \sqrt{23200}$$

**Simplifying the radical:**
- $23200 = 232 \times 100 = (4 \times 58) \times 100$
- $\sqrt{23200} = \sqrt{400 \times 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$ ✓

## Step 6: Area Calculations

**Region $\mathcal{T}$** (disk of radius $20\sqrt{58}$):
$$\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \times 400 \times 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Disk** (radius 200):
$$\text{Area}_{\text{disk}} = \pi(200)^2 = 40000\pi$$

## Step 7: Ratio Computation

$$\frac{\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}}}{\text{Area}_{\text{disk}}} = \frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

**Reducing to lowest terms:**
- Prime factorization: $23200 = 2^5 \times 5^2 \times 29$
- Prime factorization: $40000 = 2^6 \times 5^4$
- $\gcd(23200, 40000) = 2^5 \times 5^2 = 800$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{23200 \div 800}{40000 \div 800} = \frac{29}{50}$$

**Verification:** $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ since 29 is prime and doesn't divide 50. ✓

## Step 8: Final Answer

$$p = 29, \quad q = 50$$

$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$
