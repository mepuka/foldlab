# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Prime number:** $p = 503$
2. **Upper limit:** $10000$ (appears in binomial coefficients)
3. **Modulus:** $502 = 503 - 1$
4. **Range of $r$:** positive integers with $1 \le r \le 501$ (note: "less than $502$" means $r \in \{1, 2, \ldots, 501\}$)
5. **Definition of $S_r$:**
$$S_r = \sum_{m \ge 0} \binom{10000}{502m + r}$$
where $\binom{10000}{n} = 0$ for $n > 10000$

   Equivalently: $S_r$ is the sum of all binomial coefficients $\binom{10000}{k}$ where $0 \le k \le 10000$ and $k \equiv r \pmod{502}$.

## What is Being Asked

**Find the number of values in the list $S_1, S_2, \ldots, S_{501}$ that are divisible by $503$.**

In other words, determine $|\{r : 1 \le r \le 501 \text{ and } 503 \mid S_r\}|$.

**Note:** The problem statement lists "$S_0, S_1, \ldots, S_{501}$" but $r$ is restricted to positive integers less than $502$, so $S_0$ technically should not be included. The answer should count how many of the 501 values $S_1$ through $S_{501}$ are divisible by $503$.
