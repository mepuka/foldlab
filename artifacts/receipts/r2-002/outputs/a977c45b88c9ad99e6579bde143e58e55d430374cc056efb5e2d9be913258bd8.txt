# Solution with Extended Reasoning

## Step 1: Simplify the Radical Expression ✓

Starting with $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}$, I'll apply the exponent rule:
$$\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}} = \left(x^{\log_{2026}x}\right)^{1/20} = x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}$$

The equation becomes:
$$x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}} = 26x$$

## Step 2: Take Logarithm Base 2026 of Both Sides ✓

Applying $\log_{2026}$ to both sides:
$$\log_{2026}\left(x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}\right) = \log_{2026}(26x)$$

**Left side:** Using the power rule for logarithms:
$$\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x = \frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20}$$

**Right side:** Using the product rule:
$$\log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

Equation becomes:
$$\frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

## Step 3: Convert to Quadratic ✓

Let $y = \log_{2026}x$:
$$\frac{y^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + y$$

Multiply both sides by 20:
$$y^2 = 20\log_{2026}26 + 20y$$

Rearrange to standard form:
$$y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$$

## Step 4: Solve Using Vieta's Formulas ✓

For the quadratic $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$:

**Sum of roots:** $y_1 + y_2 = 20$

**Product of roots:** $y_1 \cdot y_2 = -20\log_{2026}26$

The specific values of $y_1$ and $y_2$ are:
$$y = 10 \pm \sqrt{100 + 20\log_{2026}26}$$

Both are valid since we need $x > 0$.

## Step 5: Convert Back to $x$ ✓

Since $y_i = \log_{2026}x_i$:
$$x_i = 2026^{y_i}$$

This gives us:
- $x_1 = 2026^{y_1}$
- $x_2 = 2026^{y_2}$

## Step 6: Product of All Solutions ✓

$$P = x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2026^{y_1} \cdot 2026^{y_2} = 2026^{y_1 + y_2} = 2026^{20}$$

## Step 7: Count the Divisors ✓

**Factor 2026:**
$$2026 = 2 \times 1013$$

Checking that 1013 is prime (verified: not divisible by any prime up to $\sqrt{1013} \approx 31.8$).

**Prime factorization of P:**
$$P = 2026^{20} = (2 \times 1013)^{20} = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$$

**Using the divisor formula:**
For $n = p_1^{a_1} \times p_2^{a_2}$, the number of divisors is $\tau(n) = (a_1 + 1)(a_2 + 1)$

$$\tau(P) = (20 + 1)(20 + 1) = 21 \times 21 = 441$$
