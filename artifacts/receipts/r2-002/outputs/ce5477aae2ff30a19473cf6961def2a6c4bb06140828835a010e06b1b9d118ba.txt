# Solution: Finding all n where the 6th Largest Divisor is 6

## Step 1: Establish the divisor structure

If the 6th largest divisor is exactly 6, then:
- n must have at least 6 divisors
- Exactly 5 divisors are greater than 6
- The next divisor below these 5 must be exactly 6
- All remaining divisors are ≤ 6

## Step 2: Identify divisors ≤ 6

Since 6 is a divisor of n, both 2 and 3 must divide n.

The possible divisors of n that are ≤ 6 are:
- {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6} depending on n's factorization
- Since 6 | n, at minimum we have {1, 2, 3, 6}

## Step 3: Determine prime factorization structure

Let n = 2^a · 3^b · (other primes), where a ≥ 1, b ≥ 1.

We need exactly 5 divisors greater than 6.

## Step 4: Systematically test candidates

**Case: n = 2^a · 3 (only primes 2 and 3)**

- n = 6 = 2·3: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 6} → only 4 divisors ✗

- n = 12 = 2²·3: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12} → 6 total
  - Ordered: 12, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1
  - 6th largest = 1 ✗

- n = 24 = 2³·3: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24}
  - Divisors > 6: {8, 12, 24} → only 3 ✗

- **n = 48 = 2⁴·3:** Divisors {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48}
  - Divisors > 6: {8, 12, 16, 24, 48} → exactly 5 ✓
  - Ordered: 48, 24, 16, 12, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1
  - **6th largest divisor = 6** ✓

- n = 96 = 2⁵·3: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96}
  - Divisors > 6: {8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96} → 7 divisors ✗

**Case: n = 2·3^b**

- n = 18 = 2·3²: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18}
  - Divisors > 6: {9, 18} → only 2 ✗

- n = 54 = 2·3³: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18, 27, 54}
  - Divisors > 6: {9, 18, 27, 54} → only 4 ✗

- n = 162 = 2·3⁴: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18, 27, 54, 81, 162}
  - Divisors > 6: {9, 18, 27, 54, 81, 162} → 6 divisors ✗

**Case: n involves a third prime p**

- n = 30 = 2·3·5: Divisors {1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30}
  - Divisors > 6: {10, 15, 30} → only 3 ✗

- n = 60 = 2²·3·5: Divisors total 12, divisors > 6 include {10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60}
  - More than 5 divisors > 6 ✗

- Other combinations with third primes either have too few or too many divisors > 6

## Step 5: Verify the solution

**n = 48:**
- Divisors in descending order: 48, 24, 16, 12, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1
- Position 1: 48 > 6 ✓
- Position 2: 24 > 6 ✓
- Position 3: 16 > 6 ✓
- Position 4: 12 > 6 ✓
- Position 5: 8 > 6 ✓
- Position 6: 6 = 6 ✓
- Position 7 and beyond: all ≤ 6 ✓

## Final Answer

**48**
