# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given Quantities:**
- Pentagon $VENUS$ has area $84$
- Triangle $SUN$ has area $24$

**Given Conditions:**
1. Pentagon $VENUS$ is convex
2. $NV \parallel SU$ (segment $NV$ is parallel to segment $SU$)
3. $SE \parallel UN$ (segment $SE$ is parallel to segment $UN$)

## What is Being Asked

Compute the **maximum possible area of triangle $EUV$**.

---

## Notes on Setup

The pentagon has vertices in order $V, E, N, U, S$ (forming a convex pentagon). The problem involves:
- A specific triangle $SUN$ with fixed area $24$
- Two parallel conditions that constrain the positions of the vertices
- The goal is to find how large triangle $EUV$ can be while respecting all constraints and maintaining the total pentagon area of $84$
