# Solving for Moons with All Three Characteristics

## Step 1: Define the 7 Non-Overlapping Regions

When three sets intersect, they create 7 mutually exclusive regions:
1. Only blue (B only)
2. Only icy (I only)
3. Only large (L only)
4. Blue and icy, not large (B∩I−L)
5. Blue and large, not icy (B∩L−I)
6. Icy and large, not blue (I∩L−B)
7. All three: blue AND icy AND large (B∩I∩L) = **x**

## Step 2: Interpret the Given Conditions

**"Blue but not icy: 10"** means B − I
- This includes: (only blue) + (blue and large but not icy)
- Equation: b₁ + b₅ = 10 ... (1)

**"Icy but not large: 8"** means I − L
- This includes: (only icy) + (blue and icy but not large)
- Equation: i₂ + b₄ = 8 ... (2)

**"Large but not blue: 6"** means L − B
- This includes: (only large) + (icy and large but not blue)
- Equation: l₃ + i₆ = 6 ... (3)

## Step 3: Verify Mutual Exclusivity

The three given conditions are mutually exclusive:
- B − I and I − L cannot overlap (one excludes I, the other requires it)
- B − I and L − B cannot overlap (one requires B, the other excludes it)
- I − L and L − B cannot overlap (one excludes L, the other requires it)

✓ No double counting

## Step 4: Apply the Partition Principle

All 29 moons must be distributed across all 7 regions:

b₁ + i₂ + l₃ + b₄ + b₅ + i₆ + x = 29

## Step 5: Substitute and Solve

Rearrange by grouping the equations:

(b₁ + b₅) + (i₂ + b₄) + (l₃ + i₆) + x = 29

Substitute the values from equations (1), (2), and (3):

10 + 8 + 6 + x = 29

24 + x = 29

**x = 5**

## Verification

- Blue but not icy: 10 ✓
- Icy but not large: 8 ✓
- Large but not blue: 6 ✓
- All three characteristics: 5 ✓
- Total: 10 + 8 + 6 + 5 = 29 ✓

Final answer (single integer only): **5**
