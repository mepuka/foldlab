# Extended Solution with Verification

## Step 1: Coordinate System ✓
- Origin at center of hemisphere and disk
- Disk in xy-plane (z = 0)
- Hemisphere: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$, $z \geq 0$

## Step 2: Sphere Position ✓
- Sphere rests on disk at point $P = (p, 0)$ where $p \geq 0$
- Sphere has radius 42
- Center directly above contact point: $(p, 0, 42)$
- Sphere equation: $(x-p)^2 + y^2 + (z-42)^2 = 1764$

## Step 3: Containment Condition ✓
For complete containment: every point on the sphere satisfies $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 40000$

The critical constraint is that the farthest point from the origin cannot exceed distance 200.

## Step 4: Distance Calculation ✓
Distance from origin to sphere center:
$$d_{center} = \sqrt{p^2 + 0^2 + 42^2} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764}$$

Farthest point distance:
$$d_{max} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42$$

Constraint:
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42 \leq 200$$

## Step 5: Solve for Allowed Region ✓
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} \leq 158$$

Square both sides:
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 158^2 = 24964$$

$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

$$p \leq \sqrt{23200}$$

Factor: $23200 = 400 \times 58$

$$p \leq 20\sqrt{58}$$

## Step 6: Calculate Areas ✓

**Region $\mathcal{T}$** (by rotational symmetry):
$$A_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \cdot 400 \cdot 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Disk:**
$$A_{disk} = \pi \cdot 200^2 = 40000\pi$$

## Step 7: Compute Ratio ✓
$$\frac{A_{\mathcal{T}}}{A_{disk}} = \frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Simplify by factoring:
- $23200 = 400 \times 58 = 50 \times 464 = 50 \times 29 \times 16$
- $40000 = 400 \times 100 = 50 \times 800 = 50 \times 50 \times 16$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{50 \times 464}{50 \times 800} = \frac{464}{800} = \frac{29}{50}$$

Verification: $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ since 29 is prime and doesn't divide 50 ✓

## Step 8: Final Answer ✓
- $p = 29$
- $q = 50$
- $p + q = 79$

**79**
