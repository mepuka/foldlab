# Extended Reasoning and Verification

## Step 1: Express each term using geometric series

Starting with $S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{10^n - 1}$

For each term, factor out $10^{-n}$:
$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = \frac{1}{10^n(1 - 10^{-n})}$$

Using the geometric series formula $\frac{1}{1-x} = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} x^{k-1}$ with $x = 10^{-n}$:

$$\frac{1}{10^n - 1} = 10^{-n} \cdot \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-(k-1)n} = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-kn}$$

**Verification:** For $n=1$: $\frac{1}{9} = 0.1111... = 0.1 + 0.01 + 0.001 + \cdots$ ✓

## Step 2: Rearrange the double sum

$$S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 10^{-kn}$$

To interchange the order of summation, we count how many pairs $(n,k)$ give each product $m = kn$:

- For each divisor relationship $kn = m$, the divisor $k$ of $m$ pairs with $n = m/k$
- This happens exactly $d(m)$ times, where $d(m)$ is the number of divisors of $m$

Therefore:
$$S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d(m) \cdot 10^{-m}$$

## Step 3: Compute $10^{100}S$

$$10^{100} S = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} d(m) \cdot 10^{100-m}$$

Split at $m = 100$:
$$10^{100} S = \underbrace{\sum_{m=1}^{100} d(m) \cdot 10^{100-m}}_{\text{integer part}} + \underbrace{\sum_{m=101}^{\infty} d(m) \cdot 10^{100-m}}_{\text{fractional part}}$$

## Step 4: Focus on last three digits

For the remainder mod 1000, only contributions from $10^2, 10^1, 10^0$ matter:

- $m = 98$: contributes $d(98) \cdot 10^2 = d(98) \cdot 100$
- $m = 99$: contributes $d(99) \cdot 10^1 = d(99) \cdot 10$
- $m = 100$: contributes $d(100) \cdot 10^0 = d(100)$

(Terms with $m < 98$ contribute multiples of 1000, so they vanish mod 1000)

## Step 5: Calculate divisor counts

**For $d(98)$:**
$$98 = 2 \times 7^2$$
Divisors: $\{1, 2, 7, 14, 49, 98\}$
$$d(98) = (1+1)(2+1) = 2 \times 3 = 6$$ ✓

**For $d(99)$:**
$$99 = 3^2 \times 11$$
Divisors: $\{1, 3, 9, 11, 33, 99\}$
$$d(99) = (2+1)(1+1) = 3 \times 2 = 6$$ ✓

**For $d(100)$:**
$$100 = 2^2 \times 5^2$$
Divisors: $\{1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100\}$
$$d(100) = (2+1)(2+1) = 3 \times 3 = 9$$ ✓

## Step 6: Calculate the remainder

$$\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000} = (6 \times 100 + 6 \times 10 + 9) \pmod{1000}$$

$$= (600 + 60 + 9) \pmod{1000}$$

$$= 669 \pmod{1000}$$

$$= 669$$

Final answer (single integer only): **669**
