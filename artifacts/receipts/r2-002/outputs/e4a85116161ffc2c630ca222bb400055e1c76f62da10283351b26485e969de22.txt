# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Verify the Factorization

Starting with $n = a + b + ab$:

Expanding $(a+1)(b+1) = ab + a + b + 1$

Therefore: $(a+1)(b+1) - 1 = ab + a + b + 1 - 1 = a + b + ab = n$ ✓

For $n \leq 100$, we need: $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$

## Step 2: Establish the Constraints

- $a, b$ are distinct positive integers: $a, b \geq 1$, $a \neq b$
- This gives us $(a+1), (b+1) \geq 2$
- Condition: $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$
