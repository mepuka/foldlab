# Verification of Final Answer

The final integer answer to this problem is **65**.

## Summary of Solution

The problem involves:
1. Rotating point $A$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B$ to get $A'$
2. Rotating point $B$ clockwise by angle $\theta$ around $A'$ to get $B'$
3. Finding $\theta$ such that $|AB'| = \frac{4}{3}$ (given $|AB| = 1$)

The key calculation yields:
$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$

where:
- $m = 29$ (numerator)
- $n = 36$ (denominator)
- $\gcd(29, 36) = 1$ (fraction in lowest terms)

Therefore: $$m + n = 29 + 36 = \boxed{65}$$
