# Given Quantities and Conditions

## Definition
- An operation $\circ$ is defined on integers $a$ and $b$ as:
  - $a \circ b = a - b$ if $a$ is odd AND $b$ is even
  - $a \circ b = a + b$ otherwise (all other cases)

## Constraints on the Sequence
The sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots, a_n$ must consist of:
1. **Positive integers** (all $a_i \geq 1$)
2. **Arbitrary length** $n$ (not specified, so $n$ can be any positive integer)
3. **Sum constraint**: $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \cdots + a_n = 12$
4. **Operation constraint**: $a_1 \circ a_2 \circ a_3 \circ \cdots \circ a_n = 0$ (evaluated left to right)

## What is Being Asked

Find the total number of distinct sequences satisfying both the sum constraint and the operation constraint.
