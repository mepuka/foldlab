# Verification of Solution

## Step 1: Event B Verification ✓

For all even stickers to be visible:
- Sticker 2 must not be covered by 3, 4, 5, or 6 → must land on face distinct from 4 and 6
- Sticker 4 must not be covered by 5 or 6 → must land on face distinct from 6
- Sticker 6 lands on some face (always visible as it's last)

This requires stickers 2, 4, 6 on three distinct faces. **Correct.**

## Step 2: Counting |B| ✓

Sequence of rolls and constraints:
- Roll 6: Any face = **6 choices**
- Roll 5: Any face (will be covered) = **6 choices**
- Roll 4: Must differ from $f_6$ = **5 choices**
- Roll 3: Any face (will be covered) = **6 choices**
- Roll 2: Must differ from both $f_4$ and $f_6$ = **4 choices**
- Roll 1: Any face (will be covered) = **6 choices**

$$|B| = 6 \times 6 \times 5 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6 = 25,920$$

## Step 3: Event A ∩ B Logic ✓

- Event A: Exactly 5 faces visible (one blank face)
- Event B: Stickers 2, 4, 6 occupy three distinct faces

For both to hold: The three remaining faces must each have exactly one of stickers 1, 3, 5. **Correct.**

## Step 4: Counting |A ∩ B| ✓

Given stickers 2, 4, 6 on three specific distinct faces:
- Roll 6: Any face = **6 choices**
- Roll 5: Must land on one of the 3 remaining faces = **3 choices**
- Roll 4: Must land on one of the 3 remaining faces ≠ $f_6$ = **5 choices**
- Roll 3: Must land on one of the 2 still-unoccupied remaining faces = **2 choices**
- Roll 2: Must land on the last remaining face ≠ $f_4, f_6$ = **4 choices**
- Roll 1: Must land on the last remaining face = **1 choice**

$$|A \cap B| = 6 \times 3 \times 5 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1 = 720$$

## Step 5: Calculate Conditional Probability ✓

$$P(A|B) = \frac{720}{25,920}$$

Simplifying by cancellation:
$$P(A|B) = \frac{6 \times 3 \times 5 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1}{6 \times 6 \times 5 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6}$$

- Cancel 6: $\frac{3 \times 5 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1}{6 \times 5 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6}$
- Cancel 5: $\frac{3 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1}{6 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6}$
- Cancel 4: $\frac{3 \times 2 \times 1}{6 \times 6 \times 6} = \frac{6}{216} = \frac{1}{36}$ ✓

## Step 6: Final Answer ✓

$$P(A|B) = \frac{1}{36}$$

Since $\gcd(1, 36) = 1$, we have $m = 1$ and $n = 36$.

$$m + n = 1 + 36 = 37$$

**37**
