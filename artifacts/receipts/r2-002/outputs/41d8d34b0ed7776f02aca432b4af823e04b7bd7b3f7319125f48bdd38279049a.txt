# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Grid dimensions:** 3 × 10 grid (3 rows, 10 columns)
2. **Starting position:** Cell A (located at row 2, column 2)
3. **Ending position:** Cell B (located at row 2, column 9)
4. **Movement rules:** Can walk from any cell to an adjacent cell that shares an edge (up, down, left, right; no diagonal moves)
5. **Visitation constraint:** Must visit every cell exactly once
6. **Counting convention:** Starting in cell A counts as visiting cell A

## What is Being Asked

Compute the number of distinct paths from cell A to cell B that:
- Visit all 30 cells in the grid exactly once (a Hamiltonian path)
- Start at A and end at B
- Only use edge-adjacent moves

In other words: **Count the Hamiltonian paths from A to B in a 3 × 10 grid.**
