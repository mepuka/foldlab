# Extended Solution

## Step 1: Set up the ratio constraint from angle bisector theorem

By the angle bisector theorem, the internal angle bisector from $A$ divides the opposite side $BC$ in the ratio of the adjacent sides:
$$\frac{BD}{CD} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{200}{225} = \frac{8}{9}$$

Let $BD = 8k$ and $CD = 9k$ for some positive value $k$.

Then: $$BC = BD + CD = 8k + 9k = 17k$$ ✓

## Step 2: Apply the power of a point relationship

**For point B:**
The line $AB$ intersects circle $\omega$ at points $A$ and $E$ (with $E$ between $A$ and $B$).
Using power of point $B$:
$$\text{Power}(B) = BA \cdot BE = 200(200 - AE)$$

The line $BC$ is tangent to the circle at $D$:
$$\text{Power}(B) = BD^2 = (8k)^2 = 64k^2$$

Therefore:
$$200(200 - AE) = 64k^2$$
$$AE = 200 - \frac{64k^2}{200} = 200 - \frac{8k^2}{25}$$

**For point C:**
The line $AC$ intersects circle $\omega$ at points $A$ and $F$ (with $F$ between $A$ and $C$).
$$\text{Power}(C) = CA \cdot CF = 225(225 - AF) = 81k^2$$
$$AF = 225 - \frac{81k^2}{225} = 225 - \frac{9k^2}{25}$$

## Step 3: Determine $k$ using the tangency condition

The constraint that $AE \cdot AB = AF \cdot AC$ (from the circle properties) gives:
$$AE \cdot 200 = AF \cdot 225$$

Substituting our expressions:
$$\left(200 - \frac{8k^2}{25}\right) \cdot 200 = \left(225 - \frac{9k^2}{25}\right) \cdot 225$$

Expanding:
$$40000 - 64k^2 = 50625 - 81k^2$$

$$81k^2 - 64k^2 = 50625 - 40000$$

$$17k^2 = 10625$$

$$k^2 = 625$$

$$k = 25$$

## Step 4: Verify constraints

With $k = 25$:
- $AE = 200 - \frac{8(625)}{25} = 200 - 200 = 0$ ✗
