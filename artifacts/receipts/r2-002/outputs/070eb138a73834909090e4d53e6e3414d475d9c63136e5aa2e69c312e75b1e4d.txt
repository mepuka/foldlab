# Given Quantities and Conditions

## Given Quantities:
- Tanya's speed is 2 mph faster than Patrick's speed
- Jose's speed is 7 mph faster than Tanya's speed
- Patrick starts at time $t = 0$
- Tanya starts at time $t = 1$ hour (1 hour after Patrick)
- Jose starts at time $t = 2$ hours (1 hour after Tanya)
- All three arrive at the park at the same time

## Given Conditions:
1. All three travel at constant rates along the same straight road
2. They all travel the same distance (from school to park)
3. Patrick's arrival time > 2 hours (since Jose starts at $t=2$)
4. The distance is a rational number $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms

## What is Being Asked:
Find the distance from school to park (in miles), express it as $\frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, and compute $m + n$.
