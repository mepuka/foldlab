# Solution: Maximum Area of Triangle EUV

## Step 1: Set up a coordinate system

I'll place triangle $SUN$ with known area 24 in a coordinate system:
- $U = (0, 0)$ at the origin
- $N = (12, 0)$ on the positive $x$-axis  
- $S = (0, 4)$

**Verification:** Area$(SUN) = \frac{1}{2}|12 \cdot 4| = 24$ ✓

## Step 2: Apply parallel conditions to find V and E

**For condition $NV \parallel SU$:**
$$\overrightarrow{SU} = (0, 0) - (0, 4) = (0, -4)$$

Therefore: $V = N + t_1 \cdot \overrightarrow{SU} = (12, 0) + t_1(0, -4) = (12, -4t_1)$

**For condition $SE \parallel UN$:**
$$\overrightarrow{UN} = (12, 0) - (0, 0) = (12, 0)$$

Therefore: $E = S + t_2 \cdot \overrightarrow{UN} = (0, 4) + t_2(12, 0) = (12t_2, 4)$

## Step 3: Express pentagon VENUS area as function of parameters

Pentagon vertices in order: $V(12, -4t_1)$, $E(12t_2, 4)$, $N(12, 0)$, $U(0, 0)$, $S(0, 4)$

Using the **shoelace formula:**
$$2A = \sum_{i} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)$$

Computing each term:
- $V$ to $E$: $(12)(4) - (12t_2)(-4t_1) = 48 + 48t_1t_2$
- $E$ to $N$: $(12t_2)(0) - (12)(4) = -48$
- $N$ to $U$: $(12)(0) - (0)(0) = 0$
- $U$ to $S$: $(0)(4) - (0)(0) = 0$
- $S$ to $V$: $(0)(-4t_1) - (12)(4) = -48$

$$2A = 48 + 48t_1t_2 - 48 + 0 + 0 - 48 = 48t_1t_2 - 48$$

$$\text{Area}(VENUS) = \frac{1}{2}|48t_1t_2 - 48| = 24|t_1t_2 - 1|$$

**Constraint equation:** $24|t_1t_2 - 1| = 84$

$$|t_1t_2 - 1| = 3.5$$

This gives us: **$t_1t_2 = 4.5$ or $t_1t_2 = -2.5$**

## Step 4: Express triangle EUV area as function of parameters

Triangle $EUV$ has vertices: $E(12t_2, 4)$, $U(0, 0)$, $V(12, -4t_1)$

Using the coordinate formula:
$$2A = |x_E(y_U - y_V) + x_U(y_V - y_E) + x_V(y_E - y_U)|$$

$$= |12t_2(0 - (-4t_1)) + 0(-4t_1 - 4) + 12(4 - 0)|$$

$$= |12t_2 \cdot 4t_1 + 0 + 48|$$

$$= |48t_1t_2 + 48|$$

$$\text{Area}(EUV) = 24|t_1t_2 + 1|$$

## Step 5: Optimize subject to constraints

**Case 1:** $t_1t_2 = 4.5$
$$\text{Area}(EUV) = 24|4.5 + 1| = 24 \times 5.5 = 132$$

**Case 2:** $t_1t_2 = -2.5$
$$\text{Area}(EUV) = 24|-2.5 + 1| = 24 \times 1.5 = 36$$

The maximum area occurs in **Case 1** with $t_1t_2 = 4.5$.

## Step 6: Verification

For $t_1t_2 = 4.5$:
- Pentagon area: $24|4.5 - 1| = 24(3.5) = 84$ ✓
- Triangle $EUV$ area: $24|4.5 + 1| = 24(5.5) = 132$ ✓

All constraints are satisfied.

---

**ANSWER: 132**
