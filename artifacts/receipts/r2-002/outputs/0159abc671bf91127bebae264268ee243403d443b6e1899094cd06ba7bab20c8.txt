$$\frac{10^9}{2^a \cdot 5^b} = 2^{9-a} \cdot 5^{9-b}$$

This corresponds to the pair $(9-a, 9-b)$.

The divisibility order on coordinates is: $(a,b) \preceq (a',b')$ iff $a \le a'$ AND $b \le b'$.

A downward-closed set (order ideal) must satisfy: if $(a,b) \in T$ and $(a',b') \preceq (a,b)$, then $(a',b') \in T$.
