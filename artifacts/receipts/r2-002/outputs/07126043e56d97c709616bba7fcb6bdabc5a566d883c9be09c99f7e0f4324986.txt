# Solution: Finding Moons with All Three Characteristics

## Step 1: Define the Seven Regions of the Venn Diagram

For three overlapping sets (Blue, Icy, Large), we have 7 non-overlapping regions:

1. Blue only (B ∩ I' ∩ L')
2. Icy only (B' ∩ I ∩ L')
3. Large only (B' ∩ I' ∩ L)
4. Blue and Icy, but NOT Large (B ∩ I ∩ L')
5. Blue and Large, but NOT Icy (B ∩ I' ∩ L)
6. Icy and Large, but NOT Blue (B' ∩ I ∩ L)
7. All three: Blue AND Icy AND Large (B ∩ I ∩ L) = **x** ← our target

## Step 2: Assign Known Quantities

**"Blue but not icy: 10"** means B ∩ I' = 10
- This includes Regions 1 and 5
- Region 1 + Region 5 = 10

**"Icy but not large: 8"** means I ∩ L' = 8
- This includes Regions 2 and 4
- Region 2 + Region 4 = 8

**"Large but not blue: 6"** means L ∩ B' = 6
- This includes Regions 3 and 6
- Region 3 + Region 6 = 6

## Step 3: Sum the Constraint Equations

Adding the three constraint equations:
$$(\text{Region 1} + \text{Region 5}) + (\text{Region 2} + \text{Region 4}) + (\text{Region 3} + \text{Region 6}) = 10 + 8 + 6$$

$$\text{Region 1} + \text{Region 2} + \text{Region 3} + \text{Region 4} + \text{Region 5} + \text{Region 6} = 24$$

## Step 4: Apply the Partition Principle

All 29 moons must be distributed across all 7 regions:

$$\text{Regions 1-6} + \text{Region 7} = 29$$

$$24 + x = 29$$

## Step 5: Solve for x

$$x = 29 - 24 = 5$$

## Verification
- Moons with exactly one characteristic (possibly combined with others): 24
- Moons with all three characteristics: 5
- Total: 24 + 5 = 29 ✓

**5**
