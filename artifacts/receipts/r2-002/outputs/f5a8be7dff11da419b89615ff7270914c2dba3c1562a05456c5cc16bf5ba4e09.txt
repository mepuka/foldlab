# Plan for Solving the Pentagon Area Problem

1. **Set up a coordinate system**
   - Place $O$ at the origin $(0, 0)$
   - Place $H$ at $(20, 0)$ using $HO = 20$

2. **Find point $S$ using the constraint $OS = 10$**
   - Use $OS = 10$ and the angle $\angle HOR = 135°$ to determine $S$'s position
   - Note that $S$ lies on a circle of radius 10 centered at $O$

3. **Locate point $E$ using triangle $OSE$**
   - Use $SE = 26$ and the constraint $\angle SEH = 90°$
   - Apply the right angle condition at $E$ in triangle $HEO$ where $\angle EHO = 90°$

4. **Find point $R$ using the angle constraints**
   - Use $\angle HOR = 135°$ to position $R$ relative to $H$ and $O$
   - Apply the constraint $\angle ORS = 90°$ (right angle at $R$)
   - Apply the constraint $\angle RSE = 135°$ to finalize $R$'s position

5. **Verify the pentagon is convex**
   - Check that vertices $H, O, R, S, E$ form a convex pentagon in order
   - Confirm all interior angles are less than $180°$

6. **Calculate the area**
   - Use the Shoelace formula with the coordinates of $H, O, R, S, E$
   - Alternatively, decompose into triangles and sum their areas

7. **State the final answer**
   - Present the computed area of pentagon $HORSE$
