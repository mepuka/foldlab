# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Calculate the circumradius of △ABC

**Using Heron's formula:**
$$s = \frac{13 + 14 + 15}{2} = 21$$

$$K = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)} = \sqrt{21 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6} = \sqrt{7056} = 84$$

**Area of △ABC = 84**

**Circumradius:**
$$R = \frac{abc}{4K} = \frac{13 \cdot 14 \cdot 15}{4 \cdot 84} = \frac{2730}{336} = \frac{65}{8}$$

## Step 2: Set up coordinate system

Place $B$ at origin, $C$ on positive $x$-axis:
- $B = (0, 0)$
- $C = (14, 0)$

For point $A = (x, y)$:
- From $|AB| = 13$: $x^2 + y^2 = 169$
- From $|AC| = 15$: $(x-14)^2 + y^2 = 225$

Solving: $x^2 - 28x + 196 + y^2 = 225 \Rightarrow 169 - 28x + 196 = 225 \Rightarrow x = 5$

Therefore: $y^2 = 169 - 25 = 144 \Rightarrow y = 12$

**So $A = (5, 12)$**

## Step 3: Find circumcenter O

Let $O = (h, k)$:

From $|OB| = |OC|$: $h^2 + k^2 = (h-14)^2 + k^2 \Rightarrow h = 7$

From $|OB| = |OA|$: $49 + k^2 = 4 + (k-12)^2 \Rightarrow 49 + k^2 = 4 + k^2 - 24k + 144$

$$\Rightarrow 24k = 99 \Rightarrow k = \frac{33}{8}$$

**So $O = (7, \frac{33}{8})$**

## Step 4: Determine rotation angle θ

Using law of cosines in original triangle at vertex $C$:
$$\cos C = \frac{CA^2 + CB^2 - AB^2}{2 \cdot CA \cdot CB} = \frac{225 + 196 - 169}{420} = \frac{252}{420} = \frac{3}{5}$$

For the perpendicularity constraint $A'C' \perp B'C'$, we rotate by angle $\theta$ where $\cos\theta = \frac{3}{5}$, $\sin\theta = \frac{4}{5}$.

## Step 5: Calculate rotated vertices

Using rotation matrix about $O$:

**For $A' = (-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{29}{4})$**

**For $B' = (\frac{61}{10}, -\frac{79}{20})$**

**For $C' = (\frac{29}{2}, \frac{29}{4})$**

Verification: $|A'C'| = 15$, $|B'C'| = 14$, $|A'B'| = 13$ ✓

## Step 6: Calculate hexagon area using shoelace formula

Vertices in order: $A, A', C', C, B, B'$

$$(5, 12), (-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{29}{4}), (\frac{29}{2}, \frac{29}{4}), (14, 0), (0, 0), (\frac{61}{10}, -\frac{79}{20})$$

Applying shoelace formula:
$$2 \cdot \text{Area} = \left|\sum_{i=1}^{6} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)\right|$$

Computing each term carefully:
- $5 \cdot \frac{29}{4} - (-\frac{1}{2}) \cdot 12 = \frac{145}{4} + 6$
- $(-\frac{1}{2}) \cdot \frac{29}{4} - \frac{29}{2} \cdot \frac{29}{4} = -\frac{29}{8} - \frac{841}{8}$
- $\frac{29}{2} \cdot 0 - 14 \cdot \frac{29}{4} = -\frac{406}{4}$
- $14 \cdot 0 - 0 \cdot 0 = 0$
- $0 \cdot (-\frac{79}{20}) - \frac{61}{10} \cdot 0 = 0$
- $\frac{61}{10} \cdot 12 - 5 \cdot (-\frac{79}{20}) = \frac{732}{10} + \frac{79}{4}$

Sum: $\frac{3663}{20}$

$$\text{Area} = \frac{3663}{40} \approx 91.575$$

Rounding to nearest integer:

**92**
