# Solution: Extended Reasoning

## Step 1: Set up coordinate system
- Place $B$ at the origin: $B = (0, 0)$
- Place $A$ at: $A = (1, 0)$
- This gives $|AB| = 1$ ✓

## Step 2: Find coordinates of $A'$ after first rotation

Rotating $A = (1, 0)$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B = (0, 0)$:

$$A' = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta \\ \sin\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

So $A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$

## Step 3: Verify the distance $A'B$

$$|A'B| = \sqrt{\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta} = 1$$ ✓

This confirms the rotation property preserves distance.

## Step 4: Find coordinates of $B'$ after second rotation

We rotate $B = (0, 0)$ **clockwise** by angle $\theta$ around $A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$.

The clockwise rotation matrix is:
$$R_{-\theta} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

Applying the rotation around center $A'$:
$$B' = A' + R_{-\theta}(B - A')$$

Computing the rotated displacement:
$$R_{-\theta}(B - A') = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ -\sin\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \begin{pmatrix} -\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta \\ \sin\theta\cos\theta - \sin\theta\cos\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

Therefore:
$$B' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta) + (-1, 0) = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 5: Apply the constraint $AB' = \frac{4}{3}$

With $A = (1, 0)$ and $B' = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$:

$$|AB'|^2 = (\cos\theta - 1 - 1)^2 + (\sin\theta - 0)^2$$

$$= (\cos\theta - 2)^2 + \sin^2\theta$$

$$= \cos^2\theta - 4\cos\theta + 4 + \sin^2\theta$$

Using $\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$:

$$|AB'|^2 = 1 - 4\cos\theta + 4 = 5 - 4\cos\theta$$

Since $AB' = \frac{4}{3}$, we have $|AB'|^2 = \frac{16}{9}$:

$$5 - 4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9}$$

$$-4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9} - 5 = \frac{16 - 45}{9} = -\frac{29}{9}$$

$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$

## Step 6: Verification

Check: $|AB'|^2 = 5 - 4 \cdot \frac{29}{36} = 5 - \frac{29}{9} = \frac{45-29}{9} = \frac{16}{9}$ ✓

## Step 7: Express in required form

$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$ where:
- $\gcd(29, 36) = 1$ (since 29 is prime and doesn't divide 36)
- $m = 29$, $n = 36$

$$m + n = 29 + 36 = 65$$

**65**
