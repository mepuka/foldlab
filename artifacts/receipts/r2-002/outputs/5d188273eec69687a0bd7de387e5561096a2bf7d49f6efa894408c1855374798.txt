# Solution: Step-by-Step Verification

## Step 1: Coordinate System Setup ✓
- Center at origin
- Disk in $xy$-plane ($z = 0$), radius 200
- Hemisphere: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$ for $z \geq 0$

This is correct.

## Step 2: Sphere's Center Position ✓
- Sphere has radius 42 and rests on the disk
- Contact point: $P = (p, 0, 0)$ where $p \geq 0$ (by radial symmetry)
- Center position: $(p, 0, 42)$ (sphere sits directly above contact point)
- Sphere equation: $(x-p)^2 + y^2 + (z-42)^2 = 42^2$

This is correct.

## Step 3: Containment Condition ✓
The sphere must lie entirely within the hemisphere:
$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 200^2 \text{ for all points on the sphere}$$

The farthest point on the sphere from the origin lies along the radial direction through the center.

This is correct.

## Step 4: Distance Calculation ✓
Distance from origin to sphere's center:
$$d_{\text{center}} = \sqrt{p^2 + 42^2}$$

Farthest point distance:
$$d_{\text{max}} = \sqrt{p^2 + 42^2} + 42$$

Constraint:
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 42^2} + 42 \leq 200$$

This is correct.

## Step 5: Solving for p ✓

$$\sqrt{p^2 + 42^2} \leq 158$$

Squaring both sides:
$$p^2 + 42^2 \leq 158^2$$

Computing: $42^2 = 1764$ and $158^2 = 24964$

$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$
$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

Factoring: $23200 = 400 \times 58$

$$p \leq \sqrt{23200} = \sqrt{400 \times 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$$

This is correct.

## Step 6: Areas Calculation ✓

**Region $\mathcal{T}$** (disk of radius $20\sqrt{58}$):
$$\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \times 400 \times 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Disk** (radius 200):
$$\text{Area}_{\text{disk}} = \pi \times 200^2 = 40000\pi$$

This is correct.

## Step 7: Simplifying the Ratio ✓

$$\frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Simplifying by dividing both by 100:
$$\frac{232}{400}$$

Dividing by 4:
$$\frac{58}{100}$$

Dividing by 2:
$$\frac{29}{50}$$

Checking: Is $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$? 
- 29 is prime
- $50 = 2 \times 5^2$
- No common factors ✓

Therefore: $p = 29$ and $q = 50$

## Step 8: Final Answer ✓

$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$

ANSWER: 79
