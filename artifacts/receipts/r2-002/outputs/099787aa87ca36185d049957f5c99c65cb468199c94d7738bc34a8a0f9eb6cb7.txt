# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Set up coordinate system

Place $B$ at the origin and $A$ on the positive $x$-axis:
- $B = (0, 0)$
- $A = (1, 0)$

## Step 2: Find coordinates of $A'$ after first rotation

Rotate $A = (1, 0)$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B = (0, 0)$:
$$A' = (1 \cdot \cos\theta, 1 \cdot \sin\theta) = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 3: Verify $|A'B| = 1$

$$|A'B| = \sqrt{(\cos\theta)^2 + (\sin\theta)^2} = \sqrt{\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta} = \sqrt{1} = 1$$ ✓

This confirms the distance is preserved under rotation.

## Step 4: Find coordinates of $B'$ after second rotation

Rotate $B = (0, 0)$ clockwise by angle $\theta$ around $A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$.

**Translate** so $A'$ is at the origin:
$$B - A' = (0, 0) - (\cos\theta, \sin\theta) = (-\cos\theta, -\sin\theta)$$

**Apply clockwise rotation matrix** (rotation by $-\theta$):
$$\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ -\sin\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \begin{pmatrix} -\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta \\ \sin\theta\cos\theta - \sin\theta\cos\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -(\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

**Translate back** by adding $A'$:
$$B' = (-1, 0) + (\cos\theta, \sin\theta) = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 5: Apply distance constraint $|AB'| = \frac{4}{3}$

Calculate $|AB'|^2$ where $A = (1, 0)$ and $B' = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$:

$$|AB'|^2 = [(\cos\theta - 1) - 1]^2 + [\sin\theta - 0]^2$$

$$= (\cos\theta - 2)^2 + \sin^2\theta$$

$$= \cos^2\theta - 4\cos\theta + 4 + \sin^2\theta$$

$$= (\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) - 4\cos\theta + 4$$

$$= 1 - 4\cos\theta + 4 = 5 - 4\cos\theta$$

Set this equal to $\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{16}{9}$:

$$5 - 4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9}$$

## Step 6: Solve for $\cos\theta$

$$-4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9} - 5$$

$$-4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9} - \frac{45}{9} = -\frac{29}{9}$$

$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$

## Step 7: Express in required form

We have $\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$.

**Check that $\gcd(29, 36) = 1$:**
- $29$ is prime
- $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$
- Since $29 \neq 2$ and $29 \neq 3$, we have $\gcd(29, 36) = 1$ ✓

Therefore: $m = 29$ and $n = 36$

$$m + n = 29 + 36 = 65$$

ANSWER: 65
