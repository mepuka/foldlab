# Solution: Enumerating Values of n = a + b + ab

## Step 1: Rewrite in Factored Form

Starting with $n = a + b + ab$:
$$n = a + b + ab = (a+1)(b+1) - 1$$

Verification: $(a+1)(b+1) - 1 = ab + a + b + 1 - 1 = ab + a + b$ ✓

For $n \leq 100$, we need $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$.

## Step 2: Determine Constraints

With $a, b \geq 1$ and $a \neq b$:
- Both $(a+1)$ and $(b+1)$ are at least 2
- Their product must not exceed 101

## Step 3: Key Observation About Parity

For $n = a + b + ab$ to be even, we need both $a$ and $b$ to be **even** (since if either is odd and the other odd, we get odd + odd + odd = odd; if one is odd and one even, we get odd + even + even = odd).

**For odd n:** Both $a, b$ must be odd, OR we need $a \neq b$ generally from the odd cases.

Let me enumerate systematically:

### Odd Numbers (from $a=1$):
When $a = 1$: $n = 1 + b + b = 1 + 2b$ with $b \geq 2$
- $2(b+1) \leq 101 \Rightarrow b \leq 49$
- Values: $n \in \{5, 7, 9, 11, ..., 99\}$ (all odd numbers from 5 to 99)
- **Count:** $(99-5)/2 + 1 = 48$ values

### Even Numbers (both $a, b$ even):
For $(a,b) = (2i, 2j)$ where $i \neq j$ and $i, j \geq 1$:
- $(2i+1)(2j+1) \leq 101$
- Values generated: $n = 2i + 2j + 4ij$

Systematically:
- $a=2, b \in \{4,6,8,...,32\}$: gives $n \in \{14, 20, 26, 32, 38, 44, 50, 56, 62, 68, 74, 80, 86, 92, 98\}$ (15 values)
- $a=4, b \in \{6,8,10,...,18\}$: gives $n \in \{34, 44, 54, 64, 74, 84, 94\}$ (7 new: $\{34, 54, 64, 84, 94\}$)
- $a=6, b \in \{8,10,12\}$: gives $n \in \{62, 76, 90\}$ (1 new: $\{76, 90\}$)
- $a=8, b=10$: gives $n = 98$ (duplicate)

Continuing this enumeration, the achievable even numbers $\leq 100$ are:
$$\{14, 20, 26, 32, 34, 38, 44, 50, 54, 56, 62, 64, 68, 74, 76, 80, 84, 86, 90, 92, 94, 98\}$$

**Count of even values:** 22

(Note: Some even numbers like 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 18, 22, 24, 28, 30, 36, 40, 42, 46, 48, 52, 58, 60, 66, 70, 72, 78, 82, 88, 96, 100 cannot be expressed in this form)

## Step 4: Collect All Distinct Values

- **Odd values:** 48
- **Even values:** 22  
- **Total distinct values:** $48 + 22 = 70$

## Final Answer

**70**
