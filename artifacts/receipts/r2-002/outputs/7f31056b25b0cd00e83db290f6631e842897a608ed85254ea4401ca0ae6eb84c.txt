# Analysis of the Competition Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Definition of "imbalanced"**: A positive integer $n$ is imbalanced if strictly more than 99% of its positive divisors are strictly less than 1% of $n$.
   - Mathematically: If $\tau(n)$ denotes the number of positive divisors and $d(n)$ denotes the count of divisors $d$ satisfying $d < 0.01n$, then $d(n) > 0.99 \cdot \tau(n)$.

2. **$M$ is a multiple of 2000**: 
   - $2000 = 2^4 \cdot 5^3$
   - Therefore $M = 2^a \cdot 5^b \cdot \prod p_i^{c_i}$ where $a \geq 4$, $b \geq 3$, and $p_i$ are primes other than 2 and 5.

3. **$M$ is imbalanced**: The imbalance condition must be satisfied.

## What Is Being Asked

**Find the minimum possible number of positive divisors of $M$** (i.e., minimize $\tau(M)$).

In other words: Among all positive integers $M$ that are (i) multiples of 2000 and (ii) imbalanced, determine the smallest possible value of $\tau(M)$.
