# Solution with Extended Reasoning

## Step 1: Simplify the Radical Expression

Starting with the equation: $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}=26x$

Rewrite the left side:
$$\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}} = (x^{\log_{2026}x})^{1/20} = x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}$$

So our equation becomes:
$$x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}} = 26x$$

## Step 2: Take Logarithm Base 2026 of Both Sides

Applying $\log_{2026}$ to both sides:
$$\log_{2026}\left(x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}\right) = \log_{2026}(26x)$$

Using the power rule on the left side:
$$\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x = \log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

$$\frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$$

## Step 3: Convert to Quadratic Form

Let $y = \log_{2026}x$. Substituting:
$$\frac{y^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + y$$

Multiply both sides by 20:
$$y^2 = 20y + 20\log_{2026}26$$

Rearrange to standard form:
$$y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$$

## Step 4: Solve Using Quadratic Formula

For $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$:

$$y = \frac{20 \pm \sqrt{400 + 80\log_{2026}26}}{2} = 10 \pm \sqrt{100 + 20\log_{2026}26}$$

This gives us two solutions:
- $y_1 = 10 + \sqrt{100 + 20\log_{2026}26}$
- $y_2 = 10 - \sqrt{100 + 20\log_{2026}26}$

## Step 5: Convert Back to $x$

Since $y = \log_{2026}x$, we have $x = 2026^y$:
- $x_1 = 2026^{y_1}$
- $x_2 = 2026^{y_2}$

## Step 6: Find Product of All Solutions

$$P = x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2026^{y_1} \cdot 2026^{y_2} = 2026^{y_1 + y_2}$$

By **Vieta's formulas**, for the quadratic $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$:
$$y_1 + y_2 = 20$$

Therefore:
$$P = 2026^{20}$$

## Step 7: Factor and Count Divisors

Factor 2026 into primes:
$$2026 = 2 \times 1013$$

(Note: 1013 is prime, verified by checking divisibility by all primes up to $\sqrt{1013} \approx 31.8$)

Therefore:
$$P = 2026^{20} = (2 \times 1013)^{20} = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$$

Using the divisor formula: for $n = p_1^{a_1} \times p_2^{a_2}$, we have $\tau(n) = (a_1+1)(a_2+1)$

$$\tau(P) = (20+1)(20+1) = 21 \times 21 = 441$$
