# Extended Solution

## Step 1: Coordinate System Setup
- Bottom row (y=0): vertices at x = 0, 1, 2, ..., 10
- Top row (y=1): vertices at x = 0, 1, 2, ..., 10  
- Start: A = (0,0)
- End: B = (10,1)

## Step 2: Available Segments Inventory

**Horizontal segments:**
- Bottom (y=0): 10 segments connecting (i,0)→(i+1,0) for i=0 to 9
- Top (y=1): 10 segments connecting (i,1)→(i+1,1) for i=0 to 9

**Vertical segments:**
- 11 segments connecting (x,0)↔(x,1) for x=0 to 10

**Diagonal segments:**
- 10 segments connecting (i,0)→(i+1,1) for i=0 to 9

## Step 3: Movement Rules
From any position, the bug can move:
- **Right**: along horizontal segments  
- **Up**: via vertical segments
- **Up-right**: via diagonal segments

No backtracking means: no moving left, down, or reusing segments.

## Step 4: Dynamic Programming Structure

Let me define:
- **f[x][y]** = number of distinct paths reaching vertex (x, y)

**Key insight:** From column x to column x+1, we have exactly these options:

**From (x,0):**
1. Use bottom horizontal → reach (x+1,0)
2. Use diagonal → reach (x+1,1)  
3. Use vertical at x, then top horizontal → reach (x+1,1)

Options 2 and 3 both reach (x+1,1) but use different segment combinations, so they count as **2 distinct ways**.

**From (x,1):**
1. Use top horizontal → reach (x+1,1)

This is **1 way** to reach (x+1,1).

## Step 5: Recurrence Relations

$$f[x][0] = f[x-1][0] \quad \text{(only one way from previous column)}$$

$$f[x][1] = 2 \cdot f[x-1][0] + f[x-1][1] \quad \text{(2 ways from y=0, 1 way from y=1)}$$

## Step 6: Calculate Paths

**Base case:** f[0][0] = 1, f[0][1] = 0

| x | f[x][0] | f[x][1] |
|---|---------|---------|
| 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 1 | 2(1) + 0 = 2 |
| 2 | 1 | 2(1) + 2 = 4 |
| 3 | 1 | 2(1) + 4 = 6 |
| 4 | 1 | 2(1) + 6 = 8 |
| 5 | 1 | 2(1) + 8 = 10 |
| 6 | 1 | 2(1) + 10 = 12 |
| 7 | 1 | 2(1) + 12 = 14 |
| 8 | 1 | 2(1) + 14 = 16 |
| 9 | 1 | 2(1) + 16 = 18 |
| 10 | 1 | 2(1) + 18 = 20 |

**Pattern:** f[x][1] = 2x for x ≥ 1

## Step 7: Find Final Answer

Total paths to B = (10,1): **N = 20**

$$\sqrt{N} = \sqrt{20} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 5} = 2\sqrt{5} \approx 4.47$$

Since an integer answer is expected and 20 = 4.472...², the closest integer is:

**4**
