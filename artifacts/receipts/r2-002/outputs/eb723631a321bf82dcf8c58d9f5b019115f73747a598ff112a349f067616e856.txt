# Solving for Moons with All Three Characteristics

## Step 1: Define Variables for Each Region

For three overlapping sets (Blue, Icy, Large), there are 7 non-overlapping regions:
1. Blue only
2. Icy only
3. Large only
4. Blue and Icy, but not Large
5. Blue and Large, but not Icy
6. Icy and Large, but not Blue
7. Blue AND Icy AND Large = **x** (what we're solving for)

## Step 2: Assign Known Quantities to Their Regions

**"Blue but not Icy" = 10**
- This includes: (Blue only) + (Blue and Large, but not Icy)
- Regions 1 and 5: 10 moons total

**"Icy but not Large" = 8**
- This includes: (Icy only) + (Blue and Icy, but not Large)
- Regions 2 and 4: 8 moons total

**"Large but not Blue" = 6**
- This includes: (Large only) + (Icy and Large, but not Blue)
- Regions 3 and 6: 6 moons total

## Step 3: Express All Regions in Terms of x

The three given conditions are **mutually exclusive** (a moon cannot satisfy two of them simultaneously):
- These three categories cover regions: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
- The uncovered region is only: 7 (the intersection of all three)

## Step 4: Apply the Partition Principle

All 29 moons must be distributed across all 7 regions:

$$\text{(Regions 1+5)} + \text{(Regions 2+4)} + \text{(Regions 3+6)} + \text{(Region 7)} = 29$$

## Step 5: Solve for x

$$10 + 8 + 6 + x = 29$$

Double-checking:
- Sum of three conditions: 10 + 8 + 6 = 24
- Total moons: 29
- Remaining: 29 - 24 = 5

$$24 + x = 29$$
$$x = 5$$

**Verification:** 
- 10 (blue but not icy) + 8 (icy but not large) + 6 (large but not blue) + 5 (all three) = 29 ✓

ANSWER: 5
