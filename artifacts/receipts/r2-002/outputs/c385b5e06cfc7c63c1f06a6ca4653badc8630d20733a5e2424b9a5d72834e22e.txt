# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Sequence Definition:**
- $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ is a sequence of nonnegative integers
- Each term satisfies: $0 \le a_n < 397$
- Initial condition: $a_0 = 1$

**Recurrence Relation:**
- For all nonnegative integers $n$: 
$$a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$$

**Additional Constraint:**
- The sequence is unique (satisfying all above conditions)
- $a_{2026} = 9$ (specific value at index 2026)

**Note on 397:**
- 397 is prime

## What is Being Asked

Compute the remainder when $\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{2026} a_i$ is divided by 397.

In other words, find:
$$\left(\sum_{i=0}^{2026} a_i\right) \bmod 397$$
