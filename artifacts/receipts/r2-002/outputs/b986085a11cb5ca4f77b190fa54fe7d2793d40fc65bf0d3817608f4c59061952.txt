# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Regular hexagon** with side length 4
2. **Lattice subdivision**: The hexagon is subdivided into 96 equilateral triangles of side length 1
3. **Vertex set S**: The set of all vertices of this triangular lattice
4. **Center of the hexagon**: A specific point (the geometric center)
5. **Triangle requirements**:
   - Vertices must be in S
   - Triangles must be nondegenerate (three non-collinear points)
   - The center must be **strictly in the interior** (not on the boundary)

## What is Being Asked

Count the number of triangles with vertices in S such that the center of the regular hexagon lies strictly inside each triangle.
