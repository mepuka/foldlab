# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Target range**: integers ≤ 100
2. **Form of numbers**: $n = a + b + ab$ for some choice of $a$ and $b$
3. **Constraint on $a$ and $b$**: 
   - Both must be positive integers
   - $a$ and $b$ must be distinct (i.e., $a \neq b$)

## What is Being Asked

Find how many distinct integers that are less than or equal to 100 can be expressed in the form $a + b + ab$ where $a$ and $b$ are distinct positive integers.

In other words: count the size of the set $\{n \in \mathbb{Z} : n \leq 100 \text{ and } n = a + b + ab \text{ for some distinct positive integers } a, b\}$.
