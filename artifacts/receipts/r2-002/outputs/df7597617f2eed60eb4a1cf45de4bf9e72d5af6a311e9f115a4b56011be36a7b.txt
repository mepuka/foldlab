# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Figure:** $ABCD$ is a rectangle
2. **Points:** 
   - $X$ is a point on segment $\overline{BC}$
   - $Y$ is a point on segment $\overline{AD}$
3. **Angle conditions:**
   - $\angle AXY = 90°$
   - $\angle XYC = 90°$
4. **Ratio condition:** $AX : XY : YC = 1 : 2 : 1$
5. **Length condition:** $AB = 1$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the length of $BC$ (the base of the rectangle).

---

**Note:** There appears to be a typo in the problem statement: "$\overlien{BC}$" should be "$\overline{BC}$". Also, the notation "$\angle XYC$" likely refers to the angle at vertex $Y$ in triangle $XYC$ (i.e., $\angle XYC$ is the angle formed by rays $YX$ and $YC$).
