## Step 1: Analyze the recurrence relation modulo 397

Given: $a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$

Since 397 is prime, we can rewrite this as:
$$a_{n+1} \equiv a_n(a_n + 1)^{-2} \pmod{397}$$

where $(a_n + 1)^{-1}$ is the multiplicative inverse modulo 397.

## Step 2: Work backwards from $a_{2026} = 9$

To find the inverse recurrence, we rearrange:
$$a_n \equiv a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \pmod{397}$$

This is a cubic equation in $a_n$ (since $(a_n+1)^2 \cdot a_n = a_{n+1}$), which typically has limited solutions modulo a prime.

## Step 3: Compute the forward sequence from $a_0 = 1$

**Finding $a_1$:**
- $a_1 = 1 \cdot (1+1)^{-2} = 2^{-2} \pmod{397}$
- Since $2 \cdot 199 = 398 \equiv 1 \pmod{397}$, we have $2^{-1} \equiv 199$
- $199^2 = 39601 = 99 \cdot 397 + 298 \equiv 298 \pmod{397}$
- So $a_1 \equiv 298 \pmod{397}$

**Finding $a_2$:**
- $a_2 = 298 \cdot (299)^{-2} \pmod{397}$
- Using extended Euclidean algorithm: $299^{-1} \equiv 239 \pmod{397}$
- $239^2 = 57121 = 143 \cdot 397 + 350 \equiv 350 \pmod{397}$
- $a_2 \equiv 298 \cdot 350 = 104300 \equiv 186 \pmod{397}$

**Finding $a_3$:**
- $a_3 = 186 \cdot (187)^{-2} \pmod{397}$
- $187^{-1} \equiv 138 \pmod{397}$
- $138^2 = 19044 \equiv 385 \pmod{397}$
- $a_3 \equiv 186 \cdot 385 = 71610 \equiv 150 \pmod{397}$
