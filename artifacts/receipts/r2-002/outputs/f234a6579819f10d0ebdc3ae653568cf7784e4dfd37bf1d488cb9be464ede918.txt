# Solution Plan

## 1. **Establish Coordinate System**
   - Place $A$ at the origin $(0, 0)$
   - Place $E$ at $(20, 0)$ on the positive $x$-axis
   - Use the $90°$ angles at $A$ and $E$ to determine that $AB \perp AE$ and $ED \perp AE$

## 2. **Locate Vertices $B$ and $D$**
   - Since $\angle A = 90°$: $B = (0, AB)$ where $AB > 0$
   - Since $\angle E = 90°$: $D = (20, DE)$ where $DE > 0$
   - All of $B$, $C$, $D$ are on the same side of $AE$ (same sign for $y$-coordinate)

## 3. **Use Angle Constraints at $B$ and $D$**
   - At $B$ with $\angle B = 45°$: determine the direction of side $BC$
   - At $D$ with $\angle D = 45°$: determine the direction of side $DC$
   - These directional constraints force $C$ to lie at a specific location based on $AB$ and $DE$

## 4. **Apply the $BC = 14\sqrt{2}$ Constraint**
   - Calculate the distance from $B$ to $C$ using the geometry from Step 3
   - Set this equal to $14\sqrt{2}$ to obtain a relationship between $AB$ and $DE$
   - Solve for $DE$ in terms of $AB$

## 5. **Enforce the Inequality $DE < AB$**
   - Apply the constraint from Step 4 to the inequality $DE < AB$
   - This yields an upper bound on $AB$

## 6. **Calculate the Pentagons Area**
   - Use the shoelace formula with coordinates of $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$
   - Express area as a function of $AB$ (and the relationship with $DE$ from Step 4)
   - The area should be a polynomial or rational function in $AB$

## 7. **Apply the Divisibility Constraint**
   - Require: Area $\equiv 0 \pmod{16}$
   - Use the formula from Step 6 to determine which integer values of $AB$ satisfy this
   - This constrains $AB$ to a specific residue class modulo some integer

## 8. **Verify Nonconvexity**
   - Check that the pentagon is actually nonconvex for valid solutions
   - Identify which interior angle is reflex (greater than $180°$)

## 9. **Combine All Constraints**
   - Lower bound: $AB > DE$ (from Step 5)
   - Upper bound: $AB < 2026$ (given)
   - Integer constraint: $AB \in \mathbb{Z}$
   - Divisibility constraint: Modular condition from Step 7
   - Count integer values in the valid range satisfying all conditions

## 10. **Final Count**

   - Return the total count
