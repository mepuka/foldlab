# Given Quantities and Conditions

## Given:
- Initial set of numbers: {1, 2, 3, ..., 2100} written on a board
- **Operation (performed repeatedly):** Select two numbers $a$ and $b$ on the board, erase them, and replace with $\gcd(a,b)$ and $\text{lcm}(a,b)$
- **Stopping condition:** Mark stops once any move will not change the numbers on the board (i.e., the multiset is invariant under the operation)

## Key Observations to Note:
- Each operation preserves the multiset of prime factorizations in a specific sense
- The operation is an involution on pairs (swapping a pair with their gcd and lcm returns to the original pair)
- The operation terminates because it is a finite system
- The stopping condition means we reach a "fixed point" state

## What is Being Asked:
Find the number of divisors of the 2026th smallest positive integer that appears on the board in the final state (when Mark stops).

**In other words:**
1. Determine the final multiset of numbers on the board when the process terminates
2. Sort these numbers in increasing order (with multiplicity)
3. Identify the 2026th number in this sorted list
4. Calculate the number of positive divisors of this 2026th number
