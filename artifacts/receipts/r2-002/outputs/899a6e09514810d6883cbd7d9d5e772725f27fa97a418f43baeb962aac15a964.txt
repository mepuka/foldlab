# Solution Plan

1. **Set up a coordinate system**
   - Place $A$ at the origin $(0, 0, 0)$
   - Place $B$ at $(6, 0, 0)$ along the positive $x$-axis
   - Place $C$ at $(0, 4, 0)$ along the positive $y$-axis
   - Plane $\mathcal{P}$ is the $xy$-plane ($z = 0$)

2. **Locate point $D$**
   - Find the centroid $G$ of $\triangle ABC$: $G = \left(2, \frac{4}{3}, 0\right)$
   - Find the line $BC$ and its perpendicular bisector
   - Reflect $G$ across line $BC$ to get point $D$

3. **Determine sphere centers**
   - Sphere 1 (radius 1): center at $O_1 = (0, 0, 1)$
   - Sphere 2 (radius 2): center at $O_2 = (6, 0, 2)$
   - Sphere 3 (radius 3): center at $O_3 = (0, 4, 3)$
   - Sphere 4 (radius $r$): center at $O_4 = (D_x, D_y, r)$ where $(D_x, D_y)$ are the coordinates of $D$

4. **Find the equation of plane $\mathcal{T}$**
   - Plane $\mathcal{T}$ has form $ax + by + cz = d$ (with unit normal $\vec{n}$)
   - The distance from each sphere's center to plane $\mathcal{T}$ equals its radius
   - Use three spheres (radii 1, 2, 3) to write three distance equations
   - Solve for the plane equation

5. **Apply the tangency condition for the fourth sphere**
   - Use the plane equation from step 4
   - Apply the distance condition for sphere 4 (radius $r$)
   - Solve for $r$

6. **Simplify and finalize**
   - Express $r$ as a fraction $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms
   - Verify that $\gcd(m, n) = 1$
   - Calculate and report $m + n$
