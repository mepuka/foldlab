Since we need exactly 4 total swaps across all elements, the number of swaps consumed by element 4 can be 0, 1, 2, or 3 (leaving 4, 3, 2, or 1 swaps for other elements).

- Position 3: |3-4| = **1 swap** (remaining: 3 swaps)
- Position 4: |4-4| = **0 swaps** (remaining: 4 swaps)

(Positions 1,2,6,7 would require 3,2,2,3 swaps respectively, which can work, but the most efficient arrangements use positions 3,4,5)
