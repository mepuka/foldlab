# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Objects:**
- Two circles $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ that intersect at two points $P$ and $Q$
- Two common external tangents $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ of $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$

**Tangency Points:**
- $\Gamma_1$ touches $\ell_1$ at $U_1$ and touches $\ell_2$ at $U_2$
- $\Gamma_2$ touches $\ell_1$ at $V_1$ and touches $\ell_2$ at $V_2$

**Given Measurements:**
- $PQ = 10$ (distance between the two intersection points)
- Distance from $P$ to $\ell_1$ equals $3$
- Distance from $P$ to $\ell_2$ equals $12$

# What is Being Asked

Compute the area of the quadrilateral $U_1U_2V_2V_1$ (the quadrilateral formed by the four tangency points).
