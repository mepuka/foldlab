# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

1. **Triangle ABC** with a specific configuration
2. **Point D** on segment $\overline{BC}$
3. **Segment AD** bisects $\angle BAC$ (angle bisector)
4. **Circle ω** that:
   - Passes through point $A$
   - Is tangent to segment $\overline{BC}$ at point $D$
5. **Points E and F**:
   - $E \neq A$ is the second intersection of $\omega$ with segment $\overline{AB}$
   - $F \neq A$ is the second intersection of $\omega$ with segment $\overline{AC}$
6. **Side lengths**: $AB = 200$ and $AC = 225$
7. **Integrality conditions**: All of $AE$, $AF$, $BD$, and $CD$ are positive integers

## What is Being Asked:

Find the sum of all possible values of $BC$.

---

## Key Implicit Relationships:

From the angle bisector theorem: $\frac{BD}{CD} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{200}{225} = \frac{8}{9}$

From power of a point and tangency conditions, combined with the circle through $A$, $E$, $F$, $D$:
- $AE \cdot AB = AF \cdot AC$ (power relationships)
- This gives $AE \cdot 200 = AF \cdot 225$, so $AE = \frac{9AF}{8}$

The constraint that $AE$ and $AF$ are both positive integers determines the feasible values, which then determines possible values of $BC$ through the angle bisector relation.
