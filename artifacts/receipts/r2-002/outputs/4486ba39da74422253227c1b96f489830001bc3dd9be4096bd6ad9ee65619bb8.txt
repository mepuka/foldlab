# Plan for Solving the Problem

1. **Simplify the radical expression**
   - Rewrite $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}$ as $x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}$
   - This converts the equation to: $x^{\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20}}=26x$

2. **Take logarithm base 2026 of both sides**
   - Apply $\log_{2026}$ to get: $\frac{\log_{2026}x}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x = \log_{2026}(26x)$
   - Simplify the right side: $\frac{(\log_{2026}x)^2}{20} = \log_{2026}26 + \log_{2026}x$

3. **Convert to a quadratic equation**
   - Let $y = \log_{2026}x$
   - Rearrange: $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$
   - Or equivalently: $y^2 - 20y - 20\log_{2026}26 = 0$

4. **Solve the quadratic for $y$**
   - Use the quadratic formula: $y = \frac{20 \pm \sqrt{400 + 80\log_{2026}26}}{2}$
   - Simplify: $y = 10 \pm \sqrt{100 + 20\log_{2026}26}$

5. **Convert solutions back to $x$**
   - For each value of $y_i$, compute $x_i = 2026^{y_i}$
   - This gives two positive solutions: $x_1 = 2026^{y_1}$ and $x_2 = 2026^{y_2}$

6. **Find the product of all solutions**
   - Compute $P = x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2026^{y_1 + y_2}$
   - Note that $y_1 + y_2 = 20$ (sum of roots from Vieta's formulas)
   - Therefore: $P = 2026^{20}$

7. **Factor and count divisors**
   - Factor 2026 into primes: $2026 = 2 \times 1013$
   - Then $P = 2026^{20} = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$
   - Use the divisor formula: $\tau(P) = (20+1)(20+1) = 21 \times 21 = 441$
