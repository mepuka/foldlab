   - Use condition $SE \parallel UN$ to express $E$ as: $E = S + t_2 \cdot \overrightarrow{UN}$ for some scalar $t_2$

   - Compute area of pentagon $VENUS$ using the shoelace formula in terms of $t_1$ and $t_2$

   - Use the coordinate formula for the area of triangle $EUV$ in terms of $t_1$ and $t_2$

   - Maximize the area of triangle $EUV$ subject to:
     - Pentagon area constraint: $\text{Area}(VENUS) = 84$
     - Convexity constraint: pentagon $VENUS$ must be convex
   - Use calculus (Lagrange multipliers) or parametric analysis to find the critical point(s)
