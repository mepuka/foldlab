# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Die specification**: A standard fair six-sided die with outcomes {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
2. **Rolling process**: The die is rolled repeatedly
3. **Coin distribution rules**:
   - Outcomes 1 or 2 (probability 2/6 = 1/3): Alice gets a coin
   - Outcomes 3 or 4 (probability 2/6 = 1/3): Bob gets a coin
   - Outcomes 5 or 6 (probability 2/6 = 1/3): Carol gets a coin

4. **Stopping condition**: The rolling stops once Carol receives at least one coin

5. **Target event**: Before Carol gets any coins, both Alice AND Bob each receive at least 2 coins

## What is Being Asked

Find the probability that, during the sequence of rolls before Carol receives her first coin, Alice accumulates at least 2 coins **and** Bob accumulates at least 2 coins simultaneously.

Express this probability as $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms (where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$), and compute $100m + n$.
