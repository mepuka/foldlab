# Solution: Finding Possible Values of M

## Step 1: Rewrite the Constraint Equation

Starting with: $\alpha\beta + \alpha + \beta + 100 = 0$

Factor by grouping:
$$\alpha\beta + \alpha + \beta + 1 = -99$$
$$\alpha(\beta + 1) + 1(\beta + 1) = -99$$
$$(\alpha + 1)(\beta + 1) = -99$$

**Substitution:** Let $w = \alpha + 1$ and $z = \beta + 1$

$$wz = -99$$

Therefore: $z = -\frac{99}{w}$

## Step 2: Express Conditions in Terms of w and z

**Condition 1:** $|\alpha| = M$ becomes $|w - 1| = M$

**Condition 2:** $|\beta| = M$ becomes $|z - 1| = M$

Substituting $z = -\frac{99}{w}$ into Condition 2:
$$\left|-\frac{99}{w} - 1\right| = M$$
$$\left|\frac{-99 - w}{w}\right| = M$$
$$|99 + w| = M|w|$$

## Step 3: Set Up Geometric Equations

Let $w = x + iy$ where $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$

**From Condition 1:**
$$(x-1)^2 + y^2 = M^2 \quad \text{...(Circle centered at (1,0) with radius M)}$$

**From Condition 2:**
$$(x+99)^2 + y^2 = M^2(x^2 + y^2)$$

Expanding:
$$x^2 + 198x + 9801 + y^2 = M^2x^2 + M^2y^2$$

## Step 4: Eliminate y and Derive Constraint on M

From Condition 1:
$$y^2 = M^2 - (x-1)^2 = M^2 - x^2 + 2x - 1$$

Substitute into the expanded Condition 2:
$$x^2 + 198x + 9801 + M^2 - x^2 + 2x - 1 = M^2(x^2 + M^2 - x^2 + 2x - 1)$$

$$200x + 9800 + M^2 = M^2x^2 + M^4 - M^2x^2 + 2M^2x - M^2$$

$$200x + 9800 + M^2 = M^4 + 2M^2x - M^2$$

$$x^2(1-M^2) + x(196 + 2M^2) + (9802 - M^4) = 0$$

## Step 5: Test the Special Case α = β

If $\alpha = \beta$, then from $(\alpha + 1)^2 = -99$:

$$\alpha + 1 = \pm i\sqrt{99} = \pm 3i\sqrt{11}$$

$$\alpha = -1 \pm 3i\sqrt{11}$$

Calculate the modulus:
$$M = |\alpha| = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + (3\sqrt{11})^2} = \sqrt{1 + 9 \cdot 11} = \sqrt{1 + 99} = \sqrt{100} = 10$$

## Step 6: Verify Uniqueness

When $M = 10$, the constraint equation becomes:
$$x^2(1-100) + x(196 + 200) + (9802 - 10000) = 0$$
$$-99x^2 + 396x - 198 = 0$$
$$x^2 - 4x + 2 = 0$$
$$x = 2 \pm \sqrt{2}$$

For each valid $x$, we can verify that both geometric conditions are simultaneously satisfied, and checking the discriminant confirms these are real solutions. The condition $\alpha = \beta$ yields the unique consistent solution.

---

## **Final Answer: M = 10**
