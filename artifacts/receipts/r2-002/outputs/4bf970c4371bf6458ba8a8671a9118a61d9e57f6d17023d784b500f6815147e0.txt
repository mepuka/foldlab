# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities

1. $HO = 20$
2. $SE = 26$
3. $OS = 10$

## Given Conditions

1. $HORSE$ is a convex pentagon
2. $\angle EHO = 90°$
3. $\angle ORS = 90°$
4. $\angle SEH = 90°$
5. $\angle HOR = 135°$
6. $\angle RSE = 135°$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the area of pentagon $HORSE$.
