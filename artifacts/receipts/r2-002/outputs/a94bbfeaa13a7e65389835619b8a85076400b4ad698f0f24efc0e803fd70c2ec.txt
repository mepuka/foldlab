# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Set S:**
- $S$ consists of all ordered pairs $(x, y)$ where:
  - $x \in \{0, 1, 2, \ldots, 19\}$ (20 values)
  - $y \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ (3 values)
  - Total: $|S| = 60$ pairs

**Permutation Structure:**
- We seek permutations $(x_1, y_1), (x_2, y_2), \ldots, (x_{60}, y_{60})$ of all 60 elements of $S$

**Required Conditions:**

1. **Starting condition:** $y_1 = 2$

2. **Ending condition:** $y_{60} = 0$

3. **Transition rule** (for each $i$ with $1 \leq i \leq 59$, exactly one must hold):
   - **Vertical move:** $x_i = x_{i+1}$ and $|y_i - y_{i+1}| = 1$
     - (Same $x$-coordinate, adjacent $y$-coordinates)
   - **Horizontal move:** $y_i = y_{i+1}$ and $x_i - x_{i+1} \in \{-1, 19\}$
     - (Same $y$-coordinate, with $x$-coordinate changing by $-1$ or $19$ modulo 20)

## What is Being Asked

**Compute:** The total number of valid permutations of $S$ satisfying all the above conditions.
