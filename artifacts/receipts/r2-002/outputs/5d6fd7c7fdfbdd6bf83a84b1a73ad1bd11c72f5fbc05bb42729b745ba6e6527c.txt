# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities:
1. Triangle $\triangle ABC$ lies in plane $\mathcal{P}$
2. $AB = 6$
3. $AC = 4$
4. $\angle BAC = 90°$
5. Four spheres with radii: $1, 2, 3,$ and $r$ (unknown)
6. Sphere tangency points on plane $\mathcal{P}$: $A, B, C,$ and $D$ respectively
7. $D$ is the reflection across $\overline{BC}$ of the centroid of $\triangle ABC$
8. All four spheres are on the same side of plane $\mathcal{P}$
9. All four spheres are tangent to a second plane $\mathcal{T}$
10. All four spheres are on the same side of plane $\mathcal{T}$

## Given Conditions:
- The sphere of radius 1 is tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $A$
- The sphere of radius 2 is tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $B$
- The sphere of radius 3 is tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $C$
- The sphere of radius $r$ is tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $D$
- Each sphere is simultaneously tangent to plane $\mathcal{T}$
- All four spheres lie on the same side of both planes $\mathcal{P}$ and $\mathcal{T}$

## What is Being Asked:
Find the value of $r$, and express it as a fraction $\tfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms (where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$). Then compute and report $m + n$.
