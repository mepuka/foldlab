# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **What Mark writes:** Squares of several distinct positive integers (in base 10)
2. **Constraint on the integers:** They must be distinct positive integers
3. **Constraint on the digits:** Each nonzero digit (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9) appears exactly once across all the written squares combined
4. **Allowed digits:** The digit 0 may appear any number of times (or not at all)
5. **Digit count requirement:** Exactly 9 nonzero digits total, with no digit repeated

## What is Being Asked

Find the **smallest possible sum** of all the perfect squares written on the blackboard, subject to the constraints above.

In other words: minimize $\sum n_i^2$ where the $n_i$ are distinct positive integers whose squares collectively contain each digit 1-9 exactly once (and possibly contain 0's).
