# Solution: Finding Moons with All Three Characteristics

## Step 1: Define Variables for the 7 Regions

In a three-set Venn diagram, there are 7 non-overlapping regions:
- Let x = B ∩ I ∩ L (blue AND icy AND large)
- Let a = Blue only
- Let b = Icy only  
- Let c = Large only
- Let d = Blue ∩ Icy (not large)
- Let e = Blue ∩ Large (not icy)
- Let f = Icy ∩ Large (not blue)

## Step 2: Assign Known Quantities

**"Blue but not icy"** = a + e = 10
- (This includes: blue only + blue and large but not icy)

**"Icy but not large"** = b + d = 8
- (This includes: icy only + icy and blue but not large)

**"Large but not blue"** = c + f = 6
- (This includes: large only + large and icy but not blue)

## Step 3: Sum the Known Regions

Adding all three constraints:
(a + e) + (b + d) + (c + f) = 10 + 8 + 6

**a + b + c + d + e + f = 24**

## Step 4: Apply the Total Constraint

All 29 moons must be distributed across all 7 regions:

a + b + c + d + e + f + x = 29

## Step 5: Solve for x

Substituting the sum from Step 3:

24 + x = 29

**x = 5**

## Verification
- Moons with exactly one or two characteristics: 24
- Moons with all three characteristics: 5
- Total: 24 + 5 = 29 ✓
