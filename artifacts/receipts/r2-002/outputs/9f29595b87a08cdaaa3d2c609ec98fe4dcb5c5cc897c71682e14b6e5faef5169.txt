# Analysis of Given Quantities and Conditions

## Given Quantities:
- $n$ integers arranged in a circle
- At least one integer equals $0$
- The sum of all integers equals $24$

## Given Conditions:
1. **At least one of the integers is $0$**
2. **Each pair of adjacent integers differs by exactly $1$** (meaning consecutive integers in the circle differ by $\pm 1$)
3. **The sum of the integers is exactly $24$**

## What is Being Asked:
Find the smallest value of $n$ (the number of integers in the circle) for which all three conditions can be simultaneously satisfied.
