# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Initial Setup:**
- Two one-liter flasks (A and B), each with capacity 1 liter
- Flask A: initially completely filled with pure liquid mercury
- Flask B: initially partially filled with liquid gallium (amount unspecified)

**Operations (in sequence):**
1. Pour all contents of flask A into flask B until flask B is full
2. Mix the contents of flask B thoroughly
3. Pour the mixture from flask B back into flask A until flask A is full again

**Constraint:**
- After both operations, the mixture remaining in flask B is exactly 30% mercury

## What Is Being Asked

Find the percentage composition of mercury in flask A after both operations are complete. Express this as $x$ (where the mixture in flask A is $x\%$ mercury).
