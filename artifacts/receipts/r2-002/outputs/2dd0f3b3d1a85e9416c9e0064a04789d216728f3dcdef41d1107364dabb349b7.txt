## Step 1: Establish the Constraint on Prime Factors

For any prime $p$ dividing $n$:
- If $p \mid (\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!$, then $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$
- For divisibility: $n \mid [(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450]$ requires $p \mid 450$

Since $450 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$, we need:
$$n = 2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c \text{ for some } a,b,c \geq 0$$

## Step 2: Determine the Bound
