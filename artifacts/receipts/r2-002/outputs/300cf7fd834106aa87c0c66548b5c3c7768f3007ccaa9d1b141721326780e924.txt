# Detailed Solution Verification

## Step 1: Coordinate System Setup ✓
- Center at origin
- Disk in xy-plane at z = 0
- Hemisphere: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$ with $z \geq 0$

## Step 2: Sphere's Center Position ✓
The distance between the two planes is 84, so the sphere has radius 42.
- Sphere touches disk at $P = (p, 0, 0)$ where $p \geq 0$
- By radial symmetry, center is at $(p, 0, 42)$
- Sphere equation: $(x-p)^2 + y^2 + (z-42)^2 = 42^2$ ✓

## Step 3: Containment Condition ✓
Every point on the sphere must satisfy $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 200^2$

The farthest point from the origin lies along the radial direction from origin through the sphere's center.

## Step 4: Distance Formula ✓
- Distance from origin to sphere's center: $\sqrt{p^2 + 42^2} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764}$
- Farthest point from origin: $\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42$ (center distance + radius)

## Step 5: Solve for Allowed Region ✓

$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42 \leq 200$$

$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} \leq 158$$

Square both sides:
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 158^2$$

Calculate $158^2 = (160-2)^2 = 25600 - 640 + 4 = 24964$ ✓

$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$

$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

Factor 23200:
$$23200 = 400 \times 58 = (20)^2 \times 58$$

$$p \leq \sqrt{23200} = 20\sqrt{58}$$ ✓

## Step 6: Calculate Areas ✓

**Region $\mathcal{T}$** (by rotational symmetry, a disk of radius $20\sqrt{58}$):
$$\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \cdot 400 \cdot 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Original disk** (radius 200):
$$\text{Area}_{\text{disk}} = \pi \cdot 200^2 = 40000\pi$$

## Step 7: Compute the Ratio ✓

$$\frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Find $\gcd(23200, 40000)$:
- $23200 = 2^5 \times 5^2 \times 29$
- $40000 = 2^6 \times 5^4$
- $\gcd = 2^5 \times 5^2 = 800$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{23200 \div 800}{40000 \div 800} = \frac{29}{50}$$

## Step 8: Verify and Answer ✓

Check that $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$:
- 29 is prime
- $50 = 2 \times 5^2$
- No common factors ✓

Therefore: $p = 29$ and $q = 50$

$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$

**79**
