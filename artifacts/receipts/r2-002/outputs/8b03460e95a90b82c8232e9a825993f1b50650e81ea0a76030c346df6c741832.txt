## Step 1: Understand the Matching Structure

For each element $s \in S$, it matches with some element in $T$ where the matched element must be either $s-1$ or $s+1$.

Since $S$ and $T$ are disjoint and every element of $S$ must have a cousin (a match) in $T$, we need a perfect matching where each $s \in S$ pairs with exactly one of $\{s-1, s+1\}$ (and that number must not be in $S$).

## Step 5: Recount with Gaps Between Blocks
