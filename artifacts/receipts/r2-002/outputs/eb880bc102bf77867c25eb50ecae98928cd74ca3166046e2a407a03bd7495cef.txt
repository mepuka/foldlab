# Solution: Maximum Area of Triangle EUV

## Step 1: Set up a coordinate system

I'll place triangle $SUN$ with:
- $U = (0, 0)$ at the origin
- $N = (8, 0)$ on the positive $x$-axis  
- $S = (0, 6)$

**Verification:** Area$(SUN) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot 6 = 24$ ✓

## Step 2: Apply parallel conditions

**For condition $NV \parallel SU$:**
- $\overrightarrow{SU} = (0, 0) - (0, 6) = (0, -6)$
- $V = N + t_1 \cdot \overrightarrow{SU} = (8, 0) + t_1(0, -6) = (8, -6t_1)$

**For condition $SE \parallel UN$:**
- $\overrightarrow{UN} = (8, 0) - (0, 0) = (8, 0)$
- $E = S + t_2 \cdot \overrightarrow{UN} = (0, 6) + t_2(8, 0) = (8t_2, 6)$

## Step 3: Express pentagon area

Pentagon $VENUS$ has vertices in order: $V = (8, -6t_1)$, $E = (8t_2, 6)$, $N = (8, 0)$, $U = (0, 0)$, $S = (0, 6)$

Using the shoelace formula:
$$\sum (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i) = 8(6) - 8t_2(-6t_1) + 8t_2(0) - 8(6) + 0 - 0 + 0 - 0 + 0(-6t_1) - 8(6)$$

$$= 48 + 48t_1 t_2 - 48 - 48 = 48t_1 t_2 - 48$$

$$\text{Area}(VENUS) = \frac{1}{2}|48t_1 t_2 - 48| = 24|t_1 t_2 - 1|$$

**Setting this equal to 84:**
$$24|t_1 t_2 - 1| = 84$$
$$|t_1 t_2 - 1| = 3.5$$

For a convex pentagon with $t_1, t_2 > 0$:
$$t_1 t_2 - 1 = 3.5 \implies t_1 t_2 = 4.5$$

## Step 4: Area of triangle EUV

Triangle $EUV$ has vertices $E = (8t_2, 6)$, $U = (0, 0)$, $V = (8, -6t_1)$

Using the coordinate formula:
$$\text{Area}(EUV) = \frac{1}{2}|8t_2(0-(-6t_1)) + 0 + 8(6-0)|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2}|48t_1 t_2 + 48|$$
$$= 24|t_1 t_2 + 1|$$

Since $t_1, t_2 > 0$: 
$$\text{Area}(EUV) = 24(t_1 t_2 + 1)$$

## Step 5: Optimize

The constraint from the pentagon area gives us $t_1 t_2 = 4.5$ (the unique valid configuration for a convex pentagon).

Substituting into the area formula:
$$\text{Area}(EUV) = 24(4.5 + 1) = 24 \times 5.5 = 132$$

## Step 6: Verify

- Pentagon area constraint: $24(4.5 - 1) = 24 \times 3.5 = 84$ ✓
- Triangle area: $24(4.5 + 1) = 132$ ✓
- Convexity: Satisfied for $t_1, t_2 > 0$ ✓
