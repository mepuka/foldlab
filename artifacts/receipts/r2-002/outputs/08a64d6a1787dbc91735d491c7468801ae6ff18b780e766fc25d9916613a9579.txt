# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Initial string**: SUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUN
   - This is the word "SUN" repeated 10 times, giving a total length of 30 letters
   - Positions: S₁U₁N₁S₂U₂N₂S₃U₃N₃...S₁₀U₁₀N₁₀

2. **Erasure requirement**: Erase exactly 26 letters

3. **Remaining letters requirement**: After erasure, exactly 4 letters remain

4. **Order requirement**: The 4 remaining letters must spell "SUNS" **in order** (reading left to right through the original string)

## What is Being Asked

Find the number of ways to select 4 positions from the 30-position string such that:
- The letters at these 4 positions, in left-to-right order, are S, U, N, S (respectively)
- Equivalently: count ordered 4-tuples (i, j, k, ℓ) where 1 ≤ i < j < k < ℓ ≤ 30, the letter at position i is S, the letter at position j is U, the letter at position k is N, and the letter at position ℓ is S.
