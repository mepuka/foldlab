# Analysis of Given Quantities and Conditions

## Given Quantities:
- Total number of moons: **29**
- Moons that are blue but not icy: **10**
- Moons that are icy but not large: **8**
- Moons that are large but not blue: **6**

## Given Conditions:
- Each moon has **at least one** of these three characteristics: blue (B), icy (I), or large (L)
- Some moons may have multiple characteristics (overlaps are allowed)

## What is Being Asked:
Find the number of moons that are **simultaneously blue, icy, AND large** (i.e., the size of the intersection $B \cap I \cap L$).
