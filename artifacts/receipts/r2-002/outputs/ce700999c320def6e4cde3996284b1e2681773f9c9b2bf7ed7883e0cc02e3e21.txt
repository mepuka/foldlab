# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Equiangular pentagon**: All five interior angles are equal (each measuring 108°)

2. **Sum of squares of side lengths**: $s_1^2 + s_2^2 + s_3^2 + s_4^2 + s_5^2 = 308$
   - where $s_i$ denotes the five side lengths

3. **Sum of squares of diagonal lengths**: $d_1^2 + d_2^2 + d_3^2 + d_4^2 + d_5^2 = 800$
   - where $d_i$ denotes the five diagonals (lines connecting non-adjacent vertices)

## What Is Being Asked

Find the square of the perimeter of the pentagon:
$$P^2 = (s_1 + s_2 + s_3 + s_4 + s_5)^2$$

Express this value in the form $m\sqrt{n}$ where:
- $m$ and $n$ are positive integers
- $n$ is square-free (not divisible by any perfect square other than 1)

**Final Answer**: Report $m + n$.
