# Finding the Minimum Sum of Squares Using Digits 1-9 Exactly Once

## Step 1: Establish the Theoretical Minimum

Let me first understand what we're looking for:
- We need distinct positive integers whose squares collectively contain each digit 1-9 exactly once
- We want to minimize the sum of the squares
- Zeros are allowed and don't count against our constraint

The theoretical absolute minimum (ignoring the digit constraint) would be:
1² + 2² + 3² + 4² + 5² + 6² + 7² + 8² + 9² = 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + 25 + 36 + 49 + 64 + 81 = **285**

However, this uses the *numbers* 1-9, not necessarily giving us the *digits* 1-9. Their squares are: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81 - which collectively contain digits {1,4,9,6,2,5,3,4,6,4,9,8,1} - this uses many repeats and doesn't work.
