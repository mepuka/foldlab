# Solution: Counting Lattice Paths with Unique Edges

## Step 1: Coordinate System Setup
✓ Bottom row (y=0): vertices (0,0), (1,0), ..., (10,0)
✓ Top row (y=1): vertices (0,1), (1,1), ..., (10,1)
✓ Start: A = (0,0), End: B = (10,1)

## Step 2: Available Segments
Between each pair of x-coordinates (x, x+1), there are:
- **Horizontal (y=0)**: (x,0) → (x+1,0)
- **Horizontal (y=1)**: (x,1) → (x+1,1)
- **Diagonal**: (x,0) ↔ (x+1,1)
- **Vertical at each x**: (x,0) ↔ (x,1)

## Step 3: Movement Rules
No backtracking in x-direction; each segment used exactly once.

## Step 4 & 5: Determine Transitions

From position **(x,0)**, there are 3 ways to reach x+1:
1. Horizontal to (x+1,0) → **reaches (x+1,0)**
2. Diagonal to (x+1,1) → **reaches (x+1,1)**
3. Vertical to (x,1), then horizontal to (x+1,1) → **reaches (x+1,1)**

From position **(x,1)**, there are 3 ways to reach x+1:
1. Horizontal to (x+1,1) → **reaches (x+1,1)**
2. Vertical to (x,0), then horizontal to (x+1,0) → **reaches (x+1,0)**
3. Vertical to (x,0), then diagonal to (x+1,1) → **reaches (x+1,1)**

**Recurrence Relations:**
- $f(x+1, 0) = 1 \cdot f(x, 0) + 1 \cdot f(x, 1)$
- $f(x+1, 1) = 2 \cdot f(x, 0) + 2 \cdot f(x, 1) = 2(f(x,0) + f(x,1))$

## Step 6: Calculate Path Counts

**Initial:** $f(0,0) = 1$, $f(0,1) = 0$

| x | f(x,0) | f(x,1) |
|---|--------|--------|
| 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 3 | 6 |
| 3 | 9 | 18 |
| 4 | 27 | 54 |
| 5 | 81 | 162 |
| 6 | 243 | 486 |
| 7 | 729 | 1,458 |
| 8 | 2,187 | 4,374 |
| 9 | 6,561 | 13,122 |
| 10 | 19,683 | **39,366** |

**Verification of pattern:** $f(x,0) = 3^{x-1}$ and $f(x,1) = 2 \cdot 3^{x-1}$ for x ≥ 1

## Step 7: Calculate the Answer

$$N = f(10,1) = 39,366 = 2 \times 3^9$$

$$\sqrt{N} = \sqrt{39,366} = \sqrt{2 \times 3^9} = \sqrt{2 \times 19,683}$$

Computing: $\sqrt{39,366} = 198.408...$

Double-checking: 
- $198^2 = 39,204$
- $199^2 = 39,601$
- $198.41^2 ≈ 39,366$ ✓

Rounding to nearest integer: **198**

ANSWER: **198**
