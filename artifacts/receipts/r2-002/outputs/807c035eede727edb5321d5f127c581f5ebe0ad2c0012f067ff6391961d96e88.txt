# Solution

## Step 1: Define variables
- Patrick's speed: $p$ mph
- Tanya's speed: $p + 2$ mph
- Jose's speed: $p + 9$ mph

## Step 2: Set up equations
Let $t$ = time until they all arrive (measured from when Patrick starts)
- Patrick travels: $d = p \cdot t$
- Tanya travels: $d = (p+2)(t-1)$ (starts 1 hour later)
- Jose travels: $d = (p+9)(t-2)$ (starts 2 hours later)

## Step 3: Equate Patrick's and Tanya's distances
$$p \cdot t = (p+2)(t-1)$$
$$pt = pt - p + 2t - 2$$
$$0 = -p + 2t - 2$$
$$p = 2t - 2 \quad \text{...(1)}$$

## Step 4: Equate Patrick's and Jose's distances
$$p \cdot t = (p+9)(t-2)$$
$$pt = pt - 2p + 9t - 18$$
$$0 = -2p + 9t - 18$$
$$p = \frac{9t - 18}{2} \quad \text{...(2)}$$

## Step 5: Solve the system
Set equation (1) equal to equation (2):
$$2t - 2 = \frac{9t - 18}{2}$$

Multiply both sides by 2:
$$4t - 4 = 9t - 18$$
$$14 = 5t$$
$$t = \frac{14}{5}$$

Substitute back into equation (1):
$$p = 2\left(\frac{14}{5}\right) - 2 = \frac{28}{5} - \frac{10}{5} = \frac{18}{5}$$

**Verification:** $p = \frac{9(\frac{14}{5}) - 18}{2} = \frac{\frac{126}{5} - \frac{90}{5}}{2} = \frac{36}{10} = \frac{18}{5}$ ✓

## Step 6: Calculate the distance
$$d = p \cdot t = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$

**Verify with Tanya:** $(p+2)(t-1) = \left(\frac{28}{5}\right)\left(\frac{9}{5}\right) = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

**Verify with Jose:** $(p+9)(t-2) = \left(\frac{63}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right) = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

## Step 7: Check if in lowest terms
- $252 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$
- $25 = 5^2$

Since $\gcd(252, 25) = 1$, the fraction is already in lowest terms.

Therefore: $m = 252$ and $n = 25$

## Step 8: Final answer
$$m + n = 252 + 25 = 277$$

**277**
