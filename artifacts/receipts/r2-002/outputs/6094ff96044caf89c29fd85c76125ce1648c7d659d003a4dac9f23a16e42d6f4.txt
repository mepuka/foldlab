# Detailed Verification

## Step 1: Coordinate System ✓
- Origin at center of hemisphere and disk
- Disk in xy-plane: $z = 0$
- Hemisphere: upper half of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$ with $z \geq 0$

## Step 2: Sphere Placement ✓
- Contact point: $P = (p, 0, 0)$ on disk (using radial symmetry)
- Sphere center (42 units above disk): $(p, 0, 42)$
- Sphere equation: $(x-p)^2 + y^2 + (z-42)^2 = 42^2$

## Step 3: Containment Condition ✓
- All points on sphere must satisfy: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 200^2$
- The farthest point from origin is along the radial direction through the sphere's center

## Step 4: Distance Calculation ✓
- Distance from origin to sphere center: 
$$d = \sqrt{p^2 + 0^2 + 42^2} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764}$$

- Distance to farthest point on sphere:
$$d_{\text{max}} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42$$

- Constraint: $\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42 \leq 200$

## Step 5: Solving the Inequality ✓

$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} \leq 158$$

Square both sides:
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 158^2$$

Calculate $158^2 = 24964$:
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$
$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

Simplify $\sqrt{23200}$:
$$23200 = 400 \cdot 58 \implies \sqrt{23200} = 20\sqrt{58}$$

Therefore: $0 \leq p \leq 20\sqrt{58}$

## Step 6: Area Calculations ✓

**Region $\mathcal{T}$** (disk of radius $20\sqrt{58}$):
$$\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \cdot 400 \cdot 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Full disk** (radius 200):
$$\text{Area}_{\text{disk}} = \pi \cdot 200^2 = 40000\pi$$

## Step 7: Compute the Ratio ✓

$$\frac{\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}}}{\text{Area}_{\text{disk}}} = \frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

**Factor to reduce:**
- $23200 = 2^5 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 29 = 800 \cdot 29$
- $40000 = 2^6 \cdot 5^4 = 800 \cdot 50$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{29}{50}$$

**Verify coprimality:** 29 is prime and does not divide 50, so $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓

## Step 8: Final Answer ✓

With $p = 29$ and $q = 50$:
$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$

Final answer (single integer only): **79**
