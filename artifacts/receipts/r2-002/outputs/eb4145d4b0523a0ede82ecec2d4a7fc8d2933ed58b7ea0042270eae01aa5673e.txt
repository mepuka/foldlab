# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Squares:**
- $ABCD$ is a square
- $WXYZ$ is a square

**Position Constraints:**
- $W$ lies on segment $\overline{AD}$
- $X$ lies on segment $\overline{AB}$
- Points $Y$ and $Z$ lie strictly inside $ABCD$

**Measurements:**
- $AW = 4$
- $AX = 5$
- $AB = 12$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the area of triangle $\triangle BCY$.

---

## Notes on Interpretation

- Since $ABCD$ is a square with side length $AB = 12$, we have $AD = AB = BC = CD = 12$
- The vertices of square $ABCD$ are labeled in order (either clockwise or counterclockwise)
- Since $AW = 4$ and $W$ is on $\overline{AD}$, point $W$ divides $\overline{AD}$ with $WD = 8$
- Since $AX = 5$ and $X$ is on $\overline{AB}$, point $X$ divides $\overline{AB}$ with $XB = 7$
- The side length of square $WXYZ$ must be determined from the constraint that it is a square with two adjacent vertices at $W$ and $X$
- Point $Y$ is a vertex of square $WXYZ$ and lies strictly inside $ABCD$
