# Solution Plan

1. **Define variables for speeds**
   - Let Patrick's speed = $p$ mph
   - Then Tanya's speed = $p + 2$ mph
   - Then Jose's speed = $p + 9$ mph

2. **Set up equations using distance = speed × time**
   - Let $t$ = arrival time (same for all three)
   - Patrick's distance: $d = p \cdot t$
   - Tanya's distance: $d = (p+2)(t-1)$
   - Jose's distance: $d = (p+9)(t-2)$

3. **Equate Patrick's and Tanya's distances**
   - $p \cdot t = (p+2)(t-1)$
   - Expand and solve to get one equation relating $p$ and $t$

4. **Equate Patrick's and Jose's distances**
   - $p \cdot t = (p+9)(t-2)$
   - Expand and solve to get another equation relating $p$ and $t$

5. **Solve the system of two equations**
   - Use the two equations from steps 3 and 4 to solve for $p$ and $t$

6. **Calculate the distance**
   - Substitute the values of $p$ and $t$ into $d = p \cdot t$

7. **Express as a fraction in lowest terms**
   - Reduce $d$ to the form $\frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$

8. **Compute the final answer**
   - Calculate $m + n$
