# Detailed Solution

## Step 1: Establish Coordinate System
- Place $A$ at origin $(0, 0)$
- Place $E$ at $(20, 0)$ on the positive $x$-axis
- Right angles at $A$ and $E$ mean: $AB \perp AE$ and $ED \perp AE$

## Step 2: Locate Vertices $B$ and $D$
- $B = (0, AB)$ with $AB > 0$
- $D = (20, DE)$ with $DE > 0$
- All vertices on same side of $AE$ (positive $y$-coordinates)

## Step 3: Find $C$ Using the $45°$ Angle at $B$

At $B$, with $\angle ABC = 45°$:
- $\vec{BA} = (0, -AB)$
- $\vec{BC} = C - B = (x_C, y_C - AB)$

Using $\cos(45°) = \frac{\vec{BA} \cdot \vec{BC}}{|\vec{BA}||\vec{BC}|}$:

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{-AB(y_C - AB)}{AB \cdot 14\sqrt{2}}$$

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{-(y_C - AB)}{14\sqrt{2}}$$

$$y_C - AB = -14 \implies y_C = AB - 14$$

Since $|BC| = 14\sqrt{2}$:
$$x_C^2 + 196 = 392 \implies x_C = 14$$

Therefore: **$C = (14, AB - 14)$**

## Step 4: Find $DE$ Using the $45°$ Angle at $D$

At $D$, with $\angle CDE = 45°$:
- $\vec{DC} = (14 - 20, AB - 14 - DE) = (-6, AB - 14 - DE)$
- $\vec{DE} = (0, -DE)$

Using the angle formula:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{-DE(AB - 14 - DE)}{DE \cdot \sqrt{36 + (AB - 14 - DE)^2}}$$

Let $h = AB - 14 - DE$:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{-h}{\sqrt{36 + h^2}}$$

$$36 + h^2 = 2h^2 \implies h^2 = 36 \implies h = -6$$

Therefore: **$DE = AB - 8$**

For $DE > 0$: **$AB > 8$**

## Step 5: Calculate Pentagon Area Using Shoelace Formula

With vertices: $A(0,0)$, $B(0, AB)$, $C(14, AB-14)$, $D(20, AB-8)$, $E(20, 0)$

$$2 \cdot \text{Area} = \sum (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)$$

$$= 0 - 14AB + 14(AB-8) - 20(AB-14) - 20(AB-8) + 0$$

$$= -14AB + 14AB - 112 - 20AB + 280 - 20AB + 160$$

$$= 328 - 40AB$$

Adjusting for the nonconvex pentagon orientation:

$$\text{Area} = \frac{40AB - 328}{2} = 20AB - 164$$

For valid area: $AB > 8.2$, so **$AB \geq 9$**

## Step 6: Apply Divisibility Constraint

Area $\equiv 0 \pmod{16}$:
$$20AB - 164 \equiv 0 \pmod{16}$$

Since $20 \equiv 4 \pmod{16}$ and $164 \equiv 4 \pmod{16}$:
$$4AB \equiv 4 \pmod{16}$$

$$AB \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$$

## Step 7: Combine All Constraints

Valid values: $AB \in \{9, 13, 17, 21, \ldots, 2025\}$ with $AB \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$ and $AB < 2026$

These are values of the form $AB = 4k + 1$ where:
- Lower bound: $4k + 1 \geq 9 \implies k \geq 2$
- Upper bound: $4k + 1 \leq 2025 \implies k \leq 506$

Count: $506 - 2 + 1 = 505$

**505**
