# Solution: Permutations in S₆ with π⁶ = Identity

## Step 1: Identify the Mathematical Structure
We need permutations $\pi \in S_6$ such that $\pi^6 = \text{identity}$. This means the order of $\pi$ must divide 6.

## Step 2: Determine Possible Orders
Divisors of 6: **{1, 2, 3, 6}**

The order of a permutation equals the LCM of its cycle lengths.

## Step 3 & 4: Classify and Count by Order

**Using the formula:** For cycle type with $a_i$ cycles of length $i$:
$$\text{Count} = \frac{n!}{\prod_i i^{a_i} \cdot a_i!}$$

### Order 1 (Identity):
- Cycle type: (1,1,1,1,1,1)
- Count: $\frac{6!}{1^6 \cdot 6!} = 1$

### Order 2 (LCM = 2):

**Cycle type (2,1,1,1,1)** — one 2-cycle, four fixed points:
$$\frac{6!}{1^4 \cdot 4! \cdot 2^1 \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{24 \cdot 2} = 15$$

**Cycle type (2,2,1,1)** — two 2-cycles, two fixed points:
$$\frac{6!}{1^2 \cdot 2! \cdot 2^2 \cdot 2!} = \frac{720}{2 \cdot 4 \cdot 2} = 45$$

**Cycle type (2,2,2)** — three 2-cycles:
$$\frac{6!}{2^3 \cdot 3!} = \frac{720}{8 \cdot 6} = 15$$

**Order 2 Total: 15 + 45 + 15 = 75**

### Order 3 (LCM = 3):

**Cycle type (3,1,1,1)** — one 3-cycle, three fixed points:
$$\frac{6!}{1^3 \cdot 3! \cdot 3^1 \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{6 \cdot 3} = 40$$

**Cycle type (3,3)** — two 3-cycles:
$$\frac{6!}{3^2 \cdot 2!} = \frac{720}{9 \cdot 2} = 40$$

**Order 3 Total: 40 + 40 = 80**

### Order 6 (LCM = 6):

**Cycle type (6)** — one 6-cycle:
$$\frac{6!}{6^1 \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{6} = 120$$

**Cycle type (3,2,1)** — one 3-cycle, one 2-cycle, one fixed point:
$$\frac{6!}{1^1 \cdot 1! \cdot 2^1 \cdot 1! \cdot 3^1 \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{6} = 120$$

**Order 6 Total: 120 + 120 = 240**

## Step 5: Verify and Sum

| Order | Count |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 75 |
| 3 | 80 |
| 6 | 240 |
| **Total** | **396** |

**Final Answer:**

396
