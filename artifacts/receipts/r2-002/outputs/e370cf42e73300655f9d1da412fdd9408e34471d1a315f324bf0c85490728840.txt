## Quick Verification Method

Since $(a+1)(b+1) = n+1$ where $a, b \geq 1$ and $a \neq b$:
- We need $n+1$ to be a composite number (or have divisors $\geq 2$) with at least two distinct factor pairs
- Equivalently, $n+1$ must NOT be prime

For $n \leq 100$, we need $n+1 \leq 101$.

**Key insight:** A value $n$ is achievable if and only if $n+1$ is NOT prime.
