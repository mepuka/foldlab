# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **n is a positive integer**
2. **n has at least 6 positive integer divisors**
3. **The 6th largest divisor of n is 6**

## What is Being Asked

Find the sum of all positive integers $n$ satisfying all three conditions above.

---

## Key Clarifications

- The "6th largest divisor" means: if we list all divisors of $n$ in descending order, the 6th element in that list equals 6
- We need to find every such $n$ and add them together
