# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. An infinite series $S$ is defined as:
$$S = \frac{1}{9} + \frac{1}{99} + \frac{1}{999} + \frac{1}{9999} + \cdots$$

2. The general term of this series has the form $\frac{1}{10^n - 1}$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, 4, \ldots$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the remainder when $\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor$ (the greatest integer less than or equal to $10^{100} S$) is divided by $1000$.

In other words: Find $\lfloor 10^{100} S \rfloor \pmod{1000}$.
