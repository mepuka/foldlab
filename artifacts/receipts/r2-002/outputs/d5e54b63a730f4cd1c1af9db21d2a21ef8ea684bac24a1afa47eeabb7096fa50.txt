# Solution Plan

## Step 1: Understand Event B (All even stickers visible)
Determine what configurations satisfy event B:
- Sticker 2 must not be covered by stickers 3, 4, 5, or 6
- Sticker 4 must not be covered by stickers 5 or 6
- Sticker 6 must not be covered by anything (it's last)

This means:
- Sticker 6 lands on some face $f_6$
- Sticker 4 lands on some face $f_4 \neq f_6$
- Sticker 2 lands on some face $f_2 \notin \{f_4, f_6\}$

## Step 2: Count outcomes in Event B
Calculate $|B|$ by counting valid roll sequences:
- Roll 6: 6 choices for $f_6$
- Roll 5: 6 choices (any face, as sticker 5 will be covered)
- Roll 4: 5 choices (any face except $f_6$)
- Roll 3: 6 choices (any face, as sticker 3 will be covered)
- Roll 2: 4 choices (any face except $f_2, f_4, f_6$)
- Roll 1: 6 choices (any face, as sticker 1 will be covered)

Therefore: $|B| = 6 \times 6 \times 5 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6$

## Step 3: Understand Event A ∩ B
Determine which outcomes satisfy both conditions:
- From B: stickers 2, 4, 6 are on three distinct faces
- From A: exactly 5 faces have stickers (exactly 1 face is blank)

This means stickers 1, 3, 5 must land on the 3 remaining faces (not $f_2, f_4, f_6$).

## Step 4: Count outcomes in Event A ∩ B
For each outcome in B (with stickers 2, 4, 6 on faces $f_2, f_4, f_6$):
- Roll 5: must land on one of the 3 remaining faces: 3 choices
- Roll 3: must land on one of the 2 remaining faces: 2 choices
- Roll 1: must land on the 1 remaining face: 1 choice

Therefore: $|A \cap B| = 6 \times 3 \times 5 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1$

## Step 5: Calculate conditional probability
$$P(A|B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|B|} = \frac{6 \times 3 \times 5 \times 2 \times 4 \times 1}{6 \times 6 \times 5 \times 6 \times 4 \times 6}$$

Simplify by canceling common factors:
$$P(A|B) = \frac{3 \times 2 \times 1}{6 \times 6 \times 6} = \frac{6}{216} = \frac{1}{36}$$

## Step 6: Express in lowest terms and find answer
$$p = \frac{1}{36}$$

Since $\gcd(1, 36) = 1$, we have $m = 1$ and $n = 36$.

$$m + n = 1 + 36 = \boxed{37}$$
