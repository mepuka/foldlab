# Solution: Finding Permutations $\pi \in S_6$ with $\pi^6(a) = a$ for all $a$

## Step 1: Identify the Mathematical Structure

We need $\pi^6(a) = a$ for all $a \in A$, which means $\pi^6 = \text{identity}$.

This requires that the **order of $\pi$ divides 6**.

## Step 2: Determine Possible Orders

Divisors of 6: **{1, 2, 3, 6}**

We need to count all permutations in $S_6$ whose order divides 6.

**Key fact**: The order of a permutation equals the **LCM of its cycle lengths**.

---

## Step 3: Classify by Cycle Type

For a cycle type to have order dividing 6, we need $\text{lcm}(\text{cycle lengths}) \in \{1, 2, 3, 6\}$.

### Order 1
- Cycle type: (1,1,1,1,1,1) — the identity
- $\text{lcm} = 1$ ✓

### Order 2
- Single transposition: (2,1,1,1,1)
- Two disjoint transpositions: (2,2,1,1)
- Three disjoint transpositions: (2,2,2)
- All have $\text{lcm} = 2$ ✓

### Order 3
- Single 3-cycle: (3,1,1,1)
- Two disjoint 3-cycles: (3,3)
- All have $\text{lcm} = 3$ ✓

### Order 6
- One 6-cycle: (6)
- One 2-cycle and one 3-cycle: (3,2,1)
- All have $\text{lcm}(6) = 6$ or $\text{lcm}(2,3) = 6$ ✓

---

## Step 4: Count Permutations Using the Formula

For cycle type with $c_i$ cycles of length $i$:
$$\text{Count} = \frac{n!}{\prod c_i! \cdot \prod i^{c_i}}$$

### Order 1
- **(1,1,1,1,1,1)**: $\frac{6!}{6! \cdot 1^6} = 1$

**Subtotal: 1**

### Order 2
- **(2,1,1,1,1)**: $\frac{6!}{1! \cdot 4! \cdot 2^1} = \frac{720}{24 \cdot 2} = 15$

- **(2,2,1,1)**: $\frac{6!}{2! \cdot 2! \cdot 2^2} = \frac{720}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 4} = 45$

- **(2,2,2)**: $\frac{6!}{3! \cdot 2^3} = \frac{720}{6 \cdot 8} = 15$

**Subtotal: 15 + 45 + 15 = 75**

### Order 3
- **(3,1,1,1)**: $\frac{6!}{1! \cdot 3! \cdot 3^1} = \frac{720}{6 \cdot 3} = 40$

- **(3,3)**: $\frac{6!}{2! \cdot 3^2} = \frac{720}{2 \cdot 9} = 40$

**Subtotal: 40 + 40 = 80**

### Order 6
- **(6)**: $\frac{6!}{1! \cdot 6^1} = \frac{720}{6} = 120$

- **(3,2,1)**: $\frac{6!}{1! \cdot 1! \cdot 1! \cdot 3^1 \cdot 2^1} = \frac{720}{3 \cdot 2} = 120$

**Subtotal: 120 + 120 = 240**

---

## Step 5: Verify and Sum

Total count:
$$1 + 75 + 80 + 240 = \boxed{396}$$
