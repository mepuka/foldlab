- $B = (0, 0)$

## Step 2: Find coordinates of $A'$ after first rotation

Rotating $A = (1, 0)$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B = (0,0)$:

$$A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 3: Determine the distance $A'B$

$$|A'B| = \sqrt{\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta} = \sqrt{1} = 1$$ ✓

## Step 4: Find coordinates of $B'$ after second rotation

We rotate $B = (0,0)$ **clockwise** by angle $\theta$ around $A' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$.

For a clockwise rotation by angle $\theta$ around point $A'$, the rotation matrix is:
$$R_{-\theta} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

Translating so $A'$ is at origin, rotating, then translating back:
$$B' = A' + R_{-\theta}(B - A')$$

$$B' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta) + \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ -\sin\theta \end{pmatrix}$$

Computing the matrix product:
$$\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ -\sin\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta \\ \sin\theta\cos\theta - \sin\theta\cos\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

Therefore:
$$B' = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta) + (-1, 0) = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$$

## Step 5: Apply the distance constraint $AB' = \frac{4}{3}$

With $A = (1, 0)$ and $B' = (\cos\theta - 1, \sin\theta)$:

$$|AB'|^2 = (\cos\theta - 1 - 1)^2 + (\sin\theta - 0)^2$$

$$= (\cos\theta - 2)^2 + \sin^2\theta$$

$$= \cos^2\theta - 4\cos\theta + 4 + \sin^2\theta$$

$$= 1 - 4\cos\theta + 4 = 5 - 4\cos\theta$$

Setting $|AB'|^2 = \left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{16}{9}$:

$$5 - 4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9}$$

## Step 6: Solve for $\cos\theta$

$$-4\cos\theta = \frac{16}{9} - 5$$

$$-4\cos\theta = \frac{16 - 45}{9} = \frac{-29}{9}$$

$$\cos\theta = \frac{29}{36}$$
