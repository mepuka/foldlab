# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities:
1. Pentagon $ABCDE$ is **nonconvex**
2. $\angle A = 90°$ (angle at vertex $A$)
3. $\angle E = 90°$ (angle at vertex $E$)
4. $\angle B = 45°$ (angle at vertex $B$)
5. $\angle D = 45°$ (angle at vertex $D$)
6. $DE < AB$
7. $AE = 20$
8. $BC = 14\sqrt{2}$
9. Points $B$, $C$, and $D$ lie on the **same side of line $AE$**
10. $AB$ is an **integer**
11. $AB < 2026$
12. The area of pentagon $ABCDE$ is an **integer multiple of 16**

## Conditions Imposed:
- The pentagon is nonconvex (one internal angle must be reflex/greater than 180°)
- The geometric constraints from the angles and side lengths must be satisfiable
- The constraint that $B$, $C$, $D$ are collinear with respect to line $AE$
- $DE < AB$ (strict inequality)

## What is Being Asked:
**Find the number of possible integer values of $AB$** that simultaneously satisfy:
- All the geometric constraints and angle conditions
- The integer constraint: $AB \in \mathbb{Z}$
- The inequality constraint: $AB < 2026$
- The divisibility constraint: Area $\equiv 0 \pmod{16}$

In other words: Count how many distinct integer values the side length $AB$ can take.
