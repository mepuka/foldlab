# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Triangle structure**: $\triangle ABC$ is isosceles with $AB = BC$
2. **Point $I$**: the incenter of $\triangle ABC$
3. **Perimeter ratio**: $\frac{\text{perimeter of } \triangle ABC}{\text{perimeter of } \triangle AIC} = \frac{125}{6}$
4. **Integer side lengths**: All sides of both $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle AIC$ have integer lengths
5. **Triangle inequality**: Both triangles must satisfy the triangle inequality

## What is Being Asked

Find the **minimum possible value of $AB$** (which equals $BC$ by the isosceles condition).

---

## Setup for Solution

Let me denote:
- $AB = BC = c$ (the two equal sides)
- $AC = b$ (the base)
- Semiperimeter of $\triangle ABC$: $s = \frac{2c + b}{2}$
- The inradius: $r$

The three sides of $\triangle AIC$ are:
- $AI$ (distance from vertex to incenter)
- $IC$ (distance from vertex to incenter)
- $AC = b$

This problem requires finding integer side lengths for both triangles that satisfy the perimeter ratio constraint, and then determining which configuration yields the minimum value of $AB = c$.
