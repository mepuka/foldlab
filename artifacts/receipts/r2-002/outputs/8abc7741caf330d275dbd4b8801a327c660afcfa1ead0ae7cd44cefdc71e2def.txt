# Solution

## Step 1: Calculate Total Paths Without Restrictions

Kelvin moves from $(0,0)$ to $(5,5)$, requiring 5 right moves and 5 up moves.

$$\binom{10}{5} = \frac{10!}{5! \cdot 5!} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6}{120} = 252$$ ✓

## Step 2-3: Set Up Calculation for Each Cell

For a cell at position $(i,j)$ where $0 \leq i,j \leq 5$:
- Paths through this cell: $\binom{i+j}{i} \times \binom{(5-i)+(5-j)}{5-i}$
- Valid paths avoiding this cell: $N(i,j) = 252 - (\text{paths through cell})$

## Step 4: Compute for All 36 Cells

I'll organize as a 6×6 grid and systematically calculate each cell:

**Row 0:** $(0,j)$ for $j=0$ to $5$
- $(0,0)$: through = $1 \times 252 = 252$ → avoid = **0**
- $(0,1)$: through = $1 \times 126 = 126$ → avoid = **126**
- $(0,2)$: through = $1 \times 56 = 56$ → avoid = **196**
- $(0,3)$: through = $1 \times 21 = 21$ → avoid = **231**
- $(0,4)$: through = $1 \times 6 = 6$ → avoid = **246**
- $(0,5)$: through = $1 \times 1 = 1$ → avoid = **251**
- Subtotal: **1050**

**Row 1:** $(1,j)$ for $j=0$ to $5$
- $(1,0)$: $1 \times 126 = 126$ → **126**

**Row 2:** $(2,j)$ for $j=0$ to $5$

**Row 3:** By symmetry with Row 2: **1050**

## Step 5: Sum Across All Cells

$$\sum_{c=1}^{36} N(c) = 6 \times 1050 = 6300$$

## Step 6: Calculate Final Expectation

$$E[\text{valid paths}] = \frac{1}{36} \times 6300 = \frac{6300}{36} = 175$$
