# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Die and stickers**: A blank fair six-sided die and six stickers labeled 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (each displaying a different integer)

2. **Process**: 
   - Roll the die, place sticker labeled 1 on the top face
   - Roll the die, place sticker labeled 2 on the top face
   - Continue this process for stickers 3, 4, 5, 6 in order
   - If a sticker already occupies the top face, the new sticker covers (replaces) the old one
   - Stickers can only be visible if they were never covered by a subsequent sticker

3. **Fair die**: Each of the 6 faces has probability 1/6 of landing on top at each roll

4. **Condition (event $B$)**: All even-numbered stickers (2, 4, 6) are visible on faces of the die at the end of the process
   - This means stickers 2, 4, and 6 were never covered by any subsequent sticker

5. **Target event (event $A$)**: Exactly one face has been left blank at the end of the process
   - This means exactly 5 of the 6 faces have a sticker on them

## What Is Being Asked

Find the conditional probability $p = P(A|B)$, that is, the probability that exactly one face is blank **given that** stickers 2, 4, and 6 are all visible.

Express $p = \frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, and find $m + n$.
