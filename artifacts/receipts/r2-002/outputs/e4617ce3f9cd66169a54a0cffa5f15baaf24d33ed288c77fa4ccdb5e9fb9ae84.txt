# Plan to Solve for Moons with All Three Characteristics

1. **Define variables for each region of the Venn diagram**
   - Let x = moons that are blue, icy, AND large (B ∩ I ∩ L)
   - Identify the 7 non-overlapping regions created by three intersecting sets

2. **Assign known quantities to their regions**
   - Blue but not icy: 10 (this includes: blue only + blue and large but not icy)
   - Icy but not large: 8 (this includes: icy only + icy and blue but not large)
   - Large but not blue: 6 (this includes: large only + large and icy but not blue)

3. **Express all regions in terms of x**
   - Region "blue and icy but not large" = some portion of the remaining moons
   - Region "blue and large but not icy" = some portion of the remaining moons
   - Region "icy and large but not blue" = some portion of the remaining moons
   - Other single-characteristic regions

4. **Apply the partition principle**
   - All 29 moons must be distributed across all 7 regions
   - Sum all regions: (single characteristics) + (two-way intersections) + (three-way intersection) = 29

5. **Solve for x**
   - Use: 10 + 8 + 6 + (other regions) + x = 29
   - Account for the constraint that every moon has at least one characteristic
   - Solve the resulting equation to find x = **5**
