# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- Positive integers $n$ and $k$ with $k > 1$
- $s_k(n)$ = sum of digits of $n$ in base $k$
- Example: $s_3(2025) = 5$ because $2025 = 2210000_3$

**Definition of "digiroot":**
A positive integer $n$ is a digiroot if and only if:
$$s_2(n) = \sqrt{s_4(n)}$$

where $s_2(n)$ is the sum of digits in base 2 (binary) and $s_4(n)$ is the sum of digits in base 4.

## What is Being Asked

**Find:** The sum of all digiroots less than 1000.

In other words, find all positive integers $n < 1000$ satisfying $s_2(n) = \sqrt{s_4(n)}$, then add them all together.
