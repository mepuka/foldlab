# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Variable**: A positive integer $n$
2. **Expression to analyze**: $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$
3. **Divisibility condition**: $n$ divides $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$
4. **Factorization context**: $450 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$

## What is Being Asked

Find the **largest positive integer $n$** such that:
$$n \mid \left[(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450\right]$$

In other words, find the greatest $n$ for which the quantity $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$ is divisible by $n$.
