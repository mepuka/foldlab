# Solution: Finding the Ratio of Valid Positions

I'll execute this plan step by step.

## Step 1: Coordinate System Setup
- Origin at center of hemisphere and disk
- Disk in $xy$-plane ($z = 0$)
- Hemisphere: upper half of sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 200^2$

## Step 2: Sphere's Center Position
When a sphere of radius 42 rests on the disk at point $P = (p, 0, 0)$ with $p \geq 0$ (using radial symmetry):
- The sphere's center is at $(p, 0, 42)$
- Sphere equation: $(x-p)^2 + y^2 + (z-42)^2 = 42^2$

## Step 3: Containment Condition
For the sphere to lie completely inside the hemisphere, every point on the sphere must satisfy:
$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 200^2$$

The critical constraint is that the **farthest point** on the sphere from the origin must be at distance $\leq 200$.

## Step 4: Distance Calculation
The farthest point from the origin on the sphere lies along the radial direction from the origin through the center $(p, 0, 42)$.

**Distance from origin to center:**
$$d = \sqrt{p^2 + 0^2 + 42^2} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764}$$

**Distance to farthest point on sphere:**
$$d_{\max} = \sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42$$

**Constraint:**
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} + 42 \leq 200$$

## Step 5: Solve for Allowed Region
$$\sqrt{p^2 + 1764} \leq 158$$

Squaring both sides:
$$p^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$

$$p^2 \leq 23200$$

$$p \leq \sqrt{23200}$$

Simplifying $\sqrt{23200}$:
$$\sqrt{23200} = \sqrt{400 \cdot 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$$

## Step 6: Calculate Areas
**Region $\mathcal{T}$ (valid positions):** Disk of radius $20\sqrt{58}$
$$\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}} = \pi(20\sqrt{58})^2 = \pi \cdot 400 \cdot 58 = 23200\pi$$

**Original disk:** Radius $200$
$$\text{Area}_{\text{disk}} = \pi \cdot 200^2 = 40000\pi$$

## Step 7: Compute the Ratio
$$\frac{\text{Area}_{\mathcal{T}}}{\text{Area}_{\text{disk}}} = \frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Simplifying by dividing by 800:
$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{29}{50}$$

## Step 8: Verify and Final Answer
- $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓ (29 is prime, 50 = 2·5²)
- $p = 29$ and $q = 50$

$$p + q = 29 + 50 = \boxed{79}$$
