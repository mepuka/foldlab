# Solution

## Step 1: Count the Adjacencies

In an 8×8 grid:
- **Horizontal adjacencies:** 8 rows × 7 adjacencies per row = 56
- **Vertical adjacencies:** 8 columns × 7 adjacencies per column = 56
- **Total adjacencies:** 56 + 56 = 112

## Step 2: Optimal Arrangement Strategy

To maximize the sum of absolute differences, use a **checkerboard pattern**:
- Place numbers {1, 2, ..., 32} on black squares
- Place numbers {33, 34, ..., 64} on white squares

**Verification:** In a standard checkerboard:
- 32 black squares and 32 white squares exist
- Every adjacency (horizontal or vertical) connects opposite colors
- All 112 edges connect a black square to a white square ✓

## Step 3: Calculate Average Differences

For any edge connecting a black square (value from {1,...,32}) to a white square (value from {33,...,64}):
- The white value is always ≥ 33 and black value is always ≤ 32
- So the difference = white value - black value

**Average values:**
- Average of {1, 2, ..., 32} = (1 + 32)/2 = 16.5
- Average of {33, 34, ..., 64} = (33 + 64)/2 = 48.5
- Average difference per edge = 48.5 - 16.5 = **32**

## Step 4: Calculate Total Maximum

$$M_{\max} = 112 \text{ edges} \times 32 \text{ average difference} = 3584$$

**Verification by adjacency type:**
- 56 horizontal edges × 32 avg = 1792
- 56 vertical edges × 32 avg = 1792
- Total = 1792 + 1792 = 3584 ✓

## Step 5: Final Answer

$$M_{\max} \pmod{1000} = 3584 \pmod{1000} = 584$$

**584**
