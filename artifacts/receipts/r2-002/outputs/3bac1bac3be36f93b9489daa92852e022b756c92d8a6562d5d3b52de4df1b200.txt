   - Perimeter of $\triangle ABC$: $P_1 = 2c + b$
   - Perimeter of $\triangle AIC$: $P_2 = 2 \cdot AI + b$
   - Write the ratio: $\frac{2c + b}{2 \cdot AI + b} = \frac{125}{6}$

6. **Check triangle inequality conditions**

   - For $\triangle AIC$: $2 \cdot AI > b$ and $AI + b > AI$ (automatically satisfied)
