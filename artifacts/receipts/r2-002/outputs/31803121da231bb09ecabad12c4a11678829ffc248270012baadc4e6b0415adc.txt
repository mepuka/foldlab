# Analysis of Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Face Specifications:**
- Two isosceles triangle faces with side lengths $5\sqrt{10}, 5\sqrt{10}, 10$
- Two isosceles triangle faces with side lengths $5\sqrt{10}, 5\sqrt{10}, 18$

**Sphere Conditions:**
- All four vertices of the tetrahedron lie on a sphere with center $S$ (circumsphere)
- All four faces of the tetrahedron are tangent to a sphere with center $R$ (insphere)

**Constraint on Answer:**
- The distance $RS$ can be expressed as $\frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$

## What Is Being Asked

Find $m + n$, where $RS = \frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms.

In other words: **Calculate the distance between the circumcenter and incenter of the tetrahedron, express it as a fraction in lowest terms, and report the sum of numerator and denominator.**
