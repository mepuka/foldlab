# Solution: Counting Vertex Sets as Intersections with Half-Spaces

## Step 1: Clarify the Problem

We need to count all possible sets $M$ of vertices that can arise as the vertex set of the intersection of a decagonal prism $P$ with a half-space $H = \{x : \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{x} \leq b\}$.

**Key insight:** A subset $M$ of vertices of $P$ is the vertex set of some intersection $P \cap H$ if and only if $M$ is the vertex set of a face of $P$ (in the combinatorial sense, including all dimensions).

## Step 2: Identify the Face Lattice of a Decagonal Prism

The decagonal prism has:
- **10 vertices on top base** (call them $v_1, \ldots, v_{10}$)
- **10 vertices on bottom base** (call them $v'_1, \ldots, v'_{10}$)
- **Total: 20 vertices**

**Edges:**
- 10 edges on top base
- 10 edges on bottom base  
- 10 vertical edges connecting corresponding vertices
- **Total: 30 edges**

**Verify Euler characteristic:** $V - E + F = 2$
$$20 - 30 + F = 2 \implies F = 12$$

## Step 3: Enumerate All Faces

The faces of a decagonal prism are:

| Face Type | Count | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Empty face** | 1 | $\emptyset$ |
| **0-faces (vertices)** | 20 | Single vertices |
| **1-faces (edges)** | 30 | Line segments with 2 vertices each |
| **2-faces** | 12 | Two decagonal bases (1+1) + 10 rectangles |
| **3-face (whole prism)** | 1 | The entire polytope |

**Detailed breakdown of 2-faces:**
- Top decagonal base: 1 face with 10 vertices
- Bottom decagonal base: 1 face with 10 vertices
- Lateral rectangular faces: 10 faces with 4 vertices each

## Step 4: Verify Realizability

Each face is realizable as an intersection with a half-space:

- **Empty set:** Half-space entirely misses the prism
- **Vertices:** Supporting plane touches only at that vertex
- **Edges:** Supporting plane contains the edge and touches no other vertices
- **2-faces:** Supporting plane contains the face (either a base or lateral face)
- **Whole prism:** Half-space contains the entire prism (e.g., $H = \mathbb{R}^3$)

By the fundamental theorem of convex polytopes, every face has a supporting hyperplane, and conversely, every intersection with a half-space produces a face.

## Step 5: Sum All Faces

Total number of distinct sets $M$:

$$1 + 20 + 30 + 12 + 1 = \boxed{64}$$

---

**Final Answer:** 64
