# Solution: Counting Triangles Containing the Hexagon Center

**What I'm calculating:** A suitable coordinate system for the triangular lattice hexagon.

**Setup:**
- Place the hexagon center at the origin: O = (0, 0)
- Use Cartesian coordinates (x, y)
- For a regular hexagon with side length 4 on a triangular lattice, the vertices are positioned at:
  - V₁ = (4, 0)
  - V₂ = (2, 2√3) 
  - V₃ = (-2, 2√3)
  - V₄ = (-4, 0)
  - V₅ = (-2, -2√3)
  - V₆ = (2, -2√3)

**Verification:** These 6 points form a regular hexagon with center at (0,0) ✓

## Step 2: Count Total Lattice Points

**What I'm calculating:** Total number of lattice points S within the hexagon.

**Formula for regular hexagon with side length n:**
$$|S| = 3n^2 + 3n + 1$$

**Calculation:**
$$|S| = 3(4)^2 + 3(4) + 1 = 3(16) + 12 + 1 = 48 + 12 + 1 = 61$$

**Verification:** This is a known result for hexagonal grids. The number grows quadratically with side length. ✓

## Step 3: Identify the Center Location

**What I'm calculating:** Verify O is in the interior of the hexagon.

**Result:** O = (0, 0) is at the geometric center by construction.

**Distance check:** The distance from O to each vertex is 4, and the hexagon extends from -4 to +4 in the x-direction and from -2√3 to +2√3 in the y-direction. The origin lies strictly in the interior. ✓

## Step 4: Determine Triangle Containment Method

**What I'm calculating:** The method to identify triangles containing O.

**Barycentric Coordinate Method:**

For any triangle ABC with vertices A = (x₁, y₁), B = (x₂, y₂), C = (x₃, y₃), point O = (0, 0) lies strictly inside if and only if:

$$O = \alpha A + \beta B + \gamma C$$

where α, β, γ > 0 (strictly positive) and α + β + γ = 1.

**Calculation method:**

Using determinants, the barycentric coordinates are:
$$\alpha = \frac{\text{signed area}(O, B, C)}{\text{signed area}(A, B, C)}$$
$$\beta = \frac{\text{signed area}(A, O, C)}{\text{signed area}(A, B, C)}$$
$$\gamma = \frac{\text{signed area}(A, B, O)}{\text{signed area}(A, B, C)}$$

Where: $$\text{signed area}(P, Q, R) = \frac{1}{2}\begin{vmatrix} x_P & y_P & 1 \\ x_Q & y_Q & 1 \\ x_R & y_R & 1 \end{vmatrix}$$

**Condition:** O is strictly inside ABC ⟺ all three barycentric coordinates are strictly positive.

## Step 5: Systematic Enumeration

**What I'm calculating:** Count of all valid triangles.

**Total possible triangles:**
$$\binom{61}{3} = \frac{61 \times 60 \times 59}{6} = \frac{215,940}{6} = 35,990$$

**Enumeration process:**
For each of the 35,990 possible triangles ABC:
1. Check if A, B, C are collinear (using determinant = 0) → **skip**
2. For non-degenerate triangles, calculate the three barycentric coordinates of O
3. If α > 0, β > 0, AND γ > 0 → **count this triangle**

**Verification approach:** Due to 6-fold rotational symmetry of the hexagon, the count should be consistent with rotations by 60°.
