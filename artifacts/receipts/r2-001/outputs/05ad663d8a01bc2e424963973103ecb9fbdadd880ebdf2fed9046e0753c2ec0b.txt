# Solution Plan

1. **Establish the baseline:** Calculate the total number of paths from bottom-left to top-right on a 6×6 grid with no obstacles. This requires 5 moves right and 5 moves up, giving $\binom{10}{5} = 252$ total paths.

2. **Set up a coordinate system:** Label cells with coordinates $(x, y)$ where $(0,0)$ is bottom-left and $(5,5)$ is top-right, with $0 \leq x, y \leq 5$.

3. **For each cell $(x,y)$, calculate the number of paths that pass through it:**
   - Paths through $(x,y)$ = (paths from $(0,0)$ to $(x,y)$) × (paths from $(x,y)$ to $(5,5)$)
   - Paths to $(x,y)$: $\binom{x+y}{x}$
   - Paths from $(x,y)$: $\binom{(5-x)+(5-y)}{5-x}$
   - Paths through $(x,y)$: $\binom{x+y}{x} \times \binom{10-x-y}{5-x}$

4. **For each cell, calculate paths avoiding it:**
   - $N_i = 252 - (\text{paths through cell } i)$

5. **Sum across all 36 cells:**
   $$E = \frac{1}{36}\sum_{x=0}^{5}\sum_{y=0}^{5} \left[252 - \binom{x+y}{x}\binom{10-x-y}{5-x}\right]$$

6. **Simplify:**
   $$E = 252 - \frac{1}{36}\sum_{x=0}^{5}\sum_{y=0}^{5} \binom{x+y}{x}\binom{10-x-y}{5-x}$$

7. **Compute the double sum** by evaluating the binomial products for all 36 cells and sum them.

8. **Calculate the final answer** by subtracting the average "paths through a cell" from 252.
