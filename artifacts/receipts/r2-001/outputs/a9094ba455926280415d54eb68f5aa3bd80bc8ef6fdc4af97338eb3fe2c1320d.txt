4. **Find coordinates of $Y$ and $Z$**
   - The vector from $W$ to $X$ is $\vec{WX} = (5, -4)$
   - To form a square, rotate this vector 90° counterclockwise: $(4, 5)$
   - $Z = W + (4, 5) = (0, 4) + (4, 5) = (4, 9)$
   - $Y = X + (4, 5) = (5, 0) + (4, 5) = (9, 5)$

5. **Calculate the area of △BCY**
   - Vertices: $B = (12, 0)$, $C = (12, 12)$, $Y = (9, 5)$
   - Use the formula: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|x_B(y_C - y_Y) + x_C(y_Y - y_B) + x_Y(y_B - y_C)|$
   - $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|12(12-5) + 12(5-0) + 9(0-12)| = \frac{1}{2}|84 + 60 - 108| = \frac{1}{2}(36) = 18$

6. **State the answer**
   - The area of $\triangle BCY$ is $\boxed{18}$ square units.
