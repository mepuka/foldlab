# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Triangle ABC specifications:**
- Side length $AB = 13$
- Side length $BC = 14$
- Side length $CA = 15$

**Triangle A'B'C' specifications:**
- Obtained by rotating $\triangle ABC$ about its circumcenter
- After rotation: $\overline{A'C'} \perp \overline{B'C'}$ (the image of AC is perpendicular to the image of BC)
- Constraint: $A'$ and $B$ are not on the same side of line $B'C'$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the integer closest to the area of hexagon $AA'CC'BB'$.

---

## Key Observations

The rotation about the circumcenter preserves the triangle's shape and size (it's an isometry), so $\triangle A'B'C' \cong \triangle ABC$. The constraint $\overline{A'C'} \perp \overline{B'C'}$ determines a specific rotation angle. The hexagon $AA'CC'BB'$ is formed by the vertices of the two triangles in the order: $A \to A' \to C' \to C \to B' \to B \to A$ (or similar cyclic ordering), creating a hexagon whose area can be computed from the two triangle configurations.
