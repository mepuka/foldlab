# Solution Plan for Competition Math Problem

1. **Set up inclusion-exclusion equations**
   - Let $A$, $B$, $C$ represent the sets of full moon moments for Io, Europa, and Ganymede
   - Express: $|A \cup B \cup C| = 54$ and $|A \cap B| + |A \cap C| + |B \cap C| - 2|A \cap B \cap C| = 11$

2. **Determine the number of moments with exactly one full moon**
   - From $n_1 + n_2 + n_3 = 54$ and $n_2 + n_3 = 11$
   - Calculate: $n_1 = 43$ (moments with exactly one full moon)

3. **Express pairwise intersections in terms of $I$, $E$, $G$**
   - $|A \cap B| = \gcd(I, E)$ (moments when both Io and Europa have full moons)
   - $|A \cap C| = \gcd(I, G)$ (moments when both Io and Ganymede have full moons)
   - $|B \cap C| = \gcd(E, G)$ (moments when both Europa and Ganymede have full moons)
   - $|A \cap B \cap C| = \gcd(I, E, G)$ (moments when all three have full moons)

4. **Set up the key equation**
   - From the constraint on at least two full moons:
   - $\gcd(I, E) + \gcd(I, G) + \gcd(E, G) - 2\gcd(I, E, G) = 11$

5. **Find valid integer solutions**
   - Use the constraint $\gcd(I, E, G) \geq 1$
   - Test small values of $I$, $E$, $G$ that satisfy the equation
   - Verify all three conditions are met

6. **Calculate the final answer**
   - Compute $I \cdot E \cdot G$ for the valid solution(s)
