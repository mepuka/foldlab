# Solution Plan for Finding Sum of All Valid Radii

1. **Rewrite the parabola in standard form**
   - Convert $2y = x^2 - 8x + 12$ to vertex form: $y = \frac{1}{2}(x-2)^2 + 2$
   - Identify vertex at $(2, 2)$ and opening upward

2. **Set up the tangency condition**
   - A circle with center $(4, 39)$ and radius $r$ is tangent to the parabola when the distance from the center to a point on the parabola equals $r$, AND the radius is perpendicular to the parabola's tangent line at that point

3. **Use the perpendicularity requirement**
   - For point $(x, y)$ on the parabola, the parabola's slope is $\frac{dy}{dx} = x - 4$
   - For perpendicularity: the slope from $(4,39)$ to $(x,y)$ must satisfy: $\frac{y-39}{x-4} = -\frac{1}{x-4}$ (negative reciprocal)
   - This gives the condition: $(y-39)(x-4) = -1$

4. **Solve the system of equations**
   - Substitute $y = \frac{1}{2}(x-2)^2 + 2$ into $(y-39)(x-4) = -1$
   - Expand and simplify to get a polynomial equation in $x$
   - Solve for all valid $x$-coordinates of tangency points

5. **Calculate corresponding radii**
   - For each tangency point $(x, y)$ found in step 4, compute $r = \sqrt{(x-4)^2 + (y-39)^2}$

6. **Sum all valid radii**
   - Add together all positive radii values obtained in step 5
   - This is the final answer
