# Detailed Solution with Verification

## Step 1: Establish the Constraint Condition

The sphere has radius $r = 42$ and center at $(P_x, P_y, 42)$.

**Finding the farthest point:** The distance from the origin to the center is $d_c = \sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2}$. The farthest point on the sphere from the origin is at distance:
$$d_{\text{max}} = \sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42$$

**Constraint for being inside hemisphere:** For the sphere to lie completely inside a hemisphere of radius $R = 200$:
$$\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42 \leq 200$$ ✓

## Step 2: Simplify the Constraint

Rearranging:
$$\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} \leq 158$$

Squaring both sides (valid since both sides are non-negative):
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2 \leq 158^2$$

**Computing the values:**
- $42^2 = 1764$ ✓
- $158^2 = 24964$ (verified: $(160-2)^2 = 25600 - 640 + 4 = 24964$) ✓

Therefore:
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 \leq 23200$$ ✓

## Step 3: Identify Region $\mathcal{T}$

Region $\mathcal{T}$ is a disk centered at the origin with radius:
$$\rho = \sqrt{23200}$$

**Simplifying the radical:**
$$23200 = 100 \times 232 = 100 \times 4 \times 58 = 400 \times 58$$
$$\rho = \sqrt{400 \times 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$$ ✓

Note: $\rho^2 = 23200$

## Step 4: Calculate the Area Ratio

$$\frac{\text{Area of } \mathcal{T}}{\text{Area of disk}} = \frac{\pi\rho^2}{\pi R^2} = \frac{\rho^2}{R^2} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Check: $R^2 = 200^2 = 40000$ ✓

## Step 5: Reduce to Lowest Terms

**Factor both numerator and denominator:**
- $23200 = 100 \times 232 = 100 \times 8 \times 29 = 800 \times 29$
- $40000 = 100 \times 400 = 100 \times 8 \times 50 = 800 \times 50$

Therefore:
$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{232}{400}$$

**Find $\gcd(232, 400)$:**
- $232 = 8 \times 29$
- $400 = 8 \times 50$
- $\gcd(232, 400) = 8$

**Simplify:**
$$\frac{232}{400} = \frac{29}{50}$$

**Verify lowest terms:** Since 29 is prime and $50 = 2 \times 5^2$, we have $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓

## Step 6: Compute the Final Answer

With $p = 29$ and $q = 50$ (in lowest terms):
$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$

**Verification by substitution:** If the probability is $\frac{29}{50}$, then $p + q = 79$ ✓

FINAL ANSWER: **79**
