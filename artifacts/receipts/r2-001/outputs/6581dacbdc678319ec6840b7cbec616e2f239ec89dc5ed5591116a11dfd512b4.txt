# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Initial Setup:**
- Flask A: 1 liter capacity, initially completely filled with pure mercury
- Flask B: 1 liter capacity, initially partially filled with pure gallium (amount unspecified)

**Operations:**
1. Pour all contents of flask A into flask B until flask B is full (1 liter)
2. Mix the contents of flask B
3. Pour the mixture from flask B back into flask A until flask A is full (1 liter)

**Final Condition:**
- The mixture remaining in flask B is 30% mercury (by volume)

# What Is Being Asked

Find $x$, the percentage of mercury in the mixture now in flask A.
