# Solution

## Step 1: Verify the algebraic form

Expanding $(a+1)(b+1) - 1$:
$$(a+1)(b+1) - 1 = ab + a + b + 1 - 1 = a + b + ab$$ ✓

Therefore, $a + b + ab \leq 100$ is equivalent to $(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$.

## Step 2: Set up constraints

Let $m = a+1$ and $n = b+1$:
- Since $a \geq 1$: $m \geq 2$
- Since $b \geq 1$: $n \geq 2$
- Since $a \neq b$: $m \neq n$

**Task:** Find all distinct products $m \cdot n \leq 101$ where $m, n \geq 2$ and $m \neq n$.
