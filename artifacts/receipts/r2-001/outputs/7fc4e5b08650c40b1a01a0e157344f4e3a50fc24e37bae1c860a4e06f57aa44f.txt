# Analysis of the Competition Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- A positive integer $n$ is defined to be *imbalanced* if strictly more than 99% of its positive divisors are strictly less than 1% of $n$
- $M$ is a multiple of $2000$
- $M$ is imbalanced

**Specifically for the imbalanced condition:**
- Let $\tau(M)$ = the number of positive divisors of $M$
- Let $d$ = a positive divisor of $M$
- The threshold is $1\% \text{ of } M = 0.01M$
- Let $A$ = the count of divisors $d$ where $d < 0.01M$
- The condition requires: $A > 0.99 \cdot \tau(M)$

**Constraint on M:**
- $2000 \mid M$, which means $M = 2000k$ for some positive integer $k$
- Since $2000 = 2^4 \cdot 5^3$, we have $M = 2^a \cdot 5^b \cdot \text{(other primes)}$ where $a \geq 4$ and $b \geq 3$

## What is Being Asked

**Find:** The minimum possible value of $\tau(M)$ (the number of positive divisors of $M$) over all positive integers $M$ that satisfy both conditions:
1. $M$ is a multiple of $2000$
2. $M$ is imbalanced
