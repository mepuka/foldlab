# Solution: Finding |AB - CD|

## Step 1: Set up coordinates and constraints

Place points on a line:
- $A$ at position $0$
- $B$ at position $x$ where $AB = x$
- $C$ at position $x + 10$ (since $BC = 10$)
- $D$ at position $x + 10 + y$ where $CD = y$

Since $AD = 39$:
$$x + 10 + y = 39$$
$$\boxed{x + y = 29}$$

## Step 2: Position point E using the equilateral triangle

Triangle $BEC$ is equilateral with side length 10. Setting up 2D coordinates:
- $B$ at $(x, 0)$
- $C$ at $(x + 10, 0)$
- $E$ at $(x + 5, 5\sqrt{3})$ (at perpendicular distance $5\sqrt{3}$ from midpoint of $BC$)

Verification: 
- $BE^2 = (5)^2 + (5\sqrt{3})^2 = 25 + 75 = 100$, so $BE = 10$ ✓
- $EC^2 = (5)^2 + (5\sqrt{3})^2 = 100$, so $EC = 10$ ✓

## Step 3: Calculate distances AE and ED

With $A$ at $(0, 0)$:
$$AE^2 = (x+5)^2 + 75$$

With $D$ at $(x + 10 + y, 0)$:
$$ED^2 = (x + 10 + y - x - 5)^2 + 75 = (y + 5)^2 + 75$$

## Step 4: Apply Law of Cosines with $\angle AED = 120°$

$$AD^2 = AE^2 + ED^2 - 2(AE)(ED)\cos(120°)$$

Since $\cos(120°) = -\frac{1}{2}$:
$$1521 = AE^2 + ED^2 + AE \cdot ED$$

Expanding:
$$1521 = [(x+5)^2 + 75] + [(y+5)^2 + 75] + AE \cdot ED$$
$$1521 = (x+5)^2 + (y+5)^2 + 150 + AE \cdot ED$$

Expanding $(x+5)^2 + (y+5)^2$:
$$(x+5)^2 + (y+5)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + 10(x+y) + 50 = x^2 + y^2 + 10(29) + 50 = x^2 + y^2 + 340$$

Therefore:
$$AE \cdot ED = 1521 - 340 - 150 - (x^2 + y^2) = 1031 - (x^2 + y^2)$$

## Step 5: Solve using the constraint $(AE \cdot ED)^2$

$$[(x+5)^2 + 75][(y+5)^2 + 75] = [1031 - (x^2 + y^2)]^2$$

$$s = 841 - 2p$$

$$[1031 - s]^2 = (p + 170)^2 + 75s + 31125$$
