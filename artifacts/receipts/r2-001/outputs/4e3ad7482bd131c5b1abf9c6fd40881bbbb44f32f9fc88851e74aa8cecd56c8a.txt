# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- $S$ = the set of positive integer divisors of $10^9$
- We need to find subsets $T \subseteq S$

**Conditions on $T$:**

1. **Pairing condition**: For every element $s \in S$, exactly one of the following holds:
   - $s \in T$, or  
   - $\frac{10^9}{s} \in T$
   
   (In other words, for each complementary pair $(s, 10^9/s)$, we must choose exactly one element to include in $T$)

2. **Downward closure condition**: For every element $t \in T$, all positive integer divisors of $t$ must also be in $T$
   
   (If $d | t$ and $t \in T$, then $d \in T$)

## What is Being Asked

**Find:** The total number of subsets $T$ of $S$ satisfying both conditions above.
