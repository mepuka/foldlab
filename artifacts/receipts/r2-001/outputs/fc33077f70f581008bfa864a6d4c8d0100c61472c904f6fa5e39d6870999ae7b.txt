# Solution Plan

## Step 1: Understand Element 4's Final Position
- In the sorted array (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7), element 4 must be in position 4
- During bubble sort, element 4 only swaps with adjacent elements
- We need permutations where 4 reaches position 4 with at most 1 swap

## Step 2: Identify Valid Cases
**Case 1: Zero swaps**
- Element 4 is already in position 4 in the initial permutation
- Elements {1, 2, 3} must all be in positions {1, 2, 3}
- Elements {5, 6, 7} must all be in positions {5, 6, 7}

**Case 2: Exactly one swap**
- Element 4 starts in position 3 or position 5 (adjacent to final position)
- It swaps exactly once to reach position 4
- All other elements must already be in their correct relative positions relative to 4

## Step 3: Count Case 1 (Zero Swaps)
- Permutations of {1, 2, 3} in positions {1, 2, 3}: **3! = 6**
- Permutations of {5, 6, 7} in positions {5, 6, 7}: **3! = 6**
- Total for Case 1: **6 × 6 = 36**

## Step 4: Count Case 2 (One Swap - Position 3)
- Element 4 in position 3, swaps right to position 4
- Elements {1, 2, 3} in positions {1, 2, 3}: **3! = 6**
- Elements {5, 6, 7} in positions {5, 6, 7}: **3! = 6**
- Total for this subcase: **36**

## Step 5: Count Case 2 (One Swap - Position 5)
- Element 4 in position 5, swaps left to position 4
- Elements {1, 2, 3} in positions {1, 2, 3}: **3! = 6**
- Elements {5, 6, 7} in positions {5, 6, 7}: **3! = 6**
- Total for this subcase: **36**

## Step 6: Calculate Final Answer
- Total = Case 1 + Case 2 (Position 3) + Case 2 (Position 5)
- Total = 36 + 36 + 36 = **108**
