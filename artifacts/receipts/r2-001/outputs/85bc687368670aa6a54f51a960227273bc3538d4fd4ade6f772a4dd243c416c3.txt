# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Parabola equation**: $2y = x^2 - 8x + 12$
2. **Circle center**: $(4, 39)$
3. **Circle radius**: $r$ (unknown, to be determined)
4. **Tangency condition**: The circle must be tangent to the parabola at at least one point

## What is Being Asked

Find the sum of all real numbers $r > 0$ such that there exists at least one point on the parabola where the circle centered at $(4, 39)$ with radius $r$ is tangent to the parabola.

**In other words**: Determine all possible radii for which the circle and parabola are tangent (touching at exactly one point with the same tangent line), then sum these radii.
