# Solution: Path Counting on a Grid

## Step 1: Problem Setup
We model this as a directed acyclic graph where:
- Vertices are the 22 grid points arranged in two rows (y = 0 and y = 1)
- From point A(0,0) to point B(10,1)
- Allowed moves: rightward along horizontal segments and along diagonal segments

## Step 2: State Definition
Define:
- $f(i, 0)$ = number of ways to reach vertex $(i, 0)$
- $f(i, 1)$ = number of ways to reach vertex $(i, 1)$

## Step 3: Initial Conditions
At position i = 0:
- $f(0, 0) = 1$ (starting point A)
- $f(0, 1) = 1$ (can reach via the left vertical segment)

## Step 4: Recurrence Relations
For each square i where $0 \leq i \leq 9$:

**For the bottom row:**
$$f(i+1, 0) = f(i, 0)$$
(only way to reach $(i+1, 0)$ is from $(i, 0)$ via horizontal movement)

**For the top row:**
$$f(i+1, 1) = f(i, 0) + f(i, 1)$$
(can reach $(i+1, 1)$ from $(i, 0)$ via diagonal OR from $(i, 1)$ via horizontal)

## Step 5: Computation

| i | f(i, 0) | f(i, 1) |
|---|---------|---------|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 3 | 1 | 4 |
| 4 | 1 | 5 |
| 5 | 1 | 6 |
| 6 | 1 | 7 |
| 7 | 1 | 8 |
| 8 | 1 | 9 |
| 9 | 1 | 10 |
| 10 | 1 | 11 |

**Detailed calculations:**
- $f(1, 0) = f(0, 0) = 1$; $f(1, 1) = f(0, 0) + f(0, 1) = 1 + 1 = 2$
- $f(2, 0) = 1$; $f(2, 1) = 1 + 2 = 3$
- $f(3, 0) = 1$; $f(3, 1) = 1 + 3 = 4$
- $f(4, 0) = 1$; $f(4, 1) = 1 + 4 = 5$
- $f(5, 0) = 1$; $f(5, 1) = 1 + 5 = 6$
- $f(6, 0) = 1$; $f(6, 1) = 1 + 6 = 7$
- $f(7, 0) = 1$; $f(7, 1) = 1 + 7 = 8$
- $f(8, 0) = 1$; $f(8, 1) = 1 + 8 = 9$
- $f(9, 0) = 1$; $f(9, 1) = 1 + 9 = 10$
- $f(10, 0) = 1$; $f(10, 1) = 1 + 10 = 11$

## Step 6: Final Answer

The number of paths from A to B is:
$$N = f(10, 1) = 11$$

Therefore:
$$\sqrt{N} = \sqrt{11}$$

## Verification
The pattern shows $f(i, 1) = i + 1$, which makes sense: at each step from left to right, we can either stay on the bottom row (1 way) or move to the top row and then continue (adding the previous count).

**Final Answer: $\boxed{\sqrt{11}}$**
