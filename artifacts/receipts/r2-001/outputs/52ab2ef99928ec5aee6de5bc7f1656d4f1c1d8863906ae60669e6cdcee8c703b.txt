# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Establish the outermost loop constraint

Setting $a_1 = 10$ and $b_1 = 10$:

**Cells in loop 1:**
$$2a_1 + 2b_1 - 4 = 2(10) + 2(10) - 4 = 20 + 20 - 4 = 36 \text{ cells}$$

**Remaining cells for inner loops:**
$$100 - 36 = 64 \text{ cells} \quad \checkmark$$

## Step 2: Determine valid range constraints

For loop $i+1$ to fit inside loop $i$:
- Inner rectangle of loop $i$: $(a_i - 2) \times (b_i - 2)$
- Required: $a_{i+1} \le a_i - 2$ and $b_{i+1} \le b_i - 2$
- Minimum: $a_{i+1} \ge 2$ and $b_{i+1} \ge 2$

## Step 3: Derive the sum constraint

The total cells across all 5 loops must equal 100:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{5} (2a_i + 2b_i - 4) = 100$$

$$2\sum_{i=1}^{5} (a_i + b_i) - 20 = 100$$

$$\sum_{i=1}^{5} (a_i + b_i) = 60$$

Since $a_1 + b_1 = 20$:
$$\sum_{i=2}^{5} (a_i + b_i) = 40$$

Let $s_i = a_i + b_i$. We need: $s_2 + s_3 + s_4 + s_5 = 40$

## Step 4: Determine the chain of inequalities

For the nesting constraint to allow successive loops:
$$s_{i+1} \le s_i - 4 \quad \text{(both dimensions reduce by at least 2)}$$

**Working backward from the innermost loop:**

The innermost loop (loop 5) has minimum dimensions:
$$s_5 \ge 2 + 2 = 4$$

For loop 4 to contain loop 5:
$$s_5 \le s_4 - 4 \implies 4 \le s_4 - 4 \implies s_4 \ge 8$$

For loop 3 to contain loop 4:
$$s_4 \le s_3 - 4 \implies s_3 \ge s_4 + 4 \ge 12$$

For loop 2 to contain loop 3:
$$s_3 \le s_2 - 4 \implies s_2 \ge s_3 + 4 \ge 16$$

Since $s_2 \le 16$ (from $2 \le a_2, b_2 \le 8$):
$$s_2 = 16, \quad s_3 = 12, \quad s_4 = 8, \quad s_5 = 4$$

**Verification:** $16 + 12 + 8 + 4 = 40$ ✓
