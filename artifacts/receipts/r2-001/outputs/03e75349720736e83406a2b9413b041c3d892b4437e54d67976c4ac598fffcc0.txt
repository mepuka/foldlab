# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Operation Definition:** For integers $a$ and $b$:
   - $a \circ b = a - b$ if $a$ is odd AND $b$ is even
   - $a \circ b = a + b$ otherwise (all other cases)

2. **Sequence Requirements:**
   - Sequence: $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots, a_n$ of positive integers
   - Length $n$ is unrestricted (can be any positive integer)
   - Operations evaluated left to right: $((a_1 \circ a_2) \circ a_3) \circ \cdots \circ a_n$

3. **Two Constraints:**
   - $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \cdots + a_n = 12$ (sum equals 12)
   - $a_1 \circ a_2 \circ a_3 \circ \cdots \circ a_n = 0$ (sequential operation result equals 0)

## What is Being Asked

**Find the total number of sequences** of positive integers satisfying both the sum constraint and the operation constraint simultaneously.
