# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

1. **Five distinct variables**: $E, A, R, T, H$ each represent single digits (0-9)

2. **Addition constraint**: 
   - HATER (5-digit number) + HEART (5-digit number) = EARTH (5-digit number)
   - In expanded form: $(10000H + 1000A + 100T + 10E + R) + (10000H + 1000E + 100A + 10R + T) = (10000E + 1000A + 100R + 10T + H)$

3. **Leading digit constraints**:
   - $H \neq 0$ (H is a leading digit in both HATER and HEART)
   - $E \neq 0$ (E is a leading digit in EARTH)

4. **Digit distinctness**: The problem explicitly states that $E, A, R, T, H$ are **not necessarily distinct** — they may take equal values

5. **Uniqueness**: A unique 5-digit integer EARTH exists satisfying these conditions

## What is Being Asked:

**Find the unique 5-digit number $\overline{EARTH}$ (expressed as the concatenation of digits $E, A, R, T, H$) that satisfies the given addition problem.**
