# Solution: Partitioning 8×8 Square into 3 Rectangles

## Step 1: Identify Possible Partition Structures

**What I'm calculating:** All topologically distinct ways to divide an 8×8 square into exactly 3 rectangles using straight cuts.

**Complete enumeration:**
- **Case 1:** Three parallel horizontal strips (two horizontal cuts)
- **Case 2:** Three parallel vertical strips (two vertical cuts)  
- **Case 3:** L-shaped partitions (one cut creating 2 regions, one region subdivided perpendicularly)

---

## Step 2: Case 1 - Three Horizontal Strips

**What I'm calculating:** Number of ways to place two horizontal cuts.

**Complete calculation:**
- Make two horizontal cuts at integer heights $h_1$ and $h_2$ where $0 < h_1 < h_2 < 8$
- Valid cut positions: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7} (7 positions)
- Number of ways to choose 2 positions from 7: $\binom{7}{2} = \frac{7 \times 6}{2} = 21$

**Verification:** Each pair creates three rectangles of dimensions:
- $8 \times h_1$
- $8 \times (h_2 - h_1)$  
- $8 \times (8 - h_2)$

All valid partitions. ✓

**Case 1 Total: 21**

---

## Step 3: Case 2 - Three Vertical Strips

**What I'm calculating:** Number of ways to place two vertical cuts.

**Complete calculation:**
- Make two vertical cuts at integer positions $v_1$ and $v_2$ where $0 < v_1 < v_2 < 8$
- Valid cut positions: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7} (7 positions)
- Number of ways to choose 2 positions from 7: $\binom{7}{2} = 21$

**Verification:** Each pair creates three rectangles of dimensions:
- $v_1 \times 8$
- $(v_2 - v_1) \times 8$
- $(8 - v_2) \times 8$

All valid partitions. ✓

**Case 2 Total: 21**

---

## Step 4: Case 3 - L-Shaped Partitions

**What I'm calculating:** Number of configurations with one cut followed by a perpendicular cut in one region.

**Complete calculation:**

**Subcase 3a: Horizontal cut first (at height $h$), then vertical cut in one piece (at position $v$)**

*3a-i: Vertical cut subdivides bottom piece*
- Cut heights: $0 < h < 8$ → 7 choices
- Cut positions: $0 < v < 8$ → 7 choices  
- Creates rectangles: $v \times h$, $(8-v) \times h$, $8 \times (8-h)$
- Count: $7 \times 7 = 49$

*3a-ii: Vertical cut subdivides top piece*
- Cut heights: $0 < h < 8$ → 7 choices
- Cut positions: $0 < v < 8$ → 7 choices
- Creates rectangles: $v \times (8-h)$, $(8-v) \times (8-h)$, $8 \times h$
- Count: $7 \times 7 = 49$

**Subcase 3b: Vertical cut first (at position $v$), then horizontal cut in one piece (at height $h$)**

*3b-i: Horizontal cut subdivides left piece*
- Cut positions: $0 < v < 8$ → 7 choices
- Cut heights: $0 < h < 8$ → 7 choices
- Creates rectangles: $v \times h$, $v \times (8-h)$, $(8-v) \times 8$
- Count: $7 \times 7 = 49$

*3b-ii: Horizontal cut subdivides right piece*
- Cut positions: $0 < v < 8$ → 7 choices
- Cut heights: $0 < h < 8$ → 7 choices
- Creates rectangles: $v \times 8$, $(8-v) \times h$, $(8-v) \times (8-h)$
- Count: $7 \times 7 = 49$

**Verification of no overlaps in Case 3:**
- 3a-i produces L-shapes with the vertical cut in the bottom portion
- 3a-ii produces L-shapes with the vertical cut in the top portion
- 3b-i produces L-shapes with the horizontal cut in the left portion
- 3b-ii produces L-shapes with the horizontal cut in the right portion

These geometric configurations are mutually exclusive. ✓

**Case 3 Total: $49 + 49 + 49 + 49 = 196$**

---

## Step 5: Verify No Overlaps Between Cases

**Case 1 vs Case 2:** 
- Case 1: All rectangles span full width (all 8 units wide)
- Case 2: All rectangles span full height (all 8 units tall)
- Mutually exclusive ✓

**Cases 1 & 2 vs Case 3:**
- Cases 1 & 2: Parallel strips (all cutting lines span full dimension)
- Case 3: L-shaped (one piece subdivided, creating rectangles of different widths OR heights)
- Mutually exclusive ✓

---

## Step 6: Sum All Valid Configurations

$$\text{Total} = \text{Case 1} + \text{Case 2} + \text{Case 3}$$

$$\text{Total} = 21 + 21 + 196 = 238$$

---

## Step 7: Verify Answer

**Sanity checks:**
- Each configuration creates exactly 3 rectangles ✓
- All rectangles have integer dimensions ✓
- Rectangles perfectly tile the 8×8 square with no gaps or overlaps ✓
- Total (238) falls within reasonable range for this combinatorial problem ✓

**FINAL ANSWER: 238**
