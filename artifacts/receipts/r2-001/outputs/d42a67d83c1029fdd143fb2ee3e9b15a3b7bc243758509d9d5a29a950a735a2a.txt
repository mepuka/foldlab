# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Set Up Venn Diagram Framework
I'll create a three-circle Venn diagram with:
- Circle B = Blue moons
- Circle I = Icy moons
- Circle L = Large moons

## Step 2: Identify the "Exclusive" Regions
From the given data, moons with exactly ONE characteristic:
- **Blue only** (B ∩ I' ∩ L'): 10 moons
- **Icy only** (I ∩ B' ∩ L'): 8 moons
- **Large only** (L ∩ B' ∩ I'): 6 moons

## Step 3: Add the Exclusive Regions
$$10 + 8 + 6 = 24 \text{ moons}$$

These 24 moons have exactly one characteristic each.

## Step 4: Calculate the Remainder
$$\text{Total moons} - \text{Exclusive moons} = 29 - 24 = 5 \text{ moons}$$

## Step 5: Determine What the Remainder Represents
The 5 remaining moons must be distributed among the overlapping regions:
- Blue AND Icy (but not Large)
- Blue AND Large (but not Icy)
- Icy AND Large (but not Blue)
- Blue AND Icy AND Large (all three)

Since no additional information distinguishes between these overlapping combinations, and the problem asks specifically about moons with all three characteristics, these 5 moons represent the intersection of all three sets.

## Final Answer
**5 moons are blue, icy, AND large.**
