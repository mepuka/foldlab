# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Set up time equations
- Patrick's walk time: $t$ hours
- Tanya's walk time: $t - 1$ hours (starts 1 hour later)
- Jose's walk time: $t - 2$ hours (starts 2 hours later)

## Step 2: Write distance equations
All three travel the same distance $D$:
- Patrick: $D = p \cdot t$ (where $p$ is Patrick's speed in mph)
- Tanya: $D = (p + 2)(t - 1)$
- Jose: $D = (p + 9)(t - 2)$

## Step 3: Set up equality equations

**Equation 1** (Patrick = Tanya):
$$pt = (p + 2)(t - 1)$$

**Equation 2** (Patrick = Jose):
$$pt = (p + 9)(t - 2)$$

## Step 4: Solve Equation 1 for $p$

$$pt = (p + 2)(t - 1)$$

Expand the right side:
$$pt = pt - p + 2t - 2$$

Subtract $pt$ from both sides:
$$0 = -p + 2t - 2$$

Solve for $p$:
$$p = 2t - 2$$

## Step 5: Substitute into Equation 2

Substitute $p = 2t - 2$ into $pt = (p + 9)(t - 2)$:

$$(2t - 2)t = (2t - 2 + 9)(t - 2)$$

$$(2t - 2)t = (2t + 7)(t - 2)$$

**Expand left side:**
$$2t^2 - 2t$$

**Expand right side:**
$$(2t + 7)(t - 2) = 2t^2 - 4t + 7t - 14 = 2t^2 + 3t - 14$$

**Set equal:**
$$2t^2 - 2t = 2t^2 + 3t - 14$$

Subtract $2t^2$ from both sides:
$$-2t = 3t - 14$$

$$-5t = -14$$

$$t = \frac{14}{5}$$

## Step 6: Find Patrick's speed $p$

$$p = 2t - 2 = 2 \cdot \frac{14}{5} - 2 = \frac{28}{5} - \frac{10}{5} = \frac{18}{5}$$

## Step 7: Calculate distance $D$

$$D = p \cdot t = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$

## Step 8: Verify by substitution

**Check with Tanya's equation:**
$$D = (p + 2)(t - 1) = \left(\frac{18}{5} + \frac{10}{5}\right)\left(\frac{14}{5} - \frac{5}{5}\right) = \frac{28}{5} \cdot \frac{9}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$ ✓

**Check with Jose's equation:**
$$D = (p + 9)(t - 2) = \left(\frac{18}{5} + \frac{45}{5}\right)\left(\frac{14}{5} - \frac{10}{5}\right) = \frac{63}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$ ✓

## Step 9: Reduce to lowest terms

$$D = \frac{252}{25}$$

Check $\gcd(252, 25)$:
- $252 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 7$
- $25 = 5^2$

Since these share no common factors, $\gcd(252, 25) = 1$.

Therefore: $m = 252$ and $n = 25$

## Step 10: Calculate final answer

$$m + n = 252 + 25 = 277$$

FINAL ANSWER: [277]
