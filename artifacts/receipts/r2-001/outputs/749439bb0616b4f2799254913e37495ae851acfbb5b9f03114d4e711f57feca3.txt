# Solution: Counting Divisors with Specific Congruences

## 1. What We're Calculating

We need to find the number of divisors $d$ of $17017^{17}$ such that:
- $d \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, AND
- $d \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$

Then compute this count modulo 1000.

## 2. Express Divisors in Standard Form

First, factor 17017:
- $17017 = 7 \times 11 \times 13 \times 17$ ✓

Therefore: $17017^{17} = 7^{17} \times 11^{17} \times 13^{17} \times 17^{17}$

Any divisor has the form: $d = 7^a \times 11^b \times 13^c \times 17^e$ where $0 \leq a, b, c, e \leq 17$

## 3. Analyze Modulo 3

Find residues:
- $7 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$
- $11 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$
- $13 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$
- $17 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$

Therefore:
$$d \equiv 1^a \times 2^b \times 1^c \times 2^e \equiv 2^{b+e} \pmod{3}$$

For $d \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$:
- Since $2^1 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ and $2^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$
- We need **$b + e$ to be odd**

## 4. Analyze Modulo 4

Find residues:
- $7 \equiv 3 \equiv -1 \pmod{4}$
- $11 \equiv 3 \equiv -1 \pmod{4}$
- $13 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$
- $17 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$

Therefore:
$$d \equiv (-1)^a \times (-1)^b \times 1^c \times 1^e \equiv (-1)^{a+b} \pmod{4}$$

For $d \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$:
- Since $(-1)^{\text{even}} = 1$ and $(-1)^{\text{odd}} = -1$
- We need **$a + b$ to be even**

## 5. Combine Constraints

We need:
- $b + e$ is odd (opposite parity)
- $a + b$ is even (same parity)

**Case 1: $b$ is even**
- From $a + b$ even: $a$ must be **even**
- From $b + e$ odd: $e$ must be **odd**
- $c$ has **no constraint**

**Case 2: $b$ is odd**
- From $a + b$ even: $a$ must be **odd**
- From $b + e$ odd: $e$ must be **even**
- $c$ has **no constraint**

## 6. Count Valid Exponent Choices

In $\{0, 1, 2, ..., 17\}$:
- Even values: $\{0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16\}$ → **9 choices**
- Odd values: $\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17\}$ → **9 choices**
- All values: → **18 choices**

## 7. Calculate Total Count

**Case 1** ($a$ even, $b$ even, $e$ odd, $c$ unrestricted):
$$9 \times 9 \times 18 \times 9 = 81 \times 162 = 13,122$$

**Case 2** ($a$ odd, $b$ odd, $e$ even, $c$ unrestricted):
$$9 \times 9 \times 18 \times 9 = 81 \times 162 = 13,122$$

**Total:**
$$N = 13,122 + 13,122 = 26,244$$

## 8. Find the Remainder

$$26,244 = 26 \times 1000 + 244$$

Therefore: $N \equiv 244 \pmod{1000}$

## Verification

- Both cases are mutually exclusive and exhaustive ✓
- Each case has valid choices for all four exponents ✓
- The structure respects the independence of mod 3 and mod 4 conditions (by CRT) ✓
- The calculation is symmetric, which makes sense given the symmetric roles of cases ✓

**FINAL ANSWER: 244**
