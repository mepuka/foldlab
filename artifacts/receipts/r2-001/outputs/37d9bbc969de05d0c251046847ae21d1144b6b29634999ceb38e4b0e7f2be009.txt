# Solution: Expected Number of Cells Visited on a 6×6 Grid

## Step 1: Establish the Baseline
**Calculating total shortest paths from (0,0) to (5,5):**

Total paths = $\binom{10}{5} = \frac{10!}{5!5!} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6}{120} = 252$

✓ **Verified:** We need exactly 5 moves right and 5 moves up = 10 total moves.

---

## Step 2: Coordinate System Established
- Bottom-left corner: $(0,0)$
- Top-right corner: $(5,5)$
- Grid has 36 total cells

---

## Step 3: Calculate Paths Through Each Cell

For cell $(x,y)$: $P(x,y) = \binom{x+y}{x} \times \binom{10-x-y}{5-x}$

Computing systematically by row:

**Row y=0:** 
- (0,0): $\binom{0}{0}\binom{10}{5} = 1 \times 252 = 252$
- (1,0): $\binom{1}{1}\binom{9}{4} = 1 \times 126 = 126$

- (4,0): $\binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{1} = 1 \times 6 = 6$
- (5,0): $\binom{5}{5}\binom{5}{0} = 1 \times 1 = 1$

**Row y=2:**

**Row y=4:**

## Step 7: Sum All Paths Through All Cells

By row sums:
- y=0: $252 + 126 + 56 + 21 + 6 + 1 = 462$
- y=1: $126 + 140 + 105 + 60 + 25 + 6 = 462$
- y=2: $56 + 105 + 120 + 100 + 60 + 21 = 462$
- y=3: $21 + 60 + 100 + 120 + 105 + 56 = 462$
- y=4: $6 + 25 + 60 + 105 + 140 + 126 = 462$
- y=5: $1 + 6 + 21 + 56 + 126 + 252 = 462$

**Total sum:** $6 \times 462 = 2772$

✓ **Verified:** The symmetry is correct (grid is symmetric about the diagonal).

---

## Step 8: Calculate Final Answer

$$E = 252 - \frac{1}{36} \times 2772$$

$$\frac{2772}{36} = 77$$

$$E = 252 - 77 = 175$$

✓ **Verification:** $36 \times 77 = 2,772$ ✓ and $252 - 77 = 175$ ✓

---

**FINAL ANSWER: 175**
