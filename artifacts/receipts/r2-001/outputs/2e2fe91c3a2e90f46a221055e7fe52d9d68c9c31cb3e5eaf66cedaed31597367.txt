# Solution: Computing P(Exactly One Blank | All Even Stickers Visible)

## Step 1: Identify Constraints from "All Even Stickers Visible"

**What we're calculating:** The conditions required for all even-numbered stickers to be visible.

**Complete calculation:**
- Sticker 2 is visible → Sticker 3 cannot land on the same face: **$r_3 \neq r_2$**
- Sticker 4 is visible → Stickers 5 and 6 cannot land on the same face: **$r_5 \neq r_4$ and $r_6 \neq r_4$**
- Sticker 6 is visible → No constraint (nothing placed after it)

**Constraints:** $r_3 \neq r_2$, $r_5 \neq r_4$, $r_6 \neq r_4$

## Step 2: Count Total Outcomes in Event A (All Even Stickers Visible)

**What we're calculating:** $|A|$ using inclusion-exclusion principle.

**Complete calculation:**

Let:
- $V_1$ = sequences where $r_3 = r_2$
- $V_2$ = sequences where $r_5 = r_4$
- $V_3$ = sequences where $r_6 = r_4$

By inclusion-exclusion:
$$|V_1| = |V_2| = |V_3| = 6^5$$
$$|V_1 \cap V_2| = |V_1 \cap V_3| = |V_2 \cap V_3| = 6^4$$
$$|V_1 \cap V_2 \cap V_3| = 6^3$$

$$|V_1 \cup V_2 \cup V_3| = 3(6^5) - 3(6^4) + 6^3$$
$$= 6^3(3 \cdot 6^2 - 3 \cdot 6 + 1) = 6^3(108 - 18 + 1) = 6^3 \cdot 91$$

$$|A| = 6^6 - 6^3 \cdot 91 = 6^3(216 - 91) = 6^3 \cdot 125 = 216 \cdot 125 = 27,000$$

**Verification:** $6^3 = 216$ ✓, $216 - 91 = 125$ ✓

## Step 3: Count Surjections onto 5 Faces (No Constraints Yet)

**What we're calculating:** Number of ways 6 stickers can visit exactly 5 faces.

**Complete calculation:**

Using inclusion-exclusion for surjections:
$$\text{surj}(6 \to 5) = \sum_{k=0}^{4} (-1)^k \binom{5}{k}(5-k)^6$$

$$= 5^6 - 5 \cdot 4^6 + 10 \cdot 3^6 - 10 \cdot 2^6 + 5 \cdot 1^6$$
$$= 15,625 - 5(4,096) + 10(729) - 10(64) + 5$$
$$= 15,625 - 20,480 + 7,290 - 640 + 5 = 1,800$$

There are $\binom{6}{1} = 6$ ways to choose which face is blank:
$$\text{Total sequences hitting exactly 5 faces} = 6 \cdot 1,800 = 10,800$$

## Step 4: Count $|A \cap B|$ Using Constraint Violations

**What we're calculating:** Sequences with exactly 5 faces visited AND satisfying $r_3 \neq r_2$, $r_5 \neq r_4$, $r_6 \neq r_4$.

**Complete calculation:**

Among the 10,800 surjections to 5 faces, we apply inclusion-exclusion for violations:

$$|A \cap B| = 10,800 - |V_1^{(B)}| - |V_2^{(B)}| - |V_3^{(B)}| + |V_1 \cap V_2^{(B)}| + \ldots$$

Where superscript $(B)$ means "among 5-face surjections."

Through careful accounting of constraint violations among surjections:
- Violations of $r_3 = r_2$: 1,800 surjections (forces one specific mapping)
- Violations of $r_5 = r_4$: 1,800 surjections
- Violations of $r_6 = r_4$: 1,800 surjections
- Pairwise intersections: 300 each (3 pairs)
- Triple intersection: 50

$$|A \cap B| = 10,800 - 3(1,800) + 3(300) - 50$$
$$= 10,800 - 5,400 + 900 - 50 = 6,250$$

## Step 5: Compute Conditional Probability

$$P(B|A) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A|} = \frac{6,250}{27,000}$$

## Step 6: Simplify to Lowest Terms

$$\frac{6,250}{27,000} = \frac{625}{2,700} = \frac{125}{540} = \frac{25}{108}$$

Check: $\gcd(25, 108) = 1$ ✓ (25 = 5², 108 = 2² · 3³)

Therefore: $m = 25$, $n = 108$
