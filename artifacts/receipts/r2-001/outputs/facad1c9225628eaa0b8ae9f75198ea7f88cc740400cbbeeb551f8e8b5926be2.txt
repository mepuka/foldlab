# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- We are considering positive integers $n$
- $n$ has at least 6 positive integer divisors
- The 6th largest divisor of $n$ is 6

**What is being asked:**
- Compute the sum of all positive integers $n$ satisfying the above conditions

---

# Key Observations to Clarify the Problem

To interpret "6th largest divisor," note that:
- If $n$ has divisors $d_1 > d_2 > d_3 > \cdots$, then the 6th largest divisor is the 6th element in this descending sequence
- Since $n$ must have at least 6 divisors, this 6th largest divisor is well-defined
- The largest divisor is always $n$ itself
