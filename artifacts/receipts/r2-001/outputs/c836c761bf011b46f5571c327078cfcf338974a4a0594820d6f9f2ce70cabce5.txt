# Solution Plan

## 1. **Identify the Constraint from "All Even Stickers Visible"**
   - Sticker 2 is visible → sticker 3 was NOT rolled onto the face where sticker 2 is
   - Sticker 4 is visible → stickers 5 and 6 were NOT rolled onto the face where sticker 4 is
   - Sticker 6 is visible → no constraint (it's placed last)
   - Therefore: sticker 3 ≠ face(2), sticker 5 ≠ face(4), sticker 6 ≠ face(4)

## 2. **Count Total Outcomes in the Conditional Event**
   - Total sequences: $6^6$
   - Event A (all even stickers visible): outcomes where $r_3 \neq r_2$, $r_5 \neq r_4$, and $r_6 \neq r_4$
   - Using inclusion-exclusion: $|A| = 6^6 - (\text{violations})$
   - Calculate: $|A| = 6^6 - 3 \cdot 6^5 + 2 \cdot 6^4 = 6^4(36 - 18 + 2) = 6^4 \cdot 20$

## 3. **Count Outcomes Where Exactly One Face is Blank AND All Even Stickers Visible**
   - Event B (exactly one blank face): exactly 5 of 6 faces have stickers
   - Event A ∩ B requires both conditions
   - The blank face cannot be any of {face(2), face(4), face(6)} (these have stickers)
   - So the blank face must be one of the at most 3 remaining faces
   - For each choice of blank face $f$: count valid roll sequences where $r_3 \neq r_2$, $r_5 \neq r_4$, $r_6 \neq r_4$, and exactly the faces in $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\} \setminus \{f\}$ are hit

## 4. **Apply Inclusion-Exclusion for the Intersection**
   - For each blank face $f$, use inclusion-exclusion to count surjections onto the 5 faces {1,...,6}\{f} that satisfy the sticker constraints
   - Sum over all possible blank faces (3 choices: the ones not containing even stickers initially)
   - Calculate $|A \cap B|$ using constraint violations systematically

## 5. **Compute Conditional Probability**
   $$p = P(B|A) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A|} = \frac{\text{count from step 4}}{6^4 \cdot 20}$$

## 6. **Simplify to Lowest Terms**
   - Reduce the fraction $p = \frac{m}{n}$ until $\gcd(m,n) = 1$
   - Return $m + n$
