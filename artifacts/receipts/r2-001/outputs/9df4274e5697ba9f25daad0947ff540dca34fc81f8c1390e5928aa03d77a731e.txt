# Solution

## Problem Setup
I need to find the distance from school to park when three people walking at different speeds from different start times all arrive at the same destination at the same time.

## Step 1: Set Up Time Equations
Let $t$ = time (in hours) Patrick walks until arrival
- Tanya's travel time: $t - 1$ hours
- Jose's travel time: $t - 2$ hours

Let $p$ = Patrick's walking speed (mph)
- Tanya's speed: $p + 2$ mph
- Jose's speed: $p + 9$ mph

## Step 2: Write Distance Equations
Since all three travel the same distance $D$:
- Patrick: $D = pt$
- Tanya: $D = (p+2)(t-1)$
- Jose: $D = (p+9)(t-2)$

## Step 3: Equate Patrick's and Tanya's Distances

$$pt = (p+2)(t-1)$$
$$pt = pt - p + 2t - 2$$
$$0 = -p + 2t - 2$$
$$p = 2t - 2 \quad \text{...(Equation 1)}$$

## Step 4: Equate Patrick's and Jose's Distances

$$pt = (p+9)(t-2)$$
$$pt = pt - 2p + 9t - 18$$
$$0 = -2p + 9t - 18$$
$$p = \frac{9t - 18}{2} \quad \text{...(Equation 2)}$$

## Step 5: Solve for $t$

Setting Equation 1 equal to Equation 2:
$$2t - 2 = \frac{9t - 18}{2}$$

Multiply both sides by 2:
$$4t - 4 = 9t - 18$$
$$14 = 5t$$
$$t = \frac{14}{5} \text{ hours}$$

## Step 6: Solve for $p$

Using Equation 1:
$$p = 2\left(\frac{14}{5}\right) - 2 = \frac{28}{5} - \frac{10}{5} = \frac{18}{5} \text{ mph}$$

## Step 7: Calculate Distance

$$D = pt = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$$

## Step 8: Verify Solution

**Patrick:** $D = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

**Tanya:** 
- Speed: $\frac{18}{5} + 2 = \frac{28}{5}$
- Time: $\frac{14}{5} - 1 = \frac{9}{5}$
- Distance: $\frac{28}{5} \cdot \frac{9}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

**Jose:**
- Speed: $\frac{18}{5} + 9 = \frac{63}{5}$
- Time: $\frac{14}{5} - 2 = \frac{4}{5}$
- Distance: $\frac{63}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

## Step 9: Check Lowest Terms

$$D = \frac{252}{25}$$
- $252 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$
- $25 = 5^2$
- $\gcd(252, 25) = 1$ ✓

Therefore: $m = 252$ and $n = 25$

## Final Answer

$$m + n = 252 + 25 = \boxed{277}$$
