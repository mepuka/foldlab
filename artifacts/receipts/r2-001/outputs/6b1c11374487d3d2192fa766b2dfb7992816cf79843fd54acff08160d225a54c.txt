# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- The set of numbers: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}
- These numbers are written on a blackboard in some initial order (a permutation)
- The sorting process uses bubble sort: repeatedly swapping adjacent numbers
- The process continues until the numbers are in ascending order (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7)

**Key Constraint:**
- We restrict to initial orderings where the number 4 participates in **at most 1 swap** during the entire sorting process

## What Is Being Asked

**Find:** The count of initial permutations of {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7} such that when bubble sort is applied, the element 4 is involved in at most one adjacent swap before reaching the final sorted configuration.

---

## Clarification on "Swap"

The problem asks for orderings where element 4 (not position 4) participates in at most one adjacent transposition. This means:
- 0 swaps: the element 4 is already in its final position and never moves
- 1 swap: the element 4 is moved exactly once (one adjacent transposition involving it)
