# Complete Solution

## Step 1: Identify the Constraint from "All Even Stickers Visible"

For all even stickers (2, 4, 6) to be visible:
- Sticker 2 is visible → sticker 3 doesn't land on same face: **$r_3 \neq r_2$**
- Sticker 4 is visible → stickers 5 and 6 don't land on same face: **$r_5 \neq r_4$ AND $r_6 \neq r_4$**
- Sticker 6 is visible → no constraint (placed last)

**Constraints for event A:** $r_3 \neq r_2$, $r_5 \neq r_4$, $r_6 \neq r_4$

## Step 2: Count Outcomes in Event A Using Inclusion-Exclusion

Let:
- $B_1$ = event that $r_3 = r_2$
- $B_2$ = event that $r_5 = r_4$  
- $B_3$ = event that $r_6 = r_4$

Event A is the complement of $B_1 \cup B_2 \cup B_3$.

**Single violations:**
$$|B_1| = |B_2| = |B_3| = 6^5$$

**Pairwise intersections:**
$$|B_1 \cap B_2| = |B_1 \cap B_3| = |B_2 \cap B_3| = 6^4$$

**Triple intersection:**
$$|B_1 \cap B_2 \cap B_3| = 6^3$$
(When $r_5 = r_4 = r_6$, we have $6^3$ outcomes)

**By inclusion-exclusion:**
$$|A| = 6^6 - 3 \cdot 6^5 + 3 \cdot 6^4 - 6^3$$
$$= 6^3(6^3 - 3 \cdot 6^2 + 3 \cdot 6 - 1)$$
$$= 216(216 - 108 + 18 - 1)$$
$$= 216 \cdot 125 = 27000$$

**Verification:** $46656 - 23328 + 3888 - 216 = 27000$ ✓

## Step 3: Count Outcomes in Event B (Exactly One Blank Face)

Event B requires exactly 5 of 6 faces to be occupied (hit by at least one sticker).

The number of surjections from a 6-element set to a specific 5-element set:
$$\text{Surjections} = 5! \cdot S(6,5) = 120 \cdot 6 = 720$$

where $S(6,5) = 6$ (Stirling number of second kind).

Number of ways to choose which 5 faces are occupied:
$$\binom{6}{5} = 6$$

Without constraints: $|B| = 6 \cdot 720 = 4320$

## Step 4: Count $|A \cap B|$ - Surjections with Constraints

For each choice of blank face $f$, count surjections onto the 5-face set $S = \{1,...,6\} \setminus \{f\}$ satisfying constraints.

Using inclusion-exclusion on each 5-face set (surjections satisfying all three constraints):

For surjections onto $S$ with $|S| = 5$:
- Without constraints: 720 surjections
- With violations counted via inclusion-exclusion:

For each blank face $f$, denote the surjections satisfying all constraints as $\phi_f$.

The key insight: Within the surjections onto 5 faces, we apply the same constraint counting.

After applying inclusion-exclusion (detailed calculation):
- Surjections with $r_3 = r_2$: $4! \cdot S(5,4) = 24 \cdot 10 = 240$ per blank face
- Surjections with $r_5 = r_4$: $240$
- Surjections with $r_6 = r_4$: $240$
- Pairwise: $3! \cdot S(4,3) = 6 \cdot 6 = 36$ each (3 pairs)
- Triple: $2! \cdot S(3,2) = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$

Per blank face:
$$\phi_f = 720 - 3(240) + 3(36) - 6 = 720 - 720 + 108 - 6 = 102$$

Since any of the 6 faces can be blank:
$$|A \cap B| = 6 \cdot 102 = 612$$

## Step 5: Compute Conditional Probability

$$P(B|A) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A|} = \frac{612}{27000}$$

## Step 6: Simplify to Lowest Terms

Find $\gcd(612, 27000)$:
- $612 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 17$
- $27000 = 2^3 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^3 \cdot 1 = 8 \cdot 3375$

$\gcd(612, 27000) = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 36$

$$\frac{612}{27000} = \frac{612 \div 36}{27000 \div 36} = \frac{17}{750}$$

**Verification:** $\gcd(17, 750) = 1$ ✓ (17 is prime, doesn't divide 750)

Therefore: $m = 17$, $n = 750$

$$m + n = 17 + 750 = 767$$

FINAL ANSWER: **767**
