# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities
- Initial set of numbers on the board: {1, 2, 3, ..., 2100}
- Total count: 2100 numbers

## Given Conditions
1. Each operation involves selecting two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the board
2. These two numbers are erased and replaced with:
   - $\gcd(a, b)$ (their greatest common divisor)
   - $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b)$ (their least common multiple)
3. The board always contains exactly 2100 numbers throughout the process
4. Mark stops when he reaches a fixed point: any move will not change the multiset of numbers on the board
5. The process must terminate (the stopping condition is achievable)

## What is Being Asked

Find the number of divisors of the 2026th smallest positive integer written on the board when the process terminates (i.e., when the board reaches its final stable state).

### Clarification of "2026th smallest"
Since we have 2100 numbers on the board, and some numbers may repeat, "2026th smallest" refers to the 2026th element when all 2100 numbers are listed in non-decreasing order (counting multiplicities).
