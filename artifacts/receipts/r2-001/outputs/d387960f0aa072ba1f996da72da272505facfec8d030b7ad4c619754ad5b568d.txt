# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **First term**: $a_1 = 4$
2. **Sequence type**: Arithmetic sequences of integers
3. **Included terms**: The sequence must contain both $24$ and $34$ as terms
4. **Target position**: We're interested in the 10th term

## Implicit Constraints

- Since the sequence is an arithmetic sequence of integers, the common difference $d$ must be an integer
- Both $24$ and $34$ must appear at positive integer positions in their respective sequences

## What Is Being Asked

Find the sum of all 10th terms across every arithmetic sequence satisfying these conditions. That is:
$$\sum_{d \in D} a_{10}$$

where $D$ is the set of all integer values of $d$ (common difference) such that:
- Both $24$ and $34$ appear as terms in the sequence with first term $4$ and common difference $d$

In other words, find all valid common differences, compute $a_{10} = 4 + 9d$ for each, and add them together.
