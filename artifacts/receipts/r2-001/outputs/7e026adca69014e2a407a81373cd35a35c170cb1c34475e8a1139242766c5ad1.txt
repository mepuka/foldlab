# Solution

## Step 1: Rearrange the Recurrence Relation

Given: $a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$

Since 397 is prime, modular inverses exist. We can rearrange to:
$$a_{n+1} \equiv a_n(a_n + 1)^{-2} \pmod{397}$$

## Step 2: Find a Telescoping Property

Let me explore the function $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ applied to the recurrence.

From $a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 = a_n$, dividing both sides by $a_{n+1} \cdot a_n$:

$$\frac{(a_n + 1)^2}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}}$$

Expanding the left side:
$$\frac{a_n^2 + 2a_n + 1}{a_n} = a_n + 2 + \frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}}$$

**Rearranging:**
$$\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n} = a_n + 2$$

## Step 3: Create a Telescoping Sum

Summing from $n = 0$ to $n = 2025$:
$$\sum_{n=0}^{2025}\left(\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n}\right) = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}(a_n + 2)$$

The left side telescopes:
$$\frac{1}{a_{2026}} - \frac{1}{a_0} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + 2(2026)$$

With $a_0 = 1$ and $a_{2026} = 9$:
$$\frac{1}{9} - \frac{1}{1} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + 4052$$

$$\frac{1}{9} - 1 = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + 4052$$

$$\frac{1-9}{9} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + 4052$$

$$-\frac{8}{9} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + 4052$$

## Step 4: Solve for the Sum (excluding $a_{2026}$)

$$\sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n = -\frac{8}{9} - 4052$$

## Step 5: Compute Modulo 397

First, find $9^{-1} \pmod{397}$:
- $397 = 44 \cdot 9 + 1$
- Therefore: $1 = 397 - 44 \cdot 9$
- So $9^{-1} \equiv -44 \equiv 353 \pmod{397}$

**Verification:** $9 \cdot 353 = 3177 = 8 \cdot 397 + 1$ ✓

Now: $\frac{1}{9} \equiv 353 \pmod{397}$

$$-\frac{8}{9} \equiv -8 \cdot 353 \equiv -2824 \pmod{397}$$

Calculate $2824 \pmod{397}$:
- $2824 = 7 \cdot 397 + 45 = 2779 + 45$
- So $-2824 \equiv -45 \equiv 352 \pmod{397}$

Calculate $4052 \pmod{397}$:
- $4052 = 10 \cdot 397 + 82 = 3970 + 82$
- So $4052 \equiv 82 \pmod{397}$

Therefore:
$$\sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n \equiv 352 - 82 \equiv 270 \pmod{397}$$

## Step 6: Include $a_{2026}$

$$\sum_{n=0}^{2026}a_n = \sum_{n=0}^{2025}a_n + a_{2026} \equiv 270 + 9 \equiv 279 \pmod{397}$$

## Verification

- Telescoping identity: $\frac{1}{9} - 1 = -\frac{8}{9}$ ✓
- $-\frac{8}{9} \equiv 352 \pmod{397}$ ✓
- $352 - 82 = 270$ ✓
- $270 + 9 = 279$ ✓

FINAL ANSWER: **279**
