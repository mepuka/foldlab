## Step 1: Simplify the Probability Equation

Starting with: $\binom{r}{4}\binom{b}{3} = \binom{r}{5}\binom{b}{2}$

Expand each binomial coefficient:
$$\frac{r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3)}{24} \cdot \frac{b(b-1)(b-2)}{6} = \frac{r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3)(r-4)}{120} \cdot \frac{b(b-1)}{2}$$

Simplify the left side: $\frac{r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3) \cdot b(b-1)(b-2)}{144}$

Simplify the right side: $\frac{r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3)(r-4) \cdot b(b-1)}{240}$

## Step 2: Derive the Constraint Equation

Divide both sides by $r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3) \cdot b(b-1)$ (valid for $r \geq 5, b \geq 3$):
$$\frac{b-2}{144} = \frac{r-4}{240}$$

Cross-multiply:
$$240(b-2) = 144(r-4)$$

$$240b - 480 = 144r - 576$$

$$240b = 144r - 96$$

Divide by 48:
$$5b = 3r - 2$$

**Constraint:** $b = \frac{3r - 2}{5}$

## Step 3: Apply Validity Constraints

For $b$ to be a positive integer: $3r - 2 \equiv 0 \pmod{5}$

$$3r \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$$

Testing: $3 \times 4 = 12 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$ ✓

Therefore: $r \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$, so $r \in \{4, 9, 14, 19, 24, 29, ...\}$

We also require:
