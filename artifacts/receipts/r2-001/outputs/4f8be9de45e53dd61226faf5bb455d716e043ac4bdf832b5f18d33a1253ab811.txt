# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Number system**: Base 10 (decimal)
2. **Type of numbers**: Palindromes (read the same forwards and backwards)
3. **Digit restriction**: No zero digits (all digits are from {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9})
4. **Digit sum condition**: The digits must sum to exactly 13
5. **Range**: Positive integers
6. **Example**: 42124 is cited as satisfying all properties

## What is Being Asked

Find the **count** of all positive integer palindromes (in base 10) that satisfy both:
- Contain no zero digits, AND
- Have digits that sum to 13

In other words, determine how many such palindromes exist.
