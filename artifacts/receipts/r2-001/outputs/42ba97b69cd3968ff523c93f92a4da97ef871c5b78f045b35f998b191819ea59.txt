# Solution Plan

1. **Set up a coordinate system**
   - Place $B$ at the origin $(0, 0)$
   - Place $A$ at $(1, 0)$

2. **Find the position of $A'$ after the first rotation**
   - Rotate $A$ counterclockwise by angle $\theta$ around $B$
   - Calculate: $A' = (\cos \theta, \sin \theta)$

3. **Find the position of $B'$ after the second rotation**
   - Determine the vector $\vec{A'B} = B - A' = (-\cos \theta, -\sin \theta)$
   - Rotate this vector clockwise by angle $\theta$ around $A'$ (clockwise rotation matrix with angle $-\theta$)
   - Add the rotated vector to $A'$ to get $B'$

4. **Apply the distance condition**
   - Calculate $|AB'|$ in terms of $\cos \theta$ and $\sin \theta$
   - Set $|AB'| = \frac{4}{3}$ and square both sides to eliminate the square root

5. **Solve for $\cos \theta$**
   - Use the constraint $\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1$ to convert to a single-variable equation
   - Simplify to get a quadratic equation in $\cos \theta$
   - Solve the quadratic using the quadratic formula

6. **Select the valid solution**
   - Check that $0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ (which means $0 < \cos \theta < 1$)
   - Identify which root satisfies the acute angle constraint

7. **Express in lowest terms and compute the answer**
   - Write $\cos \theta = \frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m, n) = 1$
   - Calculate $m + n$
