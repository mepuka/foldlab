# Solution to Liquid Transfer Problem

## 1. What We Are Calculating

We need to determine how many liters of mixture are poured from flask B back to flask A (variable x), given that the final mixture in B contains exactly 30% mercury.

## 2. Define Variables and Set Up

Let:
- g = initial volume of gallium in flask B (liters)
- Initial mercury in flask A = 1 liter
- Flask B capacity = (1 + g) liters (it perfectly holds its initial contents)

## 3. Step 1: Pour Mercury from A to B

When 1 liter of mercury is poured into flask B:
- Total volume would be: 1 + g liters of mercury + 1 liter mercury = 1 + g + 1 = 2 + g liters
- Flask B capacity: 1 + g liters
- **Overflow amount**: (2 + g) - (1 + g) = **1 liter of pure mercury overflows**

**After Step 1:**
- Flask B contains: 1 liter mercury + g liters gallium = (1 + g) liters total
- Concentration in B: mercury = $\frac{1}{1+g}$, gallium = $\frac{g}{1+g}$

## 4. Step 2: Pour Mixture Back from B to A

When x liters of mixture are poured from B back to A:
- Mercury removed: $x \cdot \frac{1}{1+g} = \frac{x}{1+g}$ liters
- Mercury remaining in B: $1 - \frac{x}{1+g} = \frac{1+g-x}{1+g}$ liters
- Volume remaining in B: $(1+g) - x$ liters

**After Step 2:**
- Mercury percentage in B: $\frac{\text{Mercury in B}}{\text{Total in B}} = \frac{\frac{1+g-x}{1+g}}{(1+g)-x}$

Simplifying:
$$\frac{\frac{1+g-x}{1+g}}{1+g-x} = \frac{1}{1+g}$$

## 5. Apply the 30% Mercury Condition

The problem states the final mixture in B is 30% mercury:

$$\frac{1}{1+g} = 0.30 = \frac{3}{10}$$

$$1 + g = \frac{10}{3}$$

$$g = \frac{7}{3} \text{ liters}$$

## 6. Determine x (Amount Poured Back)

The standard assumption for such problems is that **flask A is refilled to its original capacity of 1 liter**. This means:

$$x = 1 \text{ liter}$$

## 7. Verification

Let's verify with g = 7/3 and x = 1:

**After Step 1:** B contains $\frac{10}{3}$ liters
- Mercury: 1 liter
- Gallium: $\frac{7}{3}$ liters

**Pouring 1 liter of mixture:**
- Mercury poured: $1 \times \frac{1}{10/3} = 1 \times \frac{3}{10} = \frac{3}{10}$ liters
- Gallium poured: $1 \times \frac{7/3}{10/3} = \frac{7}{10}$ liters

**After Step 2:** B contains $\frac{10}{3} - 1 = \frac{7}{3}$ liters
- Mercury: $1 - \frac{3}{10} = \frac{7}{10}$ liters
- Gallium: $\frac{7}{3} - \frac{7}{10} = \frac{49}{30}$ liters

**Mercury percentage in B:**
$$\frac{7/10}{7/3} = \frac{7}{10} \times \frac{3}{7} = \frac{3}{10} = 30\%$$ ✓

✓ Verified: The answer satisfies all constraints.
