# Complete Solution: Minimum Operations to Make All Numbers Equal

## Step 1: Establish the Lower Bound (Necessity)

### Step 1.1: Count Distinct Values Requirement
Starting configuration: {1, 2, 3, ..., 2026}
- Number of distinct values initially: **2026**
- Goal state: All numbers equal (1 distinct value)
- Target: Reduce from 2026 distinct values to 1 distinct value

### Step 1.2: Operations Reduce Distinct Count
Each operation:
- Selects numbers from the blackboard
- Replaces one number with |max - min|
- In the best case: introduces a value that isn't already on the blackboard
- Effect: Reduces count of distinct values by **at most 1**

### Step 1.3: Calculate Minimum Bound
$$\text{Reductions needed} = 2026 - 1 = 2025$$

Since each operation reduces distinct values by at most 1:
$$\text{Minimum operations} \geq 2025$$

## Step 2: Construct an Explicit Strategy (Sufficiency)

### Step 2.1: Strategy Description
**Algorithm: Repeatedly replace the maximum element**

Starting set: {1, 2, 3, ..., 2026}

| Operation | Before | max | min | |max-min| | After | Distinct |
|-----------|--------|-----|-----|---------|---------|----------|
| 1 | {1,...,2026} | 2026 | 1 | 2025 | {1,...,2025} | 2025 |
| 2 | {1,...,2025} | 2025 | 1 | 2024 | {1,...,2024} | 2024 |
| 3 | {1,...,2024} | 2024 | 1 | 2023 | {1,...,2023} | 2023 |
| ⋮ | ⋮ | ⋮ | ⋮ | ⋮ | ⋮ | ⋮ |
| 2024 | {1, 2} | 2 | 1 | 1 | {1} (one 1 was already there) | 2 |
| 2025 | {1, 2} | 2 | 1 | 1 | {1, 1} | 1 |

### Step 2.2: Pattern Recognition
After operation $k$: The set contains {1, 2, 3, ..., 2026-k}

**Verification by substitution for specific operations:**

**Operation 1:** 
- Set: {1, 2, ..., 2026}
- max = 2026, min = 1
- Replacement: |2026 - 1| = 2025 ✓
- New set: {1, 2, ..., 2025} (2025 is already present, so no new distinct value)
- Distinct count: 2025 ✓

**Operation 2:**
- Set: {1, 2, ..., 2025}
- max = 2025, min = 1  
- Replacement: |2025 - 1| = 2024 ✓
- New set: {1, 2, ..., 2024} (2024 is already present)
- Distinct count: 2024 ✓

**Operation 2024:**
- Set: {1, 2}
- max = 2, min = 1
- Replacement: |2 - 1| = 1 ✓
- New set: {1, 1}
- Distinct count: 1 ✓

**Operation 2025:** Already at {1, 1} (all equal) - No more operations needed.

### Step 2.3: Convergence Verification
After exactly 2025 operations:
- All 2026 numbers equal 1
- No further operations possible (max = min = 1)
- Condition satisfied ✓

## Step 3: Verify 2025 is Both Necessary and Sufficient

### Step 3.1: Why We Can't Do Better Than 2025
- We must eliminate 2025 distinct values
- Each operation eliminates at most 1 distinct value
- **Lower bound: 2025 operations**

### Step 3.2: Why 2025 Operations Suffice  
- We constructed an algorithm achieving exactly 2025 operations
- All numbers become equal after these operations
- **Upper bound: 2025 operations**

### Step 3.3: Tight Bound
$$\text{Lower bound} = \text{Upper bound} = 2025$$

Therefore, the answer is unique and optimal.
