# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. A real number $x$ satisfies the equation: $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}=26x$
2. We're considering positive values of $x$
3. The base of the logarithm is 2026
4. The 20th root is applied to $x^{\log_{2026}x}$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the number of positive divisors of the product of all possible positive values of $x$ that satisfy the given equation.

In other words:
- Solve the equation for all positive real $x$
- Multiply all solutions together to get a single value $P$
- Count the number of positive divisors of $P$
