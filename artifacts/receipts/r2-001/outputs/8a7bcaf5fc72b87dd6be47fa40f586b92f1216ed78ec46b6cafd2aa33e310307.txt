## Step 1: Identify the Tangency Requirement

For the equation $|x^2 + ax + b| = c$ to have exactly 3 integer solutions, we need:
- One horizontal line ($y = c$ or $y = -c$) to be tangent to the parabola (1 solution)
- The other horizontal line to intersect the parabola at exactly 2 points (2 solutions)

## Step 2: Set Up Vertex Conditions

The parabola $f(x) = x^2 + ax + b$ has vertex at $x = -\frac{a}{2}$.

For the vertex to be at an integer point, let $a = 2m$ where $m$ is an integer.
Then the vertex is at $x = -m$.

## Step 3: Express the Vertex Value

At the vertex $x = -m$:
$$f(-m) = (-m)^2 + a(-m) + b = m^2 - 2m \cdot m + b = m^2 - 2m^2 + b = b - m^2$$

Let $v = b - m^2$ (the vertex value).
