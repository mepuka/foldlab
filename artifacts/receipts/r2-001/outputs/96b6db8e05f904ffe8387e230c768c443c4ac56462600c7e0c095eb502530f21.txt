# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

**Face specifications:**
- Two isosceles triangular faces with side lengths: $5\sqrt{10}, 5\sqrt{10}, 10$
- Two isosceles triangular faces with side lengths: $5\sqrt{10}, 5\sqrt{10}, 18$

**Sphere specifications:**
- All four vertices lie on a sphere with center $S$ (circumsphere)
- All four faces are tangent to a sphere with center $R$ (insphere)

**Answer format constraint:**
- The distance $RS$ can be expressed as $\frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$

## What is being asked:

Find the distance between the circumcenter $S$ and the incenter $R$ of the tetrahedron, express it as a fraction $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, and compute $m + n$.
