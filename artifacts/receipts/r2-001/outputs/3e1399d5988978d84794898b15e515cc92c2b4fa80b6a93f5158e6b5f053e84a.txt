# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Sequence domain**: $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ where each $a_n \in \{0, 1, 2, \ldots, 396\}$ (nonnegative integers less than 397)

2. **Initial condition**: $a_0 = 1$

3. **Recurrence relation**: For all nonnegative integers $n$:
$$a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$$

4. **Uniqueness**: The sequence satisfying these conditions is unique

5. **Additional constraint**: $a_{2026} = 9$

6. **Note about 397**: It is prime

## What Is Being Asked

Compute the remainder when $\displaystyle \sum_{i=0}^{2026} a_i$ is divided by 397.

In other words, find:
$$\left( a_0 + a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_{2026} \right) \bmod 397$$
