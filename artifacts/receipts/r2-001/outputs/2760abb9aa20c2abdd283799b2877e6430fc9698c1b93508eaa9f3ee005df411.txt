# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Two circles**: $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ that intersect at exactly two points $P$ and $Q$
2. **Distance between intersection points**: $PQ = 10$
3. **Common external tangents**: $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ are the two common external tangents to both circles
4. **Tangency points on $\Gamma_1$**: 
   - $\Gamma_1$ touches $\ell_1$ at $U_1$
   - $\Gamma_1$ touches $\ell_2$ at $U_2$
5. **Tangency points on $\Gamma_2$**:
   - $\Gamma_2$ touches $\ell_1$ at $V_1$
   - $\Gamma_2$ touches $\ell_2$ at $V_2$
6. **Distance from $P$ to $\ell_1$**: $d(P, \ell_1) = 3$
7. **Distance from $P$ to $\ell_2$**: $d(P, \ell_2) = 12$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the area of quadrilateral $U_1U_2V_2V_1$ (the quadrilateral with vertices at the four tangency points, in order: $U_1$ on $\ell_1$ from $\Gamma_1$, $U_2$ on $\ell_2$ from $\Gamma_1$, $V_2$ on $\ell_2$ from $\Gamma_2$, $V_1$ on $\ell_1$ from $\Gamma_2$).
