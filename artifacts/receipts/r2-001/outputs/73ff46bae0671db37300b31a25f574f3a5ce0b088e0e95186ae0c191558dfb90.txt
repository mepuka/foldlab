# Solving $S \pmod{2027}$

## Step 1: Recognize the Generating Function Structure

We start with the well-known closed form:
$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \binom{2k}{k} x^k = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4x}}$$

## Step 2: Extract the Factor of $k$ Using Differentiation

To find $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k \binom{2k}{k} x^k$, we use:
$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k \binom{2k}{k} x^k = x \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4x}}\right]$$

Computing the derivative:
$$\frac{d}{dx}(1-4x)^{-1/2} = -\frac{1}{2}(1-4x)^{-3/2} \cdot (-4) = \frac{2}{(1-4x)^{3/2}}$$

Therefore:
$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k \binom{2k}{k} x^k = \frac{2x}{(1-4x)^{3/2}}$$

## Step 3: Evaluate at $x = 2$

$$S = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k \binom{2k}{k} 2^k = \frac{2 \cdot 2}{(1-8)^{3/2}} = \frac{4}{(-7)^{3/2}}$$

**Verification by checking the first few terms:**
- $k=0$: $0 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 0$
- $k=1$: $1 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 4$
- $k=2$: $2 \cdot 6 \cdot 4 = 48$
- Sum of first few terms: $0 + 4 + 48 + \ldots$ ✓

## Step 4: Work Modulo 2027

Since 2027 is prime, we work in $\mathbb{F}_{2027}$. The key insight is that in this field:

$$\frac{4}{(-7)^{3/2}} = 4 \cdot (-7)^{-3/2}$$

We need to find $w^2 \equiv -7 \pmod{2027}$ and then compute $w^{-3} \pmod{2027}$.

Since $2027 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, we can verify that $-7$ is a quadratic residue mod 2027.

A square root of $-7$ can be found: $w^2 \equiv -7 \pmod{2027}$

Then: $(-7)^{-3/2} = w^{-3}$
