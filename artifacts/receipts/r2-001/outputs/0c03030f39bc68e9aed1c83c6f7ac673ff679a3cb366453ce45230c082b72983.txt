# Solution: Finding Integers Expressible as $a + b + ab$

## Step 1: Recognize the Algebraic Form

Starting with $a + b + ab$, I'll factor this expression:
$$a + b + ab = a(1 + b) + b = a(1 + b) + (1 + b) - 1 = (a+1)(b+1) - 1$$

The condition $a + b + ab \leq 100$ becomes:
$$(a+1)(b+1) - 1 \leq 100$$
$$(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$$

## Step 2: Determine the Range for Products

Let $m = a+1$ and $n = b+1$ where $a, b \geq 1$ (positive integers).

This gives us: $m, n \geq 2$

Since $a \neq b$, we need: $m \neq n$

We need to find all pairs $(m, n)$ with $m, n \geq 2$, $m \neq n$, and $m \cdot n \leq 101$.
