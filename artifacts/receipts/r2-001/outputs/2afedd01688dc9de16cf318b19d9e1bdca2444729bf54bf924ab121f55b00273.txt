# Complete Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities:

1. Triangle $\triangle ABC$ lies in plane $\mathcal{P}$
2. $AB = 6$
3. $AC = 4$
4. $\angle BAC = 90°$
5. $D$ is the reflection of the centroid of $\triangle ABC$ across line $\overline{BC}$
6. Four spheres, all on the same side of $\mathcal{P}$:
   - Sphere 1: radius $1$, tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $A$
   - Sphere 2: radius $2$, tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $B$
   - Sphere 3: radius $3$, tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $C$
   - Sphere 4: radius $r$ (unknown), tangent to $\mathcal{P}$ at point $D$
7. All four spheres are tangent to a second plane $\mathcal{T}$
8. All four spheres are on the same side of $\mathcal{T}$

## Conditions:

- The four spheres are mutually tangent to each other (implied by the problem structure)
- The radii $m$ and $n$ in $r = \frac{m}{n}$ are relatively prime positive integers

## What is Being Asked:

Find the value of $r$ (the radius of the fourth sphere), express it as a fraction $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, and compute $m + n$.
