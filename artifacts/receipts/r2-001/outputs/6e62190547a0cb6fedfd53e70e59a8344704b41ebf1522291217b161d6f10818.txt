# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Information

**String:** SUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUNSUN

**Structural details of the string:**
- The pattern "SUN" is repeated 10 times consecutively
- Total length: 30 letters
- Letter frequencies in the string:
  - S appears 10 times (at positions 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28)
  - U appears 10 times (at positions 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29)
  - N appears 10 times (at positions 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30)

**Operation:** Erase exactly 26 letters from the string

**Constraint:** The remaining 4 letters must spell "SUNS" *in order* (reading left to right as they appear in the original string)

## What is Being Asked

Determine the number of distinct ways to select and keep 4 letters from the original 30-letter string such that:
1. Exactly 4 letters remain (equivalently, exactly 26 letters are erased)
2. These 4 remaining letters, read in their original order in the string, spell S-U-N-S

In other words: count the number of ways to choose positions $i_1 < i_2 < i_3 < i_4$ from the string such that the letter at position $i_1$ is S, at position $i_2$ is U, at position $i_3$ is N, and at position $i_4$ is S.
