# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Definitions:**
- For positive integers $n$ and $k > 1$: $s_k(n)$ = sum of digits of $n$ in base $k$
- Example: $s_3(2025) = 5$ because $2025 = 2210000_3$

**Definition of Digiroot:**
- A positive integer $n$ is a *digiroot* if and only if:
$$s_2(n) = \sqrt{s_4(n)}$$

This means:
- $s_2(n)$ must be a positive integer (the sum of binary digits)
- $s_4(n)$ must be a perfect square
- The square root of $s_4(n)$ must equal $s_2(n)$

**Domain:**
- We only consider digiroots $n$ with $n < 1000$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the sum of all positive integers $n < 1000$ that satisfy the digiroot condition $s_2(n) = \sqrt{s_4(n)}$.
