# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Variable**: A positive integer $n$
2. **Expression**: $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$
3. **Divisibility requirement**: $n$ must divide the expression $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$

## What is Being Asked

**Find the largest positive integer $n$ such that:**
$$n \mid \left[(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450\right]$$

In other words, find the maximum value of $n$ for which $(\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor)!^{n!} + 450$ is divisible by $n$.
