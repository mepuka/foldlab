# Analysis of Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Function Definition**: For each positive integer $n$:
   - Let $d$ = the greatest digit appearing in the decimal representation of $n$
   - Let $b$ = the least integer greater than $d$ (so $b = d + 1$)
   - $f(n)$ = the value obtained by interpreting the decimal digits of $n$ as a base-$b$ numeral

2. **Domain**: Positive integers $n$ where $n < 1000$

3. **Example Given**: 
   - $n = 72$ has greatest digit $7$, so $b = 8$
   - $f(72) = 7 \cdot 8 + 2 = 58$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the **count** of positive integers $n < 1000$ such that $f(n) = n$.

In other words, find how many positive integers less than 1000 remain unchanged when their decimal digits are reinterpreted as a numeral in base $b$ (where $b$ equals one more than the greatest digit).
