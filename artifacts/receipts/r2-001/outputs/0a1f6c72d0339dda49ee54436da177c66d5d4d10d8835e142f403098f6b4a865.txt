# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Triangle type**: $\triangle ABC$ is isosceles with $AB = BC$
2. **Point definition**: $I$ is the incenter of $\triangle ABC$
3. **Perimeter ratio**: $\text{Perimeter}(\triangle ABC) : \text{Perimeter}(\triangle AIC) = 125 : 6$
4. **Integer constraint**: All sides of both $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle AIC$ have integer lengths
5. **Triangle inequality**: Both triangles must satisfy the triangle inequality

## What Is Being Asked

Find the **minimum possible value of $AB$** (one of the two equal sides of the isosceles triangle).

---

## Key Setup

With standard notation where $AB = BC = c$ and $AC = b$:
- Perimeter of $\triangle ABC$ = $2c + b$
- The sides $AI$ and $CI$ can be expressed using the inradius formula
- Perimeter of $\triangle AIC$ = $AI + IC + AC$

The condition that this ratio equals $125:6$ creates constraints on the possible values of $b$ and $c$, which must all be positive integers satisfying triangle inequalities.
