## Step 1: Identify Possible Partition Structures

For an 8×8 square divided into exactly 3 rectangles, the topologically distinct cases are:
1. **Three parallel strips** (all cuts in same direction)
2. **L-shaped partition** (one cut creates 2 regions, one region further subdivided perpendicularly)

## Step 2: Case 1 - Three Horizontal Strips

Make two horizontal cuts at heights $h_1$ and $h_2$ where $0 < h_1 < h_2 < 8$ (integer positions).

- Valid integer positions: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
- Number of ways to choose 2 positions: $\binom{7}{2} = 21$

Creates rectangles: $8 \times h_1$, $8 \times (h_2-h_1)$, $8 \times (8-h_2)$

**Case 1 total: 21 ways**

## Step 3: Case 2 - Three Vertical Strips

Make two vertical cuts at positions $v_1$ and $v_2$ where $0 < v_1 < v_2 < 8$ (integer positions).

- Valid integer positions: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
- Number of ways to choose 2 positions: $\binom{7}{2} = 21$

Creates rectangles: $v_1 \times 8$, $(v_2-v_1) \times 8$, $(8-v_2) \times 8$

**Case 2 total: 21 ways**

## Step 4: Case 3 - L-Shaped Partitions

One straight cut creates 2 rectangles; one of these is further subdivided with a perpendicular cut.

### Case 3a: Horizontal cut first, then vertical cut in one piece

- First horizontal cut at height $h$ where $0 < h < 8$: 7 choices
- This creates two rectangles: $8 \times h$ (top) and $8 \times (8-h)$ (bottom)
- **Subdivide the top rectangle** with a vertical cut at position $v$: $0 < v < 8$ gives 7 choices
  - Creates: $v \times h$, $(8-v) \times h$, $8 \times (8-h)$
  - Total: $7 \times 7 = 49$ ways
- **Subdivide the bottom rectangle** with a vertical cut at position $v$: $0 < v < 8$ gives 7 choices
  - Creates: $8 \times h$, $v \times (8-h)$, $(8-v) \times (8-h)$
  - Total: $7 \times 7 = 49$ ways

Subtotal: $49 + 49 = 98$ ways

### Case 3b: Vertical cut first, then horizontal cut in one piece

- First vertical cut at position $v$ where $0 < v < 8$: 7 choices
- This creates two rectangles: $v \times 8$ (left) and $(8-v) \times 8$ (right)
- **Subdivide the left rectangle** with a horizontal cut at height $h$: $0 < h < 8$ gives 7 choices
  - Creates: $v \times h$, $v \times (8-h)$, $(8-v) \times 8$
  - Total: $7 \times 7 = 49$ ways
- **Subdivide the right rectangle** with a horizontal cut at height $h$: $0 < h < 8$ gives 7 choices
  - Creates: $v \times 8$, $(8-v) \times h$, $(8-v) \times (8-h)$
  - Total: $7 \times 7 = 49$ ways

Subtotal: $49 + 49 = 98$ ways

**Case 3 total: $98 + 98 = 196$ ways**

- Cases 1 & 2 are mutually exclusive (all cuts parallel vs. perpendicular cuts)
