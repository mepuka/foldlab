# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Take logarithms to simplify the radical

Starting with: $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}=26x$

Applying $\log_2$ to both sides:
$$\log_2\left(\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}\right) = \log_2(26x)$$

Converting the 20th root to a power:
$$\frac{1}{20}\log_2(x^{\log_{2026}x}) = \log_2(26x)$$

## Step 2: Simplify the left side using logarithm properties

Using the power rule $\log(a^b) = b\log(a)$:
$$\frac{1}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x \cdot \log_2 x = \log_2(26x)$$

Expanding the right side:
$$\frac{1}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x \cdot \log_2 x = \log_2 26 + \log_2 x$$

## Step 3: Convert logarithms to a common base

Using change of base formula: $\log_{2026}x = \frac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 2026}$

Substituting:
$$\frac{1}{20} \cdot \frac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 2026} \cdot \log_2 x = \log_2 26 + \log_2 x$$

$$\frac{(\log_2 x)^2}{20\log_2 2026} = \log_2 26 + \log_2 x$$

## Step 4: Let $y = \log_2 x$ and form a quadratic equation

Substituting $y = \log_2 x$:
$$\frac{y^2}{20\log_2 2026} = \log_2 26 + y$$

Rearranging to standard form:
$$\frac{y^2}{20\log_2 2026} - y - \log_2 26 = 0$$

Multiplying through by $20\log_2 2026$ (let $a = \log_2 2026$ and $b = \log_2 26$):
$$y^2 - 20ay - 20ab = 0$$

## Step 5: Solve the quadratic equation

Using Vieta's formulas for $y^2 - 20ay - 20ab = 0$:
- **Sum of roots:** $y_1 + y_2 = 20a = 20\log_2 2026$
- **Product of roots:** $y_1 \cdot y_2 = -20ab = -20\log_2 2026 \cdot \log_2 26$

## Step 6: Find the product of all solutions

Converting back from $y = \log_2 x$:
$$x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2^{y_1} \cdot 2^{y_2} = 2^{y_1 + y_2}$$

$$x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2^{20\log_2 2026}$$

Using the property $2^{\log_2 n} = n$:
$$x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2^{\log_2(2026^{20})} = 2026^{20}$$

## Step 7: Determine the prime factorization

First, factor 2026:
$$2026 = 2 \times 1013$$

Checking if 1013 is prime (checking divisibility by primes up to $\sqrt{1013} \approx 31.8$):
- 1013 is odd (not divisible by 2)
- Sum of digits: 1+0+1+3 = 5 (not divisible by 3)
- Checking 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29, 31 shows none divide 1013

Therefore 1013 is prime.

$$P = 2026^{20} = (2 \times 1013)^{20} = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$$

## Step 8: Count divisors using the divisor formula

For $P = 2^{20} \times 1013^{20}$:

$$\tau(P) = (20 + 1)(20 + 1) = 21 \times 21 = 441$$

**Verification:** Each divisor has the form $2^a \times 1013^b$ where $0 \le a \le 20$ and $0 \le b \le 20$. This gives 21 choices for each exponent.

FINAL ANSWER: **441**
