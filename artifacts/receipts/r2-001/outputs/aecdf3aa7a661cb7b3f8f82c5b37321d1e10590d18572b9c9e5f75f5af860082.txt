# Solution: Finding the Area of △BCY

I'll execute this plan step by step.

## Step 1: Set Up Coordinate System

Place square $ABCD$ with:
- $A = (0, 0)$
- $B = (12, 0)$
- $C = (12, 12)$
- $D = (0, 12)$

## Step 2: Locate Vertices W and X

Given information:
- $W$ is on $\overline{AD}$ with $AW = 4$: **$W = (0, 4)$**
- $X$ is on $\overline{AB}$ with $AX = 5$: **$X = (5, 0)$**

## Step 3: Verify the Side Length of Square WXYZ

$$|WX| = \sqrt{(5-0)^2 + (0-4)^2} = \sqrt{25 + 16} = \sqrt{41}$$

The side length of square $WXYZ$ is $\sqrt{41}$.

## Step 4: Find Coordinates of Y and Z

To form a square, I need to rotate $\vec{WX}$ by 90° counterclockwise.

**Vector $\vec{WX}$:**
$$\vec{WX} = (5, -4)$$

**90° counterclockwise rotation of $(5, -4)$:**
$$(x, y) \rightarrow (-y, x)$$
$$(5, -4) \rightarrow (4, 5)$$

**Find $Z$ and $Y$:**
- $Z = W + (4, 5) = (0, 4) + (4, 5) = (4, 9)$
- $Y = X + (4, 5) = (5, 0) + (4, 5) = (9, 5)$

**Verification:** The square $WXYZ$ has vertices $(0,4) \to (5,0) \to (9,5) \to (4,9)$ ✓

## Step 5: Calculate the Area of △BCY

Using the coordinate formula for triangle area:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|x_B(y_C - y_Y) + x_C(y_Y - y_B) + x_Y(y_B - y_C)|$$

**Substituting vertices:** $B = (12, 0)$, $C = (12, 12)$, $Y = (9, 5)$

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|12(12-5) + 12(5-0) + 9(0-12)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|12(7) + 12(5) + 9(-12)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|84 + 60 - 108|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|36|$$

$$= 18$$

## Final Answer

The area of $\triangle BCY$ is $\boxed{18}$ square units.
