# Finding All Palindromes with Digit Sum 13 (No Zeros)

## Step 1: Organize by Palindrome Length

I'll categorize palindromes by the number of digits. For an n-digit palindrome, the first ⌈n/2⌉ digits determine the entire palindrome through symmetry.

---

## Step 2: Analyze 1-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Single digits from {1-9} that sum to 13

- Format: `a`
- Independent digit: a
- Required sum: a = 13
- **Problem:** a must be ≤ 9
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 3: Analyze 2-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `aa`

- Format: `aa`
- Independent digit: a
- Required sum: 2a = 13
- Solution: a = 6.5 (not an integer)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 4: Analyze 3-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `aba` with 2a + b = 13

- Independent digits: a (first), b (middle)
- Required: 2a + b = 13, so b = 13 - 2a

Finding valid combinations:
- a = 1: b = 11 ❌ (exceeds 9)
- a = 2: b = 9 ✓ → **292**
- a = 3: b = 7 ✓ → **373**
- a = 4: b = 5 ✓ → **454**
- a = 5: b = 3 ✓ → **535**
- a = 6: b = 1 ✓ → **616**
- a = 7: b = -1 ❌ (negative)

**Count: 5**

---

## Step 5: Analyze 4-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abba` with 2a + 2b = 13

- Independent digits: a, b
- Required sum: 2(a + b) = 13
- This requires a + b = 6.5 (not an integer)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 6: Analyze 5-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcba` with 2a + 2b + c = 13

- Independent digits: a, b, c (middle)
- Required: c = 13 - 2a - 2b
- Need: 1 ≤ c ≤ 9, so 4 ≤ 2(a+b) ≤ 12, thus 2 ≤ a+b ≤ 6

Finding valid combinations by value of a + b:

| a+b | c | Combinations (a,b) | Count |
|-----|---|-------------------|-------|
| 2   | 9 | (1,1) | 1 |
| 3   | 7 | (1,2), (2,1) | 2 |
| 4   | 5 | (1,3), (2,2), (3,1) | 3 |
| 5   | 3 | (1,4), (2,3), (3,2), (4,1) | 4 |
| 6   | 1 | (1,5), (2,4), (3,3), (4,2), (5,1) | 5 |

Examples: 11911, 12721, 21712, 13531, 22522, 31513, 14341, 23332, 32323, 41314, 15151, 24142, 33133, **42124**, 51125

**Count: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15**

---

## Step 7: Analyze 6-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abccba` with 2a + 2b + 2c = 13

- Required: 2(a + b + c) = 13
- Impossible (13 is odd, left side is even)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 8: Analyze 7-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcdcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + d = 13

- Independent digits: a, b, c, d (middle)
- Required: d = 13 - 2(a + b + c)
- Need: 1 ≤ d ≤ 9, so 2 ≤ a + b + c ≤ 6
- Since a,b,c ≥ 1: must have 3 ≤ a + b + c ≤ 6

| a+b+c | d | Count (stars and bars) |
|-------|---|------------------------|
| 3 | 7 | C(2,0) = 1 |
| 4 | 5 | C(3,1) = 3 |
| 5 | 3 | C(4,2) = 6 |
| 6 | 1 | C(5,2) = 10 |

**Count: 1 + 3 + 6 + 10 = 20**

---

## Step 9: Analyze 8-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcddcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + 2d = 13

- Required: 2(a + b + c + d) = 13
- Impossible (13 is odd)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 10: Analyze 9-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcdedcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + 2d + e = 13

- Independent digits: a, b, c, d, e (middle)
- Required: e = 13 - 2(a + b + c + d)
- Need: 1 ≤ e ≤ 9, so 2 ≤ a + b + c + d ≤ 6
- Since all ≥ 1: must have 4 ≤ a + b + c + d ≤ 6

| a+b+c+d | e | Count |
|---------|---|-------|
| 4 | 5 | C(3,0) = 1 |
| 5 | 3 | C(4,1) = 4 |
| 6 | 1 | C(5,2) = 10 |

**Count: 1 + 4 + 10 = 15**

---

## Step 11: Analyze 10-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcdeedcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + 2d + 2e = 13

- Required: 2(sum of 5 digits) = 13
- Impossible (13 is odd)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 12: Analyze 11-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcdefedcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + 2d + 2e + f = 13

- Independent digits: a, b, c, d, e, f (middle)
- Required: f = 13 - 2(a + b + c + d + e)
- Need: 1 ≤ f ≤ 9, so 2 ≤ a + b + c + d + e ≤ 6
- Since all ≥ 1: must have 5 ≤ a + b + c + d + e ≤ 6

| sum | f | Count |
|-----|---|-------|
| 5 | 3 | 1 |
| 6 | 1 | 5 |

**Count: 1 + 5 = 6**

---

## Step 13: Analyze 12-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes with 2(sum of 6 digits) = 13

- Impossible (13 is odd)
- **Count: 0**

---

## Step 14: Analyze 13-digit Palindromes

**What we're calculating:** Palindromes of form `abcdefgfedcba` with 2a + 2b + 2c + 2d + 2e + 2f + g = 13

- Independent digits: a, b, c, d, e, f, g (middle)
- Required: g = 13 - 2(a + b + c + d + e + f)
- Minimum sum of 6 digits (all 1's) = 6, giving g = 1
- This works! Only one case: (1,1,1,1,1,1,1)

**Count: 1**

---

## Step 15: Analyze 14+ digit Palindromes

For 14-digit palindromes: 2(sum of 8 digits) = 13 → impossible

For 15-digit: The minimum digit sum of 7 independent digits is 7, requiring 2(7)
