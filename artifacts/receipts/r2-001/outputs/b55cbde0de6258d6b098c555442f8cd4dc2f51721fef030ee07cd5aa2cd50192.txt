# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Quantities:**
- Grid dimensions: 3 rows × 10 columns = 30 cells total
- Starting cell: A (located in row 2, column 2)
- Ending cell: B (located in row 2, column 9)

**Conditions:**
1. Movement is allowed only between cells that share an edge (4-directional adjacency: up, down, left, right)
2. The path must visit every cell exactly once
3. Starting in cell A counts as the first visit
4. The path must end at cell B

# What is Being Asked

Compute the total number of distinct Hamiltonian paths from cell A to cell B on this 3 × 10 grid.

Equivalently: How many ways can Alexandrimitrov walk from A to B visiting all 30 cells exactly once?
