# Analysis of the Competition Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Initial Setup:**
- Numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 2026 are written on a blackboard
- Total of 2026 numbers initially

**Operation Rules:**
- In each operation, one number on the blackboard may be replaced
- The replacement value must be: |max(current numbers) − min(current numbers)|
- We can only replace one number per operation
- The replacement must be a positive difference (so we need max ≠ min to perform an operation)

**Constraints:**
- Each operation reduces the total count of distinct numbers by at most 1 (one number is replaced)
- The operation preserves a key property: all numbers on the board remain non-negative integers

## What Is Being Asked

Determine:
1. **The minimum number of operations needed** to reach a state where all numbers on the blackboard are equal
2. **A proof** that this minimum is both necessary (cannot do better) and sufficient (can be achieved)

In other words: Find the value of n such that:
- It is **impossible** to make all numbers equal in fewer than n operations
- It **is possible** to make all numbers equal in exactly n operations
