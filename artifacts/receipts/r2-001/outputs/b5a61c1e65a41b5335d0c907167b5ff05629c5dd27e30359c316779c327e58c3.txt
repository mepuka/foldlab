# Competition Math Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **A regular hexagon** with side length 4
2. **Lattice subdivision**: The hexagon is subdivided into 96 equilateral triangles of side length 1
3. **Vertex set S**: All vertices of this triangular lattice
4. **The center of the hexagon**: A fixed point (geometric center)

## What is Being Asked

Find the count of **nondegenerate triangles** satisfying:
- All three vertices belong to the set $S$ (lattice vertices)
- The center of the hexagon lies **strictly in the interior** of the triangle (not on the boundary)

In other words: compute $|\{\triangle ABC : A, B, C \in S, \text{ center} \in \text{int}(\triangle ABC)\}|$
