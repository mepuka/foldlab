# Solution: Palindromes with Digit Sum 13

## Step 1: Categorize by palindrome length

I need to find all palindromes where:
- All digits are from {1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9} (no zeros)
- The sum of all digits equals 13

For an n-digit palindrome, only the first ⌈n/2⌉ digits are independent.

## Step 2: Analyze feasibility by length

Key observation: When we sum digits in a palindrome, digits in symmetric positions appear twice (except possibly the middle digit in odd-length palindromes).

For even-length palindromes: all digits appear twice → total sum is even
For odd-length palindromes: all digits appear twice except middle → total sum = 2×(sum of outer digits) + (middle digit) = even + odd = odd ✓

Since 13 is odd, **only odd-length palindromes are possible**.

This eliminates: 2-digit, 4-digit, 6-digit, 8-digit, etc.

## Step 3: Enumerate valid palindromes by length

### **1-digit palindromes:** 
Need: a = 13
Since a ∈ {1,...,9}, this is impossible.
**Count: 0**
