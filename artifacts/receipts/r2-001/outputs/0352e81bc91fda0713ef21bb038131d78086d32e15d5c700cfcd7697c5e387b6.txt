# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Establish the Constraint Condition

For a sphere of radius $r = 42$ centered at $(P_x, P_y, 42)$ to lie completely inside a hemisphere of radius $R = 200$:

The farthest point on the small sphere from the origin occurs along the radial direction and is at distance:
$$d_{\max} = \sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42$$

For containment: $d_{\max} \leq 200$

$$\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42 \leq 200$$

## Step 2: Simplify the Constraint

$$\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} \leq 158$$

Square both sides:
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$

Calculate: $158^2 = 24964$ and $42^2 = 1764$ ✓

$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 \leq 23200$$

## Step 3: Identify Region $\mathcal{T}$

The region $\mathcal{T}$ is a disk centered at the origin with radius:
$$\rho = \sqrt{23200}$$

Simplify by factoring:
$$\rho = \sqrt{23200} = \sqrt{400 \cdot 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$$

## Step 4: Calculate the Area Ratio

The reference disk (in the $(P_x, P_y)$ plane) has radius $R = 200$.

$$\frac{\text{Area of } \mathcal{T}}{\text{Area of reference disk}} = \frac{\pi \rho^2}{\pi R^2} = \frac{\rho^2}{R^2}$$

$$= \frac{23200}{40000}$$

Note: $200^2 = 40000$ ✓

## Step 5: Reduce to Lowest Terms

Find the GCD of 23200 and 40000:

$$23200 = 8 \times 2900 = 8 \times 29 \times 100$$
$$40000 = 8 \times 5000 = 8 \times 50 \times 100$$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{232}{400} = \frac{29}{50}$$

Verify $\gcd(29, 50)$:
- 29 is prime
- 50 = 2 × 5²
- Therefore $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓

## Step 6: Final Answer

$$p = 29, \quad q = 50$$

$$p + q = 29 + 50 = \boxed{79}$$
