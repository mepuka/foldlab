# Analysis of the Competition Math Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions:

1. **Grid/Square**: An 8 × 8 square
2. **Number of Rectangles**: Exactly 3 rectangles
3. **Coverage**: The rectangles must divide (partition) the square completely with no gaps or overlaps
4. **Side Lengths**: Each rectangle must have positive integer side lengths
5. **Implicit Condition**: The rectangles must fit within the 8 × 8 boundary

## What is Being Asked:

Determine the total count of **distinct ways** to partition an 8 × 8 square into exactly 3 rectangles with integer side lengths.

### Clarifications on "distinct":
- Two partitions are considered different if the rectangles differ in position or dimensions
- The problem asks for the number of valid configurations, not the number of ways to label or order the rectangles
