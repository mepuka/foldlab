# Analysis of Competition Problem

## Given Quantities

1. $HO = 20$
2. $SE = 26$
3. $OS = 10$

## Given Conditions

1. $HORSE$ is a convex pentagon (vertices in order: $H, O, R, S, E$)
2. $\angle EHO = 90°$
3. $\angle ORS = 90°$
4. $\angle SEH = 90°$
5. $\angle HOR = 135°$
6. $\angle RSE = 135°$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the area of pentagon $HORSE$.

---

## Notes on Interpretation

- The five angles specified are interior angles of the pentagon at vertices $H, O, R, S, E$ respectively
- The pentagon has three right angles and two 135° angles
- The sum of interior angles is $90° + 135° + 90° + 135° + 90° = 540°$, which confirms this is a valid pentagon
- Three specific side lengths and one diagonal length ($OS$) are given, which should provide enough constraints to determine the pentagon uniquely (up to congruence)
