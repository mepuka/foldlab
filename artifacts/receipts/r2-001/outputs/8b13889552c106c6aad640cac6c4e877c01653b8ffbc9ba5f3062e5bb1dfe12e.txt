# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Die specification**: Standard fair six-sided die (outcomes 1-6, each with probability 1/6)

2. **Coin distribution rules**:
   - Alice gets a coin: outcomes 1 or 2 (probability = 2/6 = 1/3 per roll)
   - Bob gets a coin: outcomes 3 or 4 (probability = 2/6 = 1/3 per roll)
   - Carol gets a coin: outcomes 5 or 6 (probability = 2/6 = 1/3 per roll)

3. **Process**: Die is rolled repeatedly until one or more of the stopping conditions are met

4. **Stopping conditions**: The process conceptually ends when at least one person receives a coin (or we're checking if a certain event happens before someone gets a coin)

## What is Being Asked

Find the probability that **both of the following occur before Carol receives any coins**:
- Alice receives at least 2 coins, AND
- Bob receives at least 2 coins

Express this probability as $\frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, then compute $100m + n$.
