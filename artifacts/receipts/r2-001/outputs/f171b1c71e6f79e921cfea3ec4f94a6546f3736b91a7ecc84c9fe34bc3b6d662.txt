# Solution: Finding the Sum of the Sequence

## Step 1: Rearrange the Recurrence Relation

**What I'm calculating:** Expressing the recurrence in a more useful form.

**Calculation:**
From the given recurrence: $a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$

Taking modular inverses (valid since 397 is prime):
$$\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} \equiv \frac{(a_n + 1)^2}{a_n} \pmod{397}$$

**Verification:** This is valid because we can multiply both sides by $(a_n + 1)^{-2}$ when $a_n \not\equiv 0$.

## Step 2: Find a Telescoping Property

**What I'm calculating:** A relationship that will allow summing the sequence.

**Calculation:**
$$\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{(a_n + 1)^2}{a_n} = \frac{a_n^2 + 2a_n + 1}{a_n}$$

$$= a_n + 2 + \frac{1}{a_n}$$

Rearranging:
$$\frac{1}{a_n} - \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = -a_n - 2$$

**Verification:** This relation is exact and will telescope when summed.

## Step 3: Sum the Telescoping Series

**What I'm calculating:** The sum from $i=0$ to $2025$.

**Calculation:**
Summing both sides from $i = 0$ to $i = 2025$:
$$\sum_{i=0}^{2025}\left(\frac{1}{a_i} - \frac{1}{a_{i+1}}\right) = -\sum_{i=0}^{2025}(a_i + 2)$$

The left side telescopes:
$$\frac{1}{a_0} - \frac{1}{a_{2026}} = -\sum_{i=0}^{2025}a_i - 2(2026)$$

Substituting $a_0 = 1$ and $a_{2026} = 9$:
$$1 - \frac{1}{9} = -\sum_{i=0}^{2025}a_i - 4052$$

$$\frac{8}{9} = -\sum_{i=0}^{2025}a_i - 4052$$

$$\sum_{i=0}^{2025}a_i = -\frac{8}{9} - 4052$$

**Verification:** The telescoping is correct; each middle term cancels.

## Step 4: Include $a_{2026}$ in the Total Sum

**What I'm calculating:** The full sum from $i=0$ to $2026$.

**Calculation:**
$$\sum_{i=0}^{2026}a_i = \sum_{i=0}^{2025}a_i + a_{2026} = -\frac{8}{9} - 4052 + 9$$

$$= -\frac{8}{9} - 4043$$

## Step 5: Compute Modulo 397

**What I'm calculating:** The answer modulo 397.

**Calculation:**

First, find $9^{-1} \pmod{397}$ using the extended Euclidean algorithm:
$$397 = 44 \times 9 + 1$$
$$1 = 397 - 44 \times 9$$

So: $9^{-1} \equiv -44 \equiv 353 \pmod{397}$

Therefore: $\frac{8}{9} \equiv 8 \times 353 = 2824 \pmod{397}$

$$2824 = 7 \times 397 + 45$$

So: $\frac{8}{9} \equiv 45 \pmod{397}$

Thus: $-\frac{8}{9} \equiv -45 \equiv 352 \pmod{397}$

Next, reduce $4043 \pmod{397}$:
$$4043 = 10 \times 397 + 73$$

So: $4043 \equiv 73 \pmod{397}$

Therefore: $-4043 \equiv -73 \equiv 324 \pmod{397}$

**Final calculation:**
$$-\frac{8}{9} - 4043 \equiv 352 + 324 = 676 \equiv 279 \pmod{397}$$

(Since $676 = 397 + 279$)

## Step 6: Verification

**Checking reasonableness:** 
- The answer 279 is an integer between 0 and 396 ✓
- The telescoping series properly telescopes from $a_0$ to $a_{2026}$ ✓
- Modular arithmetic is correctly applied ✓
