# Solution Plan

## 1. Establish the Constraint Condition
Determine the mathematical condition for a sphere of radius $r = 42$ centered at $(P_x, P_y, 42)$ to lie completely inside the hemisphere of radius $R = 200$.

- The farthest point on the small sphere from the origin is at distance $\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42$
- For the sphere to be inside the hemisphere: $\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42 \leq 200$

## 2. Simplify the Constraint
Solve the inequality algebraically to find the region $\mathcal{T}$:

- $\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} \leq 158$
- Square both sides: $P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$
- Simplify: $P_x^2 + P_y^2 \leq 23200$

## 3. Identify Region $\mathcal{T}$
Recognize that $\mathcal{T}$ is a disk centered at the origin with radius $\rho$:

- $\rho = \sqrt{23200}$
- Simplify: $\rho = \sqrt{23200} = \sqrt{400 \cdot 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$

## 4. Calculate the Area Ratio
Compute the ratio of areas:

$$\frac{\text{Area of } \mathcal{T}}{\text{Area of disk}} = \frac{\pi \rho^2}{\pi R^2} = \frac{\rho^2}{R^2} = \frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{232}{400}$$

## 5. Reduce to Lowest Terms
Simplify the fraction by finding $\gcd(232, 400)$:

- $232 = 8 \cdot 29$
- $400 = 8 \cdot 50$
- $\frac{232}{400} = \frac{29}{50}$
- Verify: $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓

## 6. Compute the Final Answer
$$p + q = 29 + 50 = \boxed{79}$$
