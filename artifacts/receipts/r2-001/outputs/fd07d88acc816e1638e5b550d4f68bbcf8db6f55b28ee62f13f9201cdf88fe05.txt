# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given quantities:**
- Triangle $ABC$
- Point $D$ on segment $\overline{BC}$
- $AB = 200$
- $AC = 225$
- Circle $\omega$ passes through $A$ and is tangent to segment $\overline{BC}$ at $D$
- Points $E \neq A$ and $F \neq A$ are the second intersections of $\omega$ with segments $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ respectively

**Given conditions:**
1. $\overline{AD}$ bisects $\angle BAC$ (i.e., $AD$ is the angle bisector from $A$)
2. All four quantities $AE$, $AF$, $BD$, and $CD$ are positive integers
3. $E$ lies on segment $\overline{AB}$ with $E \neq A$
4. $F$ lies on segment $\overline{AC}$ with $F \neq A$
5. $\omega$ is tangent to $\overline{BC}$ at $D$ (not just touching it elsewhere)

# What is Being Asked

Find the sum of all possible values of $BC$.
