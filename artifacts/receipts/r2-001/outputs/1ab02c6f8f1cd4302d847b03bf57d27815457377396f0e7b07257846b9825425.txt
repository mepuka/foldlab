# Solution

## Step 1: Recognize the algebraic form

Starting with $a + b + ab$ where $a, b \geq 1$ and $a \neq b$:

$$a + b + ab = a(1 + b) + b = a(1 + b) + (1 + b) - 1 = (a+1)(b+1) - 1$$

We need $(a+1)(b+1) - 1 \leq 100$, which gives us:
$$(a+1)(b+1) \leq 101$$

## Step 2: Determine the range for products

Let $m = a+1$ and $n = b+1$ where:
- $m, n \geq 2$ (since $a, b \geq 1$)
- $m \neq n$ (since $a \neq b$)
- $m \cdot n \leq 101$

We need to find all pairs $(m,n)$ satisfying these conditions.
