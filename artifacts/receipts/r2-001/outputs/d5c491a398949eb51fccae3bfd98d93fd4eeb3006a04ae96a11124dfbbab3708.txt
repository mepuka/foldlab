# Solution: Finding the Second Smallest Multiple of 101 Containing "2026"

**What I'm calculating:** 2026 mod 101

**Calculation:**
- 2026 ÷ 101 = 20 remainder r
- 101 × 20 = 2020
- 2026 - 2020 = 6
- Therefore: 2026 = 101 × 20 + 6

**Result:** 2026 mod 101 = 6, so 2026 is NOT divisible by 101 ✓
