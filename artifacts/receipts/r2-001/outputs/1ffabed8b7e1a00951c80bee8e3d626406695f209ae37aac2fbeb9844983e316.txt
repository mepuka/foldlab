# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities
1. $BC = 10$
2. $AD = 39$
3. $\angle AED = 120°$
4. Triangle $BEC$ is equilateral

## Given Conditions
1. Points $A, B, C, D$ are collinear and appear in that order on a line
2. Point $E$ exists (not on the line) such that:
   - $\angle AED = 120°$ (the angle at $E$ in triangle $AED$)
   - Triangle $BEC$ is equilateral (all sides equal, all angles $60°$)

## What Is Being Asked
Find the value of $|AB - CD|$ (the absolute value of the difference between the lengths of segments $AB$ and $CD$).
