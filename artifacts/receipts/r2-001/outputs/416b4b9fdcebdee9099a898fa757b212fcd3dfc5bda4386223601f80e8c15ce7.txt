# Plan to Solve for Minimum AB

1. **Set up the perimeter ratio equation**
   - Express Perimeter($\triangle ABC$) = $2c + b$
   - Express Perimeter($\triangle AIC$) in terms of $AI$, $IC$, and $AC = b$
   - Use the formula for distances from vertices to the incenter to get $AI$ and $IC$

2. **Derive the constraint equation**
   - Apply the ratio condition: $\frac{2c + b}{\text{Perimeter}(\triangle AIC)} = \frac{125}{6}$
   - Simplify to obtain a relationship between $b$ and $c$

3. **Determine the form of valid solutions**
   - The ratio constraint will give $2c + b = 125k$ and Perimeter($\triangle AIC$) = $6k$ for some positive integer $k$
   - Solve for expressions of $b$ and $c$ in terms of $k$

4. **Apply the triangle inequality constraints**
   - For $\triangle ABC$: Check $2c > b$ (isosceles condition)
   - For $\triangle AIC$: Verify that $AI$, $IC$, and $b$ form a valid triangle
   - These constraints limit the possible values of $k$

5. **Ensure all sides are integers**
   - Verify that for valid values of $k$, both $b$ and $c$ are positive integers
   - Verify that $AI$ and $IC$ (derived from the perimeter of $\triangle AIC$) are integers or compatible with integer side constraints

6. **Find the minimum value of c = AB**
   - For all valid values of $k$ satisfying the above constraints, calculate the corresponding $c$
   - Select the smallest positive integer value
