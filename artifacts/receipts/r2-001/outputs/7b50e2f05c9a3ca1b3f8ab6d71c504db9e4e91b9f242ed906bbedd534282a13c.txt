# Solution Plan for Finding Distance RS

## 1. **Establish the Tetrahedron Structure**
   - Identify that the tetrahedron has a special structure with two pairs of congruent isosceles triangular faces
   - Determine the vertex configuration and edge lengths
   - Set up a coordinate system to position the tetrahedron conveniently

## 2. **Find the Circumcenter S**
   - Use the condition that all four vertices are equidistant from $S$
   - Set up equations: $|S - V_i|^2 = R_{circum}^2$ for each vertex $V_i$
   - Solve the system to find coordinates of $S$ and the circumradius

## 3. **Calculate the Inradius and Insphere Center R**
   - Compute the volume $V$ of the tetrahedron (using coordinates or Cayley-Menger determinant)
   - Calculate the total surface area $A$ of all four faces
   - Use the formula: $r = \frac{3V}{A}$ for the inradius
   - Find the incenter $R$ using: $R = \frac{A_1V_1 + A_2V_2 + A_3V_3 + A_4V_4}{A_1 + A_2 + A_3 + A_4}$ where $A_i$ is the area of face $i$ and $V_i$ is the opposite vertex

## 4. **Compute the Distance RS**
   - Calculate $|RS| = \sqrt{(S_x - R_x)^2 + (S_y - R_y)^2 + (S_z - R_z)^2}$
   - Simplify to obtain a rational number $\frac{m}{n}$

## 5. **Verify and Finalize**
   - Confirm that $\gcd(m, n) = 1$
   - Compute and report $m + n$
