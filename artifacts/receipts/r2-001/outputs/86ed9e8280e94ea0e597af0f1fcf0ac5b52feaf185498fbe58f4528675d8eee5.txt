# Solution Plan

1. **Set up the time equations**
   - Let $t$ = time (in hours) that Patrick walks until arrival at the park
   - Tanya's travel time = $t - 1$ hours (since she starts 1 hour later)
   - Jose's travel time = $t - 2$ hours (since he starts 2 hours later)

2. **Write distance equations for each person**
   - Patrick's distance: $D = p \cdot t$
   - Tanya's distance: $D = (p + 2)(t - 1)$
   - Jose's distance: $D = (p + 9)(t - 2)$

3. **Set up equality equations**
   - Since all distances are equal to $D$, equate Patrick's and Tanya's distances:
     $$p \cdot t = (p + 2)(t - 1)$$
   - Equate Patrick's and Jose's distances:
     $$p \cdot t = (p + 9)(t - 2)$$

4. **Solve for $p$ using the first equation**
   - Expand and simplify to find Patrick's walking speed

5. **Solve for $t$ using the second equation**
   - Expand and simplify to find the total travel time for Patrick

6. **Calculate the distance**
   - Use $D = p \cdot t$ to find the distance from school to park

7. **Express in the form $\frac{m}{n}$**
   - Reduce the fraction to lowest terms (where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$)

8. **Calculate the final answer**
   - Add $m + n$
