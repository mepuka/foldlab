# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Setup:**
- A fair six-sided die (each face equally likely to be rolled)
- Six stickers labeled with integers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (one sticker per integer)
- A sequential process: for $i = 1$ to $6$, roll the die and place sticker $i$ on the top face

**Process:**
- When sticker $i$ is placed on the top face, it covers any sticker previously on that face
- This happens for stickers in order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

**Condition Given:**
- All even-numbered stickers (2, 4, and 6) are visible on faces of the die at the end of the process
- This means stickers 2, 4, and 6 were never covered by any subsequent sticker

**What is Being Asked:**

Find the conditional probability $p$ that **exactly one face is left blank** (i.e., exactly five of the six faces have stickers on them) **given that stickers 2, 4, and 6 are all visible**.

Express $p = \frac{m}{n}$ where $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, and find $m + n$.
