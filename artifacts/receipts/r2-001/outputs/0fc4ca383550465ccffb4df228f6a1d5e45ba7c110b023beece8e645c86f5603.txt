# Given Quantities and Conditions

## Starting and Ending Points
- Starting point: $(0,0,0)$
- Ending point: $(3,3,3)$

## Movement Rules
- In a single move, Kelvin can:
  - Increase the $x$-coordinate by 1, OR
  - Increase the $y$-coordinate by 1, OR
  - Increase the $z$-coordinate by 1, OR
  - Decrease the $z$-coordinate by 1

## Constraints
1. **No revisiting**: Cannot visit any point twice
2. **Coordinate bounds**: All coordinates must stay in the range $[0,3]$ (inclusive) at all times

## What is Being Asked

Compute the total number of distinct paths from $(0,0,0)$ to $(3,3,3)$ that satisfy all the above constraints.
