# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- $a, b, c$ are integers
- $c \le 2025$
- The equation $|x^2 + ax + b| = c$ has exactly 3 distinct integer solutions for $x$

## What is Being Asked

**Find:** The number of possible values of $c$ (over all valid choices of integers $a$ and $b$)

---

## Key Observations for Solution Setup

To solve this problem, one should recognize that:

1. The equation $|x^2 + ax + b| = c$ is equivalent to the two cases:
   - $x^2 + ax + b = c$, or
   - $x^2 + ax + b = -c$

2. For exactly 3 distinct integer solutions total, the parabola $y = x^2 + ax + b$ and the horizontal lines $y = c$ and $y = -c$ must intersect the parabola at points whose $x$-coordinates consist of exactly 3 distinct integers.

3. This typically occurs when:
   - One horizontal line is tangent to the parabola (touching at 1 point), and
   - The other horizontal line intersects the parabola at 2 distinct points

4. For the tangency condition, if the parabola touches $y = k$ at its vertex, then the vertex must have integer $x$-coordinate and $k$ must be an integer.
