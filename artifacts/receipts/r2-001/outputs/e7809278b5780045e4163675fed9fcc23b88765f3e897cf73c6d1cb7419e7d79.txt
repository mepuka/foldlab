# Solution Plan

1. **Take logarithms to simplify the radical**
   - Apply $\log_2$ to both sides of $\sqrt[20]{x^{\log_{2026}x}}=26x$
   - Convert the 20th root to a power: $\frac{1}{20}\log_2(x^{\log_{2026}x}) = \log_2(26x)$

2. **Simplify the left side using logarithm properties**
   - Use the power rule: $\frac{1}{20} \cdot \log_{2026}x \cdot \log_2 x = \log_2(26x)$
   - Expand the right side: $\log_2(26x) = \log_2 26 + \log_2 x$

3. **Convert logarithms to a common base**
   - Use change of base formula: $\log_{2026}x = \frac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 2026}$
   - Substitute to get: $\frac{1}{20} \cdot \frac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 2026} \cdot \log_2 x = \log_2 26 + \log_2 x$

4. **Let $y = \log_2 x$ and form a quadratic equation**
   - Rearrange into standard form: $\frac{y^2}{20\log_2 2026} - y - \log_2 26 = 0$
   - Multiply through to clear fractions

5. **Solve the quadratic equation**
   - Use the quadratic formula to find the two values of $y$
   - These correspond to $\log_2 x_1$ and $\log_2 x_2$

6. **Find the product of all solutions**
   - Convert back: $x_1 \cdot x_2 = 2^{y_1 + y_2}$
   - Use Vieta's formulas: sum of roots = $-\frac{b}{a}$

7. **Determine the prime factorization of the product**
   - Express $P = x_1 \cdot x_2$ in the form $p_1^{a_1} \cdot p_2^{a_2} \cdots p_k^{a_k}$

8. **Count divisors using the divisor formula**
   - Apply: $\tau(P) = (a_1 + 1)(a_2 + 1) \cdots (a_k + 1)$
