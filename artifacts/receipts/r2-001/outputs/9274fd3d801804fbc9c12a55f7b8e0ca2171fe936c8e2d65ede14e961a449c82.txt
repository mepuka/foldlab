# Solution Plan

1. **Set up a Venn diagram framework** with three overlapping circles representing Blue (B), Icy (I), and Large (L) moons.

2. **Identify the "exclusive" regions** from the given data:
   - Blue only (not icy, not large): 10 moons
   - Icy only (not large, not blue): 8 moons
   - Large only (not blue, not icy): 6 moons

3. **Add the exclusive regions:**
   - 10 + 8 + 6 = 24 moons accounted for

4. **Calculate the remainder:**
   - Total moons - moons in exclusive regions = 29 - 24 = 5 moons

5. **Determine what the remainder represents:**
   - The 5 remaining moons must occupy the overlapping regions (where two or more characteristics combine)
   - This includes moons that are blue AND icy AND large

6. **Conclude:** The number of moons that are blue, icy, AND large = **5 moons**
