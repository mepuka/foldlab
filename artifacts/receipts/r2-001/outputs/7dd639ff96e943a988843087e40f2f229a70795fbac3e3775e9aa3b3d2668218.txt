# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Squares:**
- $ABCD$ is a square
- $WXYZ$ is a square

**Point Locations:**
- $W$ lies on segment $\overline{AD}$
- $X$ lies on segment $\overline{AB}$
- $Y$ lies strictly inside $ABCD$
- $Z$ lies strictly inside $ABCD$

**Measurements:**
- $AW = 4$
- $AX = 5$
- $AB = 12$

## What is Being Asked

Compute the area of triangle $\triangle BCY$.

---

## Implicit Information to Determine

From the given measurements, we can deduce:
- Side length of square $WXYZ$ is $\sqrt{(AX-AW)^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{5^2 - 4^2} = \sqrt{25-16} = 3$

(This follows from the fact that $W$ and $X$ are adjacent vertices of the square $WXYZ$, with $W$ on $\overline{AD}$ and $X$ on $\overline{AB}$, and the constraint that $Y$ and $Z$ lie inside $ABCD$.)
