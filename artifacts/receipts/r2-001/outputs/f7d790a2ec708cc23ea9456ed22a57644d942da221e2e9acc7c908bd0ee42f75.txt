# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **n is a positive integer**
2. **n is divisible by 101** (i.e., 101 | n)
3. **The decimal representation of n contains 2026 as a consecutive sequence of digits** (i.e., 2026 appears as a substring in the decimal digits of n)

## What is Being Asked

Find the **second smallest positive integer** that satisfies both conditions 2 and 3 above. In other words:
- Identify all positive integers divisible by 101 whose decimal representation contains "2026" as consecutive digits
- Order these integers from smallest to largest
- Report the second element in this ordered sequence
