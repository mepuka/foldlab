# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given Quantities:**
- $n$ integers arranged in a circle
- At least one integer equals $0$
- The sum of all integers equals $24$

**Conditions:**
1. Each pair of adjacent integers (including the last and first, since they're in a circle) differs by exactly $1$
   - If integers $a$ and $b$ are adjacent, then $|a - b| = 1$

2. At least one of the $n$ integers is $0$

3. The sum of all $n$ integers is exactly $24$

# What is Being Asked

**Find:** The smallest possible value of $n$ (the number of integers in the circle) such that all three conditions can simultaneously be satisfied.
