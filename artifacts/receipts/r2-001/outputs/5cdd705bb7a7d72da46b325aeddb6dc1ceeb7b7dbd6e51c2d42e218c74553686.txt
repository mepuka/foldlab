# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Simplify the probability equation

**What we're calculating:** Expand the binomial coefficients and simplify the equation.

**Calculation:**

Starting with: $\binom{r}{4}\binom{b}{3} = \binom{r}{5}\binom{b}{2}$

Divide both sides to get:
$$\frac{\binom{r}{4}}{\binom{r}{5}} = \frac{\binom{b}{2}}{\binom{b}{3}}$$

For the left side:
$$\frac{\binom{r}{4}}{\binom{r}{5}} = \frac{r!/(4!(r-4)!)}{r!/(5!(r-5)!)} = \frac{5!(r-5)!}{4!(r-4)!} = \frac{5}{r-4}$$

For the right side:
$$\frac{\binom{b}{2}}{\binom{b}{3}} = \frac{b!/(2!(b-2)!)}{b!/(3!(b-3)!)} = \frac{3!(b-3)!}{2!(b-2)!} = \frac{3}{b-2}$$

Therefore:
$$\frac{5}{r-4} = \frac{3}{b-2}$$

**Verification:** Cross-multiplying: $5(b-2) = 3(r-4)$ ✓

---

## Step 2: Derive the constraint equation

**What we're calculating:** Solve for the relationship between $r$ and $b$.

**Calculation:**

From $5(b-2) = 3(r-4)$:
$$5b - 10 = 3r - 12$$
$$5b = 3r - 2$$
$$b = \frac{3r - 2}{5}$$

**Verification:** For $b$ to be an integer, we need $3r - 2 \equiv 0 \pmod{5}$, which means $r \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$. ✓

---

## Step 3: Apply validity constraints

**What we're calculating:** Find the minimum value of $r$.

**Calculation:**

From $b = \frac{3r - 2}{5} \geq 7$:
$$3r - 2 \geq 35$$
$$3r \geq 37$$
$$r \geq 12.33...$$

Combined with $r \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$ and $r \geq 13$:

The valid values are: $r \in \{14, 19, 24, 29, 34, 39, ...\}$
