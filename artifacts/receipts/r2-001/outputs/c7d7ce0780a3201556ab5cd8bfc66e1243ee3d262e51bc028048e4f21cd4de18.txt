# Given Quantities and Conditions

## Variables and Rates
- Patrick's walking speed: $p$ mph (constant)
- Tanya's running speed: $p + 2$ mph (constant)
- Jose's bicycling speed: $(p + 2) + 7 = p + 9$ mph (constant)

## Timeline and Departures
- Patrick departs from school at time $t = 0$
- Tanya departs from school at time $t = 1$ hour (1 hour after Patrick)
- Jose departs from school at time $t = 2$ hours (1 hour after Tanya, 2 hours after Patrick)
- All three travel from school to the park along the same straight road

## Key Condition
- All three people arrive at the park at the same time

## Distance
- The distance from school to the park is unknown (what we're solving for)

---

# What Is Being Asked

Find the distance from school to the park in the form $\frac{m}{n}$ miles, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, and then calculate $m + n$.
