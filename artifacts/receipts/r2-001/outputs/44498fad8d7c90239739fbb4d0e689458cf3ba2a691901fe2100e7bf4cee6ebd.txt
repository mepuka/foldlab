## Step 1: Calculate Total Paths
From $(0,0)$ to $(5,5)$ requires 5 moves right and 5 moves up.
$$\binom{10}{5} = \frac{10!}{5!5!} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6}{5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{30240}{120} = 252$$

## Step 8: Calculate Expected Value

$$\sum_{x=0}^{5}\sum_{y=0}^{5} \binom{x+y}{x}\binom{10-x-y}{5-x} = 6 \times 462 = 2772$$

$$E = 252 - \frac{2772}{36} = 252 - 77 = 175$$

**Verification:** $2772 ÷ 36 = 77$ ✓ and $252 - 77 = 175$ ✓
