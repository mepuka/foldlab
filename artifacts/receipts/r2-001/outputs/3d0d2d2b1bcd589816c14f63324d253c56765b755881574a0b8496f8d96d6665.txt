# Solution: Finding the Area of △BCY

## Step 1: State What I'm Calculating
I need to find the area of triangle BCY, where:
- B and C are vertices of square ABCD
- Y is a vertex of inscribed square WXYZ

## Step 2: Complete Calculation with All Intermediate Steps

**Coordinate System Setup:**
- $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (12, 0)$, $C = (12, 12)$, $D = (0, 12)$

**Locating W and X:**
- $W = (0, 4)$ on $\overline{AD}$ with $AW = 4$
- $X = (5, 0)$ on $\overline{AB}$ with $AX = 5$

**Finding the side length of square WXYZ:**
$$|WX| = \sqrt{(5-0)^2 + (0-4)^2} = \sqrt{25 + 16} = \sqrt{41}$$

**Finding Y and Z:**
The vector $\vec{WX} = (5, -4)$

Rotating 90° counterclockwise to get the next side: $(5, -4) \rightarrow (4, 5)$

Therefore:
- $Y = X + (4, 5) = (5, 0) + (4, 5) = (9, 5)$
- $Z = W + (4, 5) = (0, 4) + (4, 5) = (4, 9)$

**Verification that WXYZ is a square:**
All sides have length $\sqrt{41}$ and consecutive vectors are perpendicular. ✓

**Calculating Area of △BCY:**

With vertices $B = (12, 0)$, $C = (12, 12)$, $Y = (9, 5)$:

Using the shoelace formula:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|x_B(y_C - y_Y) + x_C(y_Y - y_B) + x_Y(y_B - y_C)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|12(12-5) + 12(5-0) + 9(0-12)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|12(7) + 12(5) + 9(-12)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|84 + 60 - 108|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|36| = 18$$

## Step 3: Verify Results Make Logical Sense

The side $\overline{BC}$ is vertical (from $(12, 0)$ to $(12, 12)$) with length 12.

The perpendicular distance from point $Y = (9, 5)$ to the line $x = 12$ is: $|12 - 9| = 3$

Using base-height formula:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 3 = 18$$ ✓

## Step 4: Check Answer by Substitution

The coordinates of Y satisfy the constraints of being part of square WXYZ inscribed in square ABCD. The triangle BCY has a reasonable area relative to the 12×12 square ABCD.

## Step 5: Confirm Final Answer

The answer 18 is:
- A reasonable integer
- Verified by two independent methods (shoelace formula and base-height)
- Geometrically sensible (3 units from B-C line, 12 units long)

**FINAL ANSWER: 18**
