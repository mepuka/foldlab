# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Points and distance**: A plane contains points $A$ and $B$ with $AB = 1$

2. **First rotation**: Point $A$ is rotated counterclockwise through an acute angle $\theta$ around point $B$ to get point $A'$
   - This means $BA' = BA = 1$ and $\angle ABA' = \theta$

3. **Second rotation**: Point $B$ is rotated clockwise through angle $\theta$ around point $A'$ to get point $B'$
   - This means $A'B' = A'B$ and $\angle BA'B' = \theta$

4. **Result condition**: $AB' = \frac{4}{3}$

5. **Constraint on $\theta$**: The angle $\theta$ is acute (so $0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}$)

## What is Being Asked

Find $\cos \theta$ expressed as $\frac{m}{n}$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, then compute $m + n$.
