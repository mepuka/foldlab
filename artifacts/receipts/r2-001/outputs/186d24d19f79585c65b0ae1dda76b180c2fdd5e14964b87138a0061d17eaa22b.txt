# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Analyze the Recurrence Relation

Given: $a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 \equiv a_n \pmod{397}$ with $a_{2026} = 9$ and $a_0 = 1$

Rearranging: $a_{n+1} \equiv a_n(a_n + 1)^{-2} \pmod{397}$ (since 397 is prime)

## Step 2: Find a Telescoping Property

Let me manipulate the recurrence to find a useful form. From:
$$a_{n+1}(a_n + 1)^2 = a_n$$

Dividing both sides by $a_n \cdot a_{n+1}$ (valid since these are nonzero):
$$\frac{(a_n + 1)^2}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}}$$

Expanding the left side:
$$\frac{a_n^2 + 2a_n + 1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}}$$

$$a_n + 2 + \frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}}$$

**Key telescoping relation:**
$$\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n} = a_n + 2$$

## Step 3: Sum from n = 0 to 2025

Summing both sides:
$$\sum_{n=0}^{2025} \left(\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n}\right) = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} (a_n + 2)$$

The left side telescopes:
$$\frac{1}{a_{2026}} - \frac{1}{a_0} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 2(2026)$$

## Step 4: Substitute Known Values

With $a_0 = 1$ and $a_{2026} = 9$:
$$\frac{1}{9} - \frac{1}{1} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 4052$$

$$\frac{1}{9} - 1 = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 4052$$

$$\frac{1 - 9}{9} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 4052$$

$$-\frac{8}{9} = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 4052$$

$$\sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n = -\frac{8}{9} - 4052$$

## Step 5: Include $a_{2026}$

$$\sum_{n=0}^{2026} a_n = \sum_{n=0}^{2025} a_n + 9 = -\frac{8}{9} - 4052 + 9 = -\frac{8}{9} - 4043$$

## Step 6: Convert to Modulo 397

**Find $9^{-1} \pmod{397}$:**

Using the extended Euclidean algorithm:
- $397 = 44 \times 9 + 1$
- $1 = 397 - 44 \times 9$

Therefore: $9^{-1} \equiv -44 \equiv 353 \pmod{397}$

Verification: $9 \times 353 = 3177 = 8 \times 397 + 1$ ✓

**Calculate $-\frac{8}{9} \pmod{397}$:**
$$-\frac{8}{9} \equiv -8 \times 353 \pmod{397}$$

$$8 \times 353 = 2824 = 7 \times 397 + 75 \equiv 75 \pmod{397}$$

$$-8 \times 353 \equiv -75 \equiv 322 \pmod{397}$$

**Calculate $-4043 \pmod{397}$:**
$$4043 = 10 \times 397 + 73 \equiv 73 \pmod{397}$$

$$-4043 \equiv -73 \equiv 324 \pmod{397}$$

## Step 7: Final Calculation

$$\sum_{n=0}^{2026} a_n \equiv 322 + 324 \equiv 646 \pmod{397}$$

$$646 = 397 + 249$$

$$646 \equiv 249 \pmod{397}$$

**Verification of key step:**
- $9 \times 353 = 3177 = 8(397) + 1$ ✓
- $2824 = 7(397) + 75$ ✓
- $322 + 324 = 646 = 397 + 249$ ✓

FINAL ANSWER: **249**
