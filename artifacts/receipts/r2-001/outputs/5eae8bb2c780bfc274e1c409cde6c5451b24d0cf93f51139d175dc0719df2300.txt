# Analysis of the Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **Urn contents:** $n$ marbles total
2. **Marble colors:** Each marble is either red or blue
3. **Color distribution constraint:** At least 7 marbles of each color
4. **Drawing process:** 7 marbles drawn randomly without replacement
5. **Probability condition:** P(exactly 4 red) = P(exactly 5 red)

Let me denote:
- $r$ = number of red marbles
- $b$ = number of blue marbles
- So $n = r + b$
- Constraint: $r \geq 7$ and $b \geq 7$

## What Is Being Asked

Find the sum of the **five smallest values of $n$** for which there exists a valid distribution of red and blue marbles satisfying all the above conditions.

## Mathematical Translation of the Probability Condition

The condition P(exactly 4 red) = P(exactly 5 red) means:

$$\binom{r}{4}\binom{b}{3} = \binom{r}{5}\binom{b}{2}$$

This is the key equation to solve for the relationship between $r$ and $b$, given that $r, b \geq 7$ and we seek all valid values of $n = r + b$.
