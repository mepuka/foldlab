# Step-by-Step Solution

## 1. Establish the Constraint Condition

We have a small sphere with radius $r = 42$ centered at $(P_x, P_y, 42)$ that must lie completely inside a hemisphere of radius $R = 200$ centered at the origin.

The center of the small sphere is at distance $\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2}$ from the origin. The farthest point on the small sphere is at distance:
$$d_{\max} = \sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42$$

**Constraint:** $\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} + 42 \leq 200$ ✓

## 2. Simplify the Constraint

$$\sqrt{P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2} \leq 158$$

Square both sides:
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 42^2 \leq 158^2$$

**Calculate the squares:**
- $42^2 = 1764$ ✓
- $158^2 = (160-2)^2 = 25600 - 640 + 4 = 24964$ ✓

Therefore:
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 + 1764 \leq 24964$$
$$P_x^2 + P_y^2 \leq 23200$$

**Verification:** $24964 - 1764 = 23200$ ✓

## 3. Identify Region $\mathcal{T}$

Region $\mathcal{T}$ is a disk centered at the origin with $\rho^2 = 23200$.

**Simplify** $\sqrt{23200}$:
$$23200 = 400 \times 58$$
$$\rho = \sqrt{400 \times 58} = 20\sqrt{58}$$

**Verification:** $(20\sqrt{58})^2 = 400 \times 58 = 23200$ ✓

## 4. Calculate the Area Ratio

$$\text{Area of } \mathcal{T} = \pi \rho^2 = 23200\pi$$
$$\text{Area of disk of radius } R = 200: \quad \pi R^2 = \pi(200)^2 = 40000\pi$$

**Ratio:**
$$\frac{\text{Area of } \mathcal{T}}{\text{Area of disk}} = \frac{23200\pi}{40000\pi} = \frac{23200}{40000}$$

## 5. Reduce to Lowest Terms

Factor both numerator and denominator:
- $23200 = 100 \times 232 = 100 \times 8 \times 29$
- $40000 = 100 \times 400 = 100 \times 8 \times 50$

$$\frac{23200}{40000} = \frac{100 \times 8 \times 29}{100 \times 8 \times 50} = \frac{29}{50}$$

**Verify lowest terms:**
- $29$ is prime
- $50 = 2 \times 5^2$
- Since 29 is prime and doesn't equal 2 or 5: $\gcd(29, 50) = 1$ ✓

**Check by substitution:** $\frac{29}{50} = 0.58$ and $\frac{23200}{40000} = 0.58$ ✓

## 6. Compute Final Answer

$$p = 29, \quad q = 50$$
$$p + q = 29 + 50 = 79$$

FINAL ANSWER: **79**
