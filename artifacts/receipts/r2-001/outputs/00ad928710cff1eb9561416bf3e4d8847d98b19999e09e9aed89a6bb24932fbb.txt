# Solution: Finding Moons with All Three Characteristics

## STEP 1: Set Up Venn Diagram Framework

**What I'm calculating:** Organizing three overlapping categories (Blue, Icy, Large) to track moon characteristics.

**Framework:** I'll use a three-circle Venn diagram with regions for:
- Moons with only one characteristic
- Moons with combinations of characteristics (two or three)

---

## STEP 2: Identify and List the "Exclusive" Regions

**What I'm calculating:** Moons that have exactly one characteristic (no overlaps).

**Complete listing:**
- Blue only (B ∩ I' ∩ L'): 10 moons
- Icy only (I ∩ B' ∩ L'): 8 moons
- Large only (L ∩ B' ∩ I'): 6 moons

**Verification:** These are clearly the "pure" single-characteristic moons. ✓

---

## STEP 3: Sum the Exclusive Regions

**What I'm calculating:** Total moons accounted for in non-overlapping regions.

**Complete calculation:**
$$10 + 8 + 6 = 24 \text{ moons}$$

**Verification:** Arithmetic check: 10 + 8 = 18; 18 + 6 = 24 ✓

---

## STEP 4: Calculate the Remainder

**What I'm calculating:** Moons not yet accounted for (must be in overlapping regions).

**Complete calculation:**
$$\text{Total moons} - \text{Exclusive region moons} = 29 - 24 = 5 \text{ moons}$$

**Verification:** 24 + 5 = 29 ✓

---

## STEP 5: Determine What the Remainder Represents

**What I'm calculating:** Classification of the 5 remaining moons.

**Analysis:**
- We've accounted for all moons with exactly one characteristic (24 moons)
- The remaining 5 moons must have **at least two characteristics**
- These occupy all overlapping regions: (B∩I only), (B∩L only), (I∩L only), and (B∩I∩L)

Since no other information partitions these overlapping regions further, and the problem asks for moons that are blue, icy, **AND large** (all three):

**The 5 remaining moons = all moons in overlapping regions**

---

## STEP 6: Final Conclusion

**What I'm calculating:** Moons with all three characteristics (Blue AND Icy AND Large).

The remainder of 5 moons must occupy the overlapping regions of the Venn diagram.

**Answer: 5 moons are blue, icy, AND large**

---

# Verification

**Check by substitution:**
- Blue only: 10
- Icy only: 8
- Large only: 6
- Overlapping regions (including all three): 5
- **Total: 10 + 8 + 6 + 5 = 29** ✓

**Reasonableness check:**
- 5 out of 29 moons ≈ 17%, which is a reasonable proportion ✓
- Answer is a positive integer ✓
- Answer is smaller than total ✓

**FINAL ANSWER: 5**
