# Analysis of the Competition Problem

## Given Quantities and Conditions

1. **The sum:** $S = \sum_{k=0}^{2026} k \binom{2k}{k} 2^k$
   - Summing from $k=0$ to $k=2026$
   - Each term is: $k$ times the binomial coefficient $\binom{2k}{k}$ times $2^k$

2. **The modulus:** 2027
   - Explicitly stated to be prime

## What is Being Asked

Compute $S \pmod{2027}$, that is, find the remainder when $S$ is divided by 2027 (equivalently, find the unique integer $r$ with $0 \le r < 2027$ such that $S \equiv r \pmod{2027}$).
