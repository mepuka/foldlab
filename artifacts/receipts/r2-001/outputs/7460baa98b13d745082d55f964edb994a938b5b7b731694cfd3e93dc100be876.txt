# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Set up time equations
- Patrick's time: $t$ hours
- Tanya's time: $t - 1$ hours (starts 1 hour later)
- Jose's time: $t - 2$ hours (starts 2 hours later)

## Step 2: Write distance equations
- Patrick: $D = p \cdot t$
- Tanya: $D = (p + 2)(t - 1)$
- Jose: $D = (p + 9)(t - 2)$

## Step 3: Set up equality equations

**Equation 1** (Patrick = Tanya):
$$p \cdot t = (p + 2)(t - 1)$$

**Equation 2** (Patrick = Jose):
$$p \cdot t = (p + 9)(t - 2)$$

## Step 4: Solve for $p$ using Equation 1

$$pt = (p + 2)(t - 1)$$
$$pt = pt - p + 2t - 2$$
$$0 = -p + 2t - 2$$
$$p = 2t - 2$$

**Verification**: If $p = 2t - 2$, then:
- LHS: $(2t - 2)t = 2t^2 - 2t$
- RHS: $(2t - 2 + 2)(t - 1) = 2t(t - 1) = 2t^2 - 2t$ ✓

## Step 5: Solve for $t$ using Equation 2

$$pt = (p + 9)(t - 2)$$
$$pt = pt - 2p + 9t - 18$$
$$0 = -2p + 9t - 18$$
$$2p = 9t - 18$$

Substitute $p = 2t - 2$:
$$2(2t - 2) = 9t - 18$$
$$4t - 4 = 9t - 18$$
$$14 = 5t$$
$$t = \frac{14}{5} \text{ hours}$$

## Step 6: Calculate $p$ and distance $D$

$$p = 2t - 2 = 2 \cdot \frac{14}{5} - 2 = \frac{28}{5} - \frac{10}{5} = \frac{18}{5} \text{ mph}$$

$$D = p \cdot t = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25} \text{ miles}$$

## Step 7: Verify all three people arrive at the same distance

**Patrick**: $D = \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

**Tanya**: 
- Speed: $\frac{18}{5} + 2 = \frac{28}{5}$ mph
- Time: $\frac{14}{5} - 1 = \frac{9}{5}$ hours
- Distance: $\frac{28}{5} \cdot \frac{9}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

**Jose**: 
- Speed: $\frac{18}{5} + 9 = \frac{63}{5}$ mph
- Time: $\frac{14}{5} - 2 = \frac{4}{5}$ hours
- Distance: $\frac{63}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{252}{25}$ ✓

## Step 8: Reduce to lowest terms and find $m + n$

$$D = \frac{252}{25}$$

Check if this is in lowest terms:
- $252 = 4 \cdot 63 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$
- $25 = 5^2$

Since $\gcd(252, 25) = 1$, the fraction is already in lowest terms.

Therefore: $m = 252$ and $n = 25$

$$m + n = 252 + 25 = 277$$

FINAL ANSWER: [277]
