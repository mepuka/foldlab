# Given Quantities and Conditions

**Given:**
- The number $17017^{17}$
- We need divisors of $17017^{17}$ that satisfy: $d \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$

**Implicit given information:**
- We need to determine the prime factorization of $17017$:
  - $17017 = 7 \times 11 \times 13 \times 17$
  - Therefore: $17017^{17} = 7^{17} \times 11^{17} \times 13^{17} \times 17^{17}$

**Conditions:**
- $d$ must be a positive divisor of $17017^{17}$
- $d \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$ (remainder is 5 when divided by 12)

This is equivalent to the system:
- $d \equiv 5 \pmod{3}$ (which means $d \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$)
- $d \equiv 5 \pmod{4}$ (which means $d \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$)

---

# What is Being Asked

**Find:** $N$ = the count of positive divisors of $17017^{17}$ that leave a remainder of 5 when divided by 12

**Then:** Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by 1000
