# Solution Plan

1. **Set up a coordinate system**
   - Place $A$ at the origin $(0, 0)$
   - Place $B$ at $(1, 0)$ since $AB = 1$
   - Place $D$ at $(0, h)$ where $h = BC$ (unknown)
   - Place $C$ at $(1, h)$

2. **Express positions of $X$ and $Y$ using parameters**
   - Let $X = (1, x)$ for some $0 \leq x \leq h$ (point on $\overline{BC}$)
   - Let $Y = (0, y)$ for some $0 \leq y \leq h$ (point on $\overline{AD}$)

3. **Apply the length ratio conditions**
   - Use $AX = k$: $\sqrt{1^2 + x^2} = k$
   - Use $XY = 2k$: $\sqrt{1^2 + (x-y)^2} = 2k$
   - Use $YC = k$: $\sqrt{1^2 + (h-y)^2} = k$

4. **Apply the perpendicularity conditions**
   - Use $\angle AXY = 90°$: $\vec{XA} \cdot \vec{XY} = 0$
   - Use $\angle XYC = 90°$: $\vec{YX} \cdot \vec{YC} = 0$

5. **Solve the system of equations**
   - From conditions in steps 3 and 4, establish relationships between $x$, $y$, $h$, and $k$
   - Eliminate parameters to find $h$

6. **Verify the solution**
   - Check that all five conditions are satisfied
   - Confirm that $X$ and $Y$ lie within their respective segments
