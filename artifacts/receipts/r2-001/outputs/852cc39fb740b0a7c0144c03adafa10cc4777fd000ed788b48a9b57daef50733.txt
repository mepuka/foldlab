# Problem Analysis

## Given Quantities and Conditions

**Total number of moons:**
- 29 moons total

**Classification:**
- Each moon is blue, icy, or large (or some combination)

**Specific counts:**
1. 10 moons are blue but not icy
2. 8 moons are icy but not large
3. 6 moons are large but not blue

## What is Being Asked

Find the number of moons that are **simultaneously blue, icy, and large** (i.e., possess all three characteristics at once).
