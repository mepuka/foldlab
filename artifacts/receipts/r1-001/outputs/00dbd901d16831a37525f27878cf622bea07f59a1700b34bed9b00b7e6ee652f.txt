# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 19 × 8 × 7 = 1,064 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defective rate: 1,064 × 0.16 = 170.24 defective parts (or approximately 170 defective parts)
