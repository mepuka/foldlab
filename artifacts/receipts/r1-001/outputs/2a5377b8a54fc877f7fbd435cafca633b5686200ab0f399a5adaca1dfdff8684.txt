# Execution of Two-Step Plan

## Step 1: Calculate Total Number of Parts

12 pallets × 3 boxes/pallet = 36 boxes

36 boxes × 4 parts/box = 144 parts

**Total parts = 144**

## Step 2: Calculate Number of Defective Parts

144 parts × 0.05 = 7.2 defective parts

**Defective parts = 7.2**

**Rounded to whole number = 7 defective parts**
