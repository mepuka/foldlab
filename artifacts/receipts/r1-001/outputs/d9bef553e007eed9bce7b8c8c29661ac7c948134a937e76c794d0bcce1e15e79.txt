# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 14 × 6 × 11 = 924 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defective percentage: 924 × 0.11 = 101.64 ≈ **102 defective parts** (rounded to the nearest whole number)
