# Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts:**
   Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 37 × 6 × 5 = 1,110 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts:**
   Multiply the total number of parts by the defective percentage: 1,110 × 0.10 = 111 defective parts
