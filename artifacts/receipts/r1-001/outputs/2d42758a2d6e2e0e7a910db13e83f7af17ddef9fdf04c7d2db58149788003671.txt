# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 28 × 7 × 6 = 1,176 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total parts by the defective percentage: 1,176 × 0.13 = 152.88 ≈ **153 defective parts**
