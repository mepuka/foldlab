# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts:** Multiply the number of pallets (26) by boxes per pallet (4) by parts per box (10) to determine the total quantity of parts.
   - Total parts = 26 × 4 × 10 = 1,040 parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts:** Multiply the total number of parts (1,040) by the defective rate (7% or 0.07) to determine how many parts are defective.
   - Defective parts = 1,040 × 0.07 = 72.8
