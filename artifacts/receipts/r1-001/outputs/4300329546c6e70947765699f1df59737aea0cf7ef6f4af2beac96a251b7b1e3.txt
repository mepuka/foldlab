# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply: 47 pallets × 10 boxes per pallet × 8 parts per box = 3,760 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total parts by the defect rate: 3,760 parts × 0.20 (20%) = 752 defective parts
