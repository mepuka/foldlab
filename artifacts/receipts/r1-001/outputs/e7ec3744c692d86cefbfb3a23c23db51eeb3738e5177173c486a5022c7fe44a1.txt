# Checking the Arithmetic

## Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts

23 pallets × 5 boxes per pallet = 115 boxes ✓

115 boxes × 10 parts per box = 1,150 total parts ✓

## Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts at 8 percent

1,150 total parts × 0.08 = 92 defective parts ✓

**All steps are correct. Answer: 92 defective parts**
