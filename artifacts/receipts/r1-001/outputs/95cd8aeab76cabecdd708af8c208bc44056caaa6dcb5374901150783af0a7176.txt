# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply: 14 pallets × 6 boxes per pallet × 11 parts per box = 924 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of damaged parts**
   - Multiply the total by the damage percentage: 924 × 0.11 = 101.64 damaged parts (or approximately 102 damaged parts if rounding to the nearest whole number)
