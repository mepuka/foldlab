# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets (40) by boxes per pallet (5) by parts per box (5)
   - Total parts = 40 × 5 × 5 = 1,000 parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts (1,000) by the defective rate (9%)
   - Defective parts = 1,000 × 0.09 = 90 defective parts
