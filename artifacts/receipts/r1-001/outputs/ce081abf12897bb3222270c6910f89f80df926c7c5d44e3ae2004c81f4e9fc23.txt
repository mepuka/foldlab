# Execution of Plan: Finding the Number of Defective Parts

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**

18 × 3 = 54

54 × 14 = 756

**Total parts in shipment = 756 parts**

**Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts**

756 × 0.23 = 173.88

173.88 ≈ 174

**Number of defective parts = 174 defective parts**
