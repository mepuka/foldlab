# Execution of the Defective Parts Problem

## Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment

25 × 8 = 200

200 × 6 = 1,200 total parts

## Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts

1,200 × 0.14 = 168 defective parts

---

**Answer: There are 168 defective parts in the shipment.**
