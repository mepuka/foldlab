# Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts:** Multiply the number of pallets (12) by boxes per pallet (3) by parts per box (4) to determine the total quantity: 12 × 3 × 4 = 144 total parts.

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts:** Apply the 5 percent defective rate to the total by multiplying 144 × 0.05 = **7.2 defective parts** (or approximately 7 defective parts if rounding down to whole units).
