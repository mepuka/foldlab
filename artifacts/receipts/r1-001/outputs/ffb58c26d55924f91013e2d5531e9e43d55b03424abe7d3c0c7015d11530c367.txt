# Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 42 × 8 × 12 = 4,032 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total parts by the defective rate: 4,032 × 0.15 = 604.8 defective parts (or approximately 605 defective parts when rounded)
