# Executing the Two-Step Plan

## Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment

47 pallets × 10 boxes per pallet = 470 boxes

470 boxes × 8 parts per box = 3,760 total parts

## Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts

3,760 total parts × 0.20 = 752 defective parts

**Answer: There are 752 defective parts in the shipment.**
