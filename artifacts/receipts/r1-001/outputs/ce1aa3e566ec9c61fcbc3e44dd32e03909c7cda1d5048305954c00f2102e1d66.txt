# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 44 × 11 × 8 = 3,872 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defect rate: 3,872 × 0.21 = 813.12 (or approximately 813 defective parts)
