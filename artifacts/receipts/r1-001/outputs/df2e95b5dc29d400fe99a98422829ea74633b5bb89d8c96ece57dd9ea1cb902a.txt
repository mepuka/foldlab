# Execution of Defective Parts Problem

## Step 1: Calculate total number of parts in the shipment

42 × 8 = 336

336 × 12 = 4,032

**Total parts in shipment: 4,032**

## Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts

4,032 × 0.15 = 604.8

**Defective parts: 604.8 (or 605 when rounded to nearest whole number)**

## Summary
- **Total parts:** 4,032
- **Defective parts:** ~605 parts (604.8 before rounding)
