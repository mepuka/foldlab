# Executing the Plan

## Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment

14 pallets × 6 boxes per pallet = 84 boxes

84 boxes × 11 parts per box = 924 total parts

## Step 2: Calculate the number of damaged parts

924 total parts × 0.11 = 101.64 damaged parts

Rounded to the nearest whole number: **102 damaged parts**

---

## Summary
- **Total parts in shipment:** 924 parts
- **Damaged parts:** 102 parts (or 101.64 if using the exact decimal)
