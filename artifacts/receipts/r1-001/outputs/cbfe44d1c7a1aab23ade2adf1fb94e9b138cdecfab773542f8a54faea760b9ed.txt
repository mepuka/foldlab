# Two-Step Plan to Find Non-Defective Parts

1. **Calculate the total number of parts:**
   - Multiply: 21 pallets × 11 boxes per pallet × 14 parts per box = 3,234 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of non-defective parts:**
   - Since 22% are defective, 78% are non-defective
   - Multiply: 3,234 × 0.78 = **2,522.52 non-defective parts**

*(Note: In practice, this would round to 2,523 non-defective parts since partial parts aren't possible.
