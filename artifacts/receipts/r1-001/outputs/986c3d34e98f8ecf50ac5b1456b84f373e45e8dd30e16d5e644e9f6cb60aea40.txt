# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 26 × 4 × 10 = 1,040 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defect rate: 1,040 × 0.07 = 72.8 defective parts (or approximately 73 defective parts when rounded)
