# Plan to Find the Number of Defective Parts

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box:
- 18 pallets × 3 boxes × 14 parts = 756 total parts

**Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts**
Multiply the total parts by the defective percentage:
- 756 parts × 0.23 = 173.88 ≈ **174 defective parts** (rounded to nearest whole number)
