# Two-Step Plan to Find Defective Parts

1. **Calculate the total number of parts:**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box
   - Total parts = 12 pallets × 3 boxes/pallet × 4 parts/box = 144 parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts:**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defect rate (5%)
   - Defective parts = 144 parts × 0.05 = **7.2 defective parts**

*Note: Since you cannot have a fractional part, this would round to approximately **7
