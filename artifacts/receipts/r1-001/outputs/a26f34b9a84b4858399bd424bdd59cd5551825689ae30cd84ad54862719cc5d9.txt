# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 40 × 5 × 5 = 1,000 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defective rate: 1,000 × 0.09 = 90 defective parts
