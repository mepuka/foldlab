# Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 33 × 9 × 13 = 3,861 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total parts by the defect rate: 3,861 × 0.18 = 694.98 ≈ 695 defective parts
