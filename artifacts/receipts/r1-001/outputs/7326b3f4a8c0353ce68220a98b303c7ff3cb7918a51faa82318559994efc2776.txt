# Plan to Solve the Defective Parts Problem

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of parts**
Multiply the number of pallets by the number of boxes per pallet by the number of parts per box:
- 35 pallets × 3 boxes × 9 parts = 945 total parts

**Step 2: Calculate the number of defective parts**
Multiply the total number of parts by the defective rate:
- 945 total parts × 0.24 (24%) = 226.8 defective parts
- *Round to 227 defective parts* (or 226 if rounding down)
