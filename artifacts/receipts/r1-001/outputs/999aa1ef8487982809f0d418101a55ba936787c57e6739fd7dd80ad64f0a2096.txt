# Two-Step Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets × boxes per pallet × parts per box
   - 30 pallets × 10 boxes × 13 parts = 3,900 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defective percentage
   - 3,900 parts × 0.19 (19%) = 741 defective parts
