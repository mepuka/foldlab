# Plan to Solve the Problem

1. **Calculate the total number of parts in the shipment**
   - Multiply the number of pallets by boxes per pallet by parts per box: 16 × 9 × 7 = 1,008 total parts

2. **Calculate the number of defective parts**
   - Multiply the total number of parts by the defective percentage: 1,008 × 0.17 = 171.36 (or approximately 171 defective parts)
