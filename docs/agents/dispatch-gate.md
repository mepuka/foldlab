# The dispatch gate: board acts are gated like code

The standing estate laws (root `AGENTS.md`) gate what lands. This file
gates what gets **cut and dispatched** — the seam the laws cannot reach,
because a mis-cut ticket mints its damage before any PR exists. Every
seat and every coordinator applies this gate when creating a ticket,
when assigning one, and when reviewing a breakdown. Violations are
findings, never style notes.

## The lane test (apply first)

One lane is active: the machine-generated type kernel is the core, and
the estate is cutting over to it. Before cutting any ticket, answer in
the body:

> **Does this work move the cutover?** Concretely: does it convert
> ticketed-debt public types to generated-core-derived (the executed
> DEV-796 walk's count is the meter), route judgment through the one
> door, replace a hand-authored surface with a generated one, or harden
> a wall that proves one of these?

Work that does not move the cutover needs a stated reason to exist now.
"Useful," "interesting," and "worth evaluating" are not reasons to
dispatch; they are reasons to write a research note.

## The admission test rides every public-surface ticket, verbatim

The operator-authored API skill
(`.claude/skills/estate-api-development/`) rules the mechanism: seats
don't load skills — the ticket text is how the discipline reaches
them. A ticket touching any public surface carries that skill's
six-question admission test pasted verbatim into its body, plus the
authorities it names. The ratification questions below are the
board-level complement (they gate the ticket's existence; the
admission test gates its surface), and neither substitutes for the
other.

## The ratification questions (answerable in every ticket body)

A ticket that cannot answer these is not dispatchable:

1. **What type universe does this work speak?** The corpus and its
   generated projections, or a staged-debt type wearing its unification
   ticket. The truth primitives are in the second class until they
   converge under the generated core; they are not a third floor. A
   ticket that introduces any other type universe is refused at cutting
   time.
2. **Who ratified the vocabulary it uses?** New kinds, sorts, brands,
   refusal reasons, or notation cite their ruling (a grill record, a
   K/KM/AE row, an ADR). Unratified vocabulary in a ticket body is a
   proposal wearing a work order's clothes — route it to a grill.
3. **Which law licenses each capability it builds?** The lawful-surface
   rule (ADR-0010) applied at cutting time: name the theorem, law
   family, or ruling. A capability with no license is either carriage
   (fence it) or a missing ruling (grill it) — never a dispatch.

## Third-party type universes

A framework or library whose types twin corpus concepts (models,
messages, candidates, results) is a Law 1 hazard by construction:

- **Evaluation is research, never dispatch.** It produces a note with
  the library's shapes, maturity, and correspondence to the estate's
  constructs — elements and approaches, no adoption.
- **Adoption requires a grill row** ruling the correspondence and the
  waiver shape for the twin types, before any build ticket names the
  library.
- A dispatched ticket that would `bun add` a new type universe without
  that ruling is refused at review of the breakdown, and cancelled if
  found running.

## The vertical-slice standard

A vertical slice takes **ratified types through existing machinery to a
measured wall**. That is the whole definition. Consequences:

- A slice that re-proves an existing theorem "at a new layer" while
  minting untraced types inverts its own value: the theorem was already
  proven, and the types are new debt. Refused.
- A slice's evidence includes the wall it moves: a gate turned green, a
  count moved on the type-universe walk, a generated surface replacing
  a hand-written one — never only "it runs."
- Demonstration slices for un-ruled designs are scratch spikes under
  Law 9's discipline: briefs and spikes only, graduate or die, and they
  are cited by the grill they inform, not dispatched as build work.

## The dispatch checklist (before `assign`)

- The body carries the tracker contract (`issue-tracker.md`) **and**
  answers the three ratification questions.
- `Build against` is pinned to current `main` at dispatch time, not at
  cutting time.
- Every `Blocked by` edge is real; an unblocked ticket in the active
  lane dispatches rather than queues (seats run concurrent tasks).
- Evidence commands are runnable on the pinned commit — verified, not
  assumed.
- Review dispatches name their authorities and verdict form; build
  dispatches name their seams.

## Coordinator addendum

The coordinator applies this gate to its own cuts first. History: this
file exists because a coordinator-cut slice (DEV-791) dispatched an
unratified third-party type universe mid-cutover and was cancelled by
operator direction. The gate is the mechanical form of that lesson.
