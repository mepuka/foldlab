# The search

Every frame below was rendered, opened at the 900px width a reader actually
gets, and judged there. Only the 900px previews are kept — the full-size
exports were the input to that judgement, not the evidence of it.

The two earlier poster rounds were rejected by the operator as "completely cut
off" and then "still completely incoherent and difficult to understand". Both
had visualised the data. Neither had told the story. So the standard applied to
every frame here was the wordless test: cover the labels, look at the picture
alone, and see whether the fork and the two fates still read.

| frame | why it was not the poster |
| --- | --- |
| `01-fork-two-fates` | The shape of the idea was right, but the record was grand and the structure was a tiny pile of blocks off in a corner — two fates that plainly were not equals. The caption also sat on top of the wordmark. |
| `02-fan-broken-shard` | Rebalanced onto the bisecting lane and gave the broken event a detached piece so it reads as damaged, not merely orange. Still had the caption colliding with the wordmark at the bottom edge. |
| `03-footer-row` | Wordmark moved to the top corner and the two bottom runs of type put on one baseline. The stack still read as four squares standing near each other rather than as one thing that was built. |
| `04-built-structure` | Blocks closed up onto a plinth, and the stack finally read as built. Full-size inspection then showed the broken shape had lost so much of its outline that it was no longer recognisably the same square as the others, and the trajectory ran straight through the middle of the shape it was supposed to be carrying. |
| `05-broken-corner` | Corner break tightened and a mask cut the trajectory around the shape. The hop now departed from a bare stretch of ground for no visible reason. |
| `06-departure` | Departure given a proper lift off the ground. The wordless test on this frame is where the real gap showed: nothing on the lower path connected the arriving shapes to the structure they became. |
| `08-late-skip` | Hop moved to the last moment, at the foot of the structure, so it reads as *this one declined to join* rather than *this one left early*. The broken shape still had no core, so it read as a dark blob rather than as one of the family. |
| `09-family-resemblance` | Broken shape given the same core the sound ones carry. Closest yet; still no visible delivery on the lower path. |
| `10-five-second-test-wordless` | Not a candidate — the instrument. The same frame with every word switched off, which is the only honest way to ask whether the picture carries the idea on its own. |
| `11-delivery` | Two shapes in transit on the ground, drawn in the outline the stream uses rather than the solid the stack uses. This is the accepted composition; the shipped frame differs only by a three-pixel nudge that sets those two down on the ground line instead of just above it. |

## The four siblings

Same standard and the same instrument: every frame was opened at 900px and
judged with the words switched off. The prefixes are `ca` cut-anywhere, `rv`
refusal-is-a-value, `rl` register-linearizes, `jl` journal-linearizes.

| frame | why it was not the poster |
| --- | --- |
| `ca-01-two-columns` | The two-column shape was right — the long way on the left, the short way on the right, both answers standing on one shelf — but the snip was a thin line in a ten-pixel gap and read as a divider between two runs rather than as a cut through one. The equals was too small to carry the claim it was making. |
| `ca-02-wider-cut-wordless` | Standoff widened, faint ticks lengthened at the gaps that were not taken, equals enlarged. This frame is where the real defect showed: both streams enter on lines that are not there. A gradient in bounding-box units on a horizontal `<line>` has a box of zero height and paints nothing, so every straight lane had been silently absent — from these frames and from the flagship. All of them are now stated in user space. |
| `rv-01-gate-and-card-wordless` | The card read and the gate did not: two floating pills with clear ground above and below, and you can walk around a pillar. The handback also landed its arrow in mid-air short of the card, and the arrival lane was missing to the same gradient defect. |
| `rv-02-full-wall-wordless` | Wall lengthened, arrival restored, the whole thing tightened into one anticlockwise loop. The wall still ended inside the frame at both ends, so it still read as an object in a room rather than as the boundary of one. It now leaves through the top and bottom edges. |
| `rl-01-bar-and-bounce` | The bar and the bounce read at once, which was the good news. The bar was too short to be a condition of the world, the late claimant's approach swept so low that it crossed the motto, and the climb arrow was drawn straight through the middle of the label naming it. |
| `rl-02-wide-fence-wordless` | Bar taken nearly frame-wide on two ratchet posts, approach lifted clear of the footer, labels moved off the arrow. The falling shape then came to rest exactly on a ghost bar, which reads as landing on the old fence — the one thing this poster denies. It now falls squarely between two of them. |
| `jl-01-strip-and-hop-wordless` | Strip, contest and bounce all read. The hop away from the lost cell died in the air near the next cell instead of arriving in it, so nothing said the loser had actually got in. The hop was raised, given a head, and landed on the next cell's centre line. |
