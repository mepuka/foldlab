# Estate

This page is a projection of the estate's law, never its source. Every rule
lives in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and the owning docs; open
[AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) whole before acting — conduct, procedures, and
routing are there, not here.

## Conduct handles (C1–C7)

Handles only; the letter of each law is in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

- **C1 propose-before-act** — propose actions, await operator assent
- **C2 shared understanding** — divergence is a stop condition
- **C3 mode discipline** — conception and proof registers differ
- **C4 ratification** — grilling gates docs and formal
- **C5 claim discipline** — soundness words link their judgment
- **C6 provenance** — external assertions carry resolved pins
- **C7 legibility** — outsider-legible surfaces, jargon glossed

## Done means

Judge each flow against its binary observable. The procedure that gets you
there is in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md); the vocabulary in
[docs/lab-core/CONTEXT.md](../../../docs/lab-core/CONTEXT.md).

- **Minted** ⇔ the owning CONTEXT.md carries an entry with name, artifact
  kind, carrier or judgment form, obligations, and avoid-list.
- **Claimed** ⇔ the statement carries its highest satisfied gate stamp G0–G6
  ([docs/effect-typescript-semantics/CLAIM-GATES.md](../../../docs/effect-typescript-semantics/CLAIM-GATES.md))
  and every soundness word links a named judgment or theorem.
- **Produced** ⇔ the thing carries a kind from
  [docs/lab-core/KINDS.md](../../../docs/lab-core/KINDS.md), sits under
  `experiments/` or `formal/`, and `mise run gen` leaves a clean tree.
- **Promoted** ⇔ a commit moves the material from `.staging/` into a graded
  home, and the grade change is a declared transformation.
- **Admitted** ⇔ [docs/lab-core/TOOLS.md](../../../docs/lab-core/TOOLS.md)
  carries the tool's row — role and trust statement — before its output lands
  in gated work.
