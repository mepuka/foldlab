## The headline: the aggregator theorem is corroborated three independent ways

`Panel.all` preserves compositionality; `Panel.any` does not
(kernel-checked today, `Judge.lean`). The same asymmetry appears:

1. **Geometrically** (Xiong §3.2, App. B): the concept meet is an
   exact half-space intersection with a closure proof; the join
   CANNOT close — "approximated by the conic hull," no bound.
2. **Model-theoretically** (Gregory–Prest, derivable from their
   Def. 3.5 closure properties): definable classes are closed under
   PRODUCTS, so a product of one witness from each panel member lies
   in neither class — a union of definable classes is definable only
   under containment. Stronger than our monoid argument: it
   generalizes to infinite panels.
3. **Empirically-by-omission** (Asperti §3.5, §4.3.2 fn. 5): the
   field's headline metric, pass@k (Kimina: pass@8192), IS union
   aggregation — the morphism-destroying aggregator — and majority
   vote sits strictly between ∩ and ∪, covered by neither theorem.
   The benchmark literature is innocent of the distinction.

## The amendment — two independent votes to re-rank the lattice (PROPOSED, ask J8)

Both the survey and the algebra paper, from unrelated directions, say
`Compositional` sits too low:

- Asperti (MathConstruct, fn. 18): average 53.77% vs ROBUST 34.92% —
  judges are unstable under structure-preserving variation, and
  `Compositional` PRESUPPOSES that stability.
- Gregory–Prest (§1, Thm 3.2): the right closure condition includes
  closure under DIRECT LIMITS — "does acceptance survive the limit of
  a growing chain of accepted prefixes?" — exactly what a
  bounded-context judge on unbounded input fails; the monoid law is a
  proper fragment of it. Asperti's length cliff (§6.3.1: >90% inside
  the trained band, ~0% past it) is the falsification protocol.

## The theorem backlog (ranked; RUN-003 scout material)

1. **T-J1 codomain**: no monoid morphism into `(Bool, ∧)` can express
   progress — two elements, no order; "closer to accepted" is not in
   the image (Asperti §5.2.2/§7.3 named the bottleneck; we have the
   algebra). Fix: graded judge into an ordered monoid — `([0,1], min)`
   first, Bool as the sharp limit (converges with Xiong §3.1's α→∞).
2. **T-J2 brittleness ideal**: under `Compositional`, J is
   constant-false on the ideal generated by any rejected word — the
   one-line formal content of the smooth-vs-brittle error landscape
   (Asperti §7.1), stated as a theorem WITH its warning attached.
3. **T-J3 preservation/faithfulness split**: state the naming theorem
   in two halves per Gregory–Prest Thm 2.1 — homomorphism always;
   EMBEDDING (distinct structures ⇒ distinct names) only under a
   unique-decomposition side condition. `names.json`'s Nodup guards
   are the empirical face; the theorem is owed.
4. **T-J4 finite-panel compactness**: if an expensive judge's
   acceptance region is covered by cheap judges' regions, finitely
   many suffice (Ziegler-open compactness, G–P Def. 3.4).
5. **T-J5 complexity transport**: we hold three structure-preserving
   maps (naming, fold, defunctionalization) and transport NO invariant
   along any of them; Cor 2.2 is the template — pick one invariant per
   map, prove monotonicity with direction.
6. **T-J6 fold-law capacity**: the honest fold law carries a domain
   restriction (G–P Remark 4.8: a finite source cannot totally
   interpret an infinite target) — state it rather than inherit it as
   a surprise.
7. **T-J7 limit-stability**: define it, relate it to `Compositional`
   (proper-fragment relation), give the falsification protocol.

stored content, prove the derived object invariant).

**Refuse**: wild / tame / domestic as loose adjectives (load-bearing
representation-type terms); "interpretation functor" as a synonym for
"translation" (Thm 3.2 makes it an iff; loose use destroys what the
word buys); soft subsumption / degree-of-equality (heuristics dressed
as judgments); the conic-hull join (unbounded approximation); naive
Curry–Howard as a store justification (Asperti §7.1: buggy programs
inform, buggy proofs do not — the store must say what it does about
that asymmetry).

**Transport warnings**: Xiong's Galois connection is ANTITONE —
store-union corresponds to concept-MEET; every theorem moved across
that bridge flips polarity. His name monoid is the powerset
(commutative, idempotent); ours is free — nothing transports without
checking the invariant factors through the quotient. And G–P's
recovery lives in the eq+ closure of derived sorts — a registry closed
under nothing cannot host its recovery theorem; state which derived
sorts decoders may use.
