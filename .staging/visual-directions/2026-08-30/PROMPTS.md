# Visual direction prompt library

Each concept has three treatments: **A** is a 1969-1976 collective-print interpretation, **B** is a 1981-1988 personal-computing interpretation, and **C** is a contemporary editorial reinterpretation. Every prompt assumes an original composition with no project name, logos, readable copy, recognizable book cover, or watermark.

## 01. Underground technology collective

- **A - selected:** Waist-level documentary view of five women collaborating in a 1972 community media workshop around a portable video camera, CRT, patch bay, punch tape, and paper-covered table. Unbleached paper, carbon black, signal magenta, oxide red; coarse newsprint, photocopy grain, hand-cut masks, and misregistered screen print. Handwritten lambda terms, type trees, and signal-flow arrows.
- **B:** High oblique view over a 1984 cooperative computer lab where people pass cables and cards between beige terminals and a small CRT wall. IBM beige, phosphor green, ultraviolet, hot coral, midnight navy; flash-lit 35mm photography, scanlines, and postmodern geometric overlays. Recursive windows, luminous lambdas, parentheses, and circuit traces.
- **C:** Wide architectural tableau of a present-day cooperative studio built from period equipment, with people small among stacked CRTs and cable grids. Bone, aubergine, acid chartreuse, digital cyan, vermilion; crisp editorial photography interrupted by risograph dots, torn-paper shadows, and translucent planes. Abstract lambdas, recursive screens, and composition arrows.

## 02. Algorithmic plotter field

- **A:** Straight-on scan of warm paper covered by a hand-plotted square lattice that destabilizes until its gaps imply a lambda. Warm ivory, India ink, vermilion, muted mustard; technical pen, plotter chatter, ink pooling, registration crosses, and deckled fibers. Fold lines and tiny composition arrows.
- **B - selected:** Isometric grid plunging toward a lambda-shaped discontinuity while wireframe tiles lift toward the viewer. Laser white, CRT cyan, electric violet, safety orange, near black; early vector-plotter lines, restrained airbrush glow, dot-matrix banding, and coated-stock grain. Nested functions, recursion spirals, and Cartesian coordinates.
- **C:** Macro photograph of real paper beneath a pen plotter; black lines accumulate at one edge and dissolve into sparse colored rules at the other, revealing a lambda only from a distance. Chalk white, graphite, Klein blue, fluorescent persimmon; real ink, blind emboss, and one translucent overprint.

## 03. Counterculture computing collage

- **A - selected:** Flatbed view of a 1974 paste-up radiating from a homemade terminal, with punch cards, hands, cables, arrows, comic mechanical creatures, Mobius strips, recursion trees, flowcharts, and geometric scraps. Acid yellow, cobalt, tomato red, carbon black, newsprint cream; cut paper, wax pencil, halftones, staples, tape shadows, and screen-print misregistration.
- **B:** Exploded axonometric arrangement of terminal, keyboard, modem, floppies, and abstract diagrams floating against a strict grid broken by playful postmodern objects. Aqua, raspberry, sunflower, royal blue, cool gray; airbrush, technical illustration, dithering, and glossy print reflections. Lambda cursor trails, recursive windows, and geometric combinator birds.
- **C:** Deep shadow-box collage photographed at a 30-degree angle, layering period components, translucent acrylic symbols, folded diagrams, and archival-looking fragments. Off-white, ink black, burnt sienna, electric lilac, signal green; tactile paper, acrylic, laser-cut card, and subtle generative patterns.

## 04. Imaginary functional-programming monograph

- **A:** Centered robed figure in rough profile opens a small black box from which recursive trees and lambdas rise like cut-paper smoke. Unbleached cream, oxblood, deep indigo, antique gold; woodcut lines, flat screen print, worn clothbound grain, and slight ink trapping. Combinators, parentheses, binary branches, and morphism arrows.
- **B - selected:** Enigmatic figure seen from behind at a severe low angle opens a geometric box projecting wireframe staircases, recursive windows, evaluation planes, a fixed-point spiral, and one floating lambda. Midnight blue, muted plum, brass gold, phosphor green; period gouache and airbrush, technical linework, scanlines, and laminate scuffs.
- **C:** Cropped overhead view of two hands opening a paper reliquary whose vellum folds form an abstract lambda above infinite descending chambers. Charcoal, parchment, aubergine, copper foil, pale mint; studio still life, embossed paper, foil fragments, and risograph grain.

## 05. Technical-catalog futurism

- **A:** Orthographic exploded view of a modular computer assembled from translucent colored shells, cards, knobs, and cables, surrounded by asymmetrical inset diagrams. Warm white, ultramarine, vermilion, lemon yellow, smoke gray; hand-drafted ink, flat spot color, and aged catalog paper. Lambda wiring paths and product-and-sum shapes.
- **B - selected:** Three-quarter studio view of a fictional translucent desktop computer floating over a razor-clean grid while its modular components fan out behind a cyan CRT. Optical white, CRT cyan, ultramarine, coral, brushed silver; immaculate 1980s catalog airbrush, chrome highlights, technical ruling, and offset grain.
- **C:** Panoramic macro view through stacked translucent components producing prismatic depth around a distant cyan display. Frost white, cobalt glass, neon coral, pale cyan, titanium; photoreal acrylic and anodized metal with precise diagrammatic overlays. Lambda-shaped light paths and branching modules.

## 06. Cooperative computing workshop

- **A:** Eye-level candid view across a crowded circular worktable where five collaborators trade punch cards, solder a homemade terminal, and sketch on butcher paper. Mustard, rust, olive, faded denim, warm cream; documentary 35mm translated through newsprint halftones and imperfect overprinting.
- **B:** Low three-quarter angle from beside a translucent keyboard toward a diverse group working at glowing CRTs. Electric cyan, coral, ultraviolet, charcoal, phosphor green; saturated transparency film, airbrushed highlights, scanlines, and restrained postmodern overlays.
- **C - selected:** Overhead documentary photograph of expressive hands arranging an old beige terminal, circuit boards, graph paper, coffee cups, plants, and bright folded-paper models. Marigold, oxblood, cobalt, moss, bone; contemporary editorial photography with risograph grain. Patch cables form a loose lambda.

## 07. Functional-systems graphic

- **A:** Flat frontal poster where an oversized white lambda slices through an irregular hand-ruled grid and composition arrows radiate to the margins. Vermilion, soot black, warm cream, dusty teal; letterpress ink, fibrous stock, pencil construction marks, and slight misregistration.
- **B - selected:** A monumental lambda assembled from stacked geometric planes, surrounded only by abstract arrows, dots, grids, punched squares, and empty terminal-like rectangles. Signal orange, cobalt, graphite, cool gray, white; crisp 1984 offset print, spot-color blocks, dot-matrix texture, and hard asymmetry.
- **C:** Extreme close crop of a sculptural white lambda casting a long shadow across a modular grid, with one edge escaping the frame. Tomato red, chalk white, anthracite, pale concrete, acid lime; precise geometry combined with embossed paper and subtle ink bleed.

## 08. Mathematical-computing still life

- **A:** High oblique view over a scratched communal desk where punch cards spill from a beige terminal beside graph paper, an oscilloscope, pencils, solder, and a brass lambda token. Tobacco, avocado, amber, parchment, deep black; grainy tungsten 35mm and muted magazine inks.
- **B:** Desk-level macro past reflective floppy disks toward a green CRT and brushed-metal oscilloscope, with diagonal perspective and shallow focus. Phosphor green, midnight navy, amber, mauve, chrome; cinematic slide film, scanlines, hard flash, and subtle airbrushed accents.
- **C - selected:** Carefully composed top-down still life with one empty quadrant: restored terminal, brass lambda, punched cards, folded type-tree diagram, translucent cable, and oscilloscope. Walnut, moss, oxidized brass, ivory, cobalt; natural-window editorial photography with dust, patina, and restrained grain.

## 09. Optical lambda void

- **A - selected:** Hand-drawn black-and-white bands buckle inward around a radiant lambda-shaped absence while concentric circles and recursive grids drift off register. Fluorescent orange, turquoise, violet, dense black, paper white; hand-pulled screen print, saturated ink, rough paper tooth, and trapping errors.
- **B:** Tilted wireframe tunnel whose vanishing point forms a lambda, surrounded by concentric pixel rings and checkerboard fragments. Hot magenta, electric cyan, ultraviolet, black, icy white; early vector-computer graphics translated to glossy offset print with stair-stepping and CRT bloom.
- **C:** Extreme close-up of layered paper bands physically folded around a crisp lambda aperture and photographed at a raking angle. Carbon black, warm white, safety orange, ultramarine, pale lavender; paper sculpture combined with generative linework and subtle moire.

## 10. Shared-knowledge linocut

- **A - selected:** Slightly elevated view of many differently aged hands passing books, punched cards, cables, drafting tools, and small devices around a shared circular table, with no leader. Brick red, ochre, coal black, cream; rough linocut, heavy carved marks, uneven ink, cheap poster stock. Connected hands and cables imply a central lambda.
- **B:** Steep overhead view of hands entering from every edge toward a faceted round table, with terminals, floppies, books, cables, and rulers forming an asymmetric pinwheel. Coral, teal, sunflower, royal blue, black; chunky woodcut forms, postmodern geometry, halftones, and fluorescent accents.
- **C:** Close overhead photograph of a real shared table where hands exchange a punched card, folded paper model, pencil, cable, and circuit board. Terracotta, marigold, ink blue, sage, warm white; documentary photography overlaid with sparse carved contours and risograph fields.

## 11. Constructivist functional notation

- **A - selected:** Dynamic overhead view of functional notation transformed into communal industrial machinery; arrows, folds, product crosses, sum branches, type trees, parentheses, and lambdas interlock along forceful diagonals. Scarlet, bone, carbon black, dusty teal; torn-paper collage, hand-cut masks, coarse screen print, and visible ink overlap.
- **B:** Low oblique view across a gridded drafting table where geometric notation becomes a bright personal-computer interface and angular machine. Laser red, computer beige, electric cyan, matte black, mauve gray; airbrush, technical pen, transfer-tone dots, and glossy print grain.
- **C:** Wide axonometric exploded view of a modular function factory whose notation becomes architectural components with generous negative space. Signal orange, graphite, warm white, mineral blue, pale lilac; crisp vector geometry, scanned fibers, risograph noise, and embossed shadows.

## 12. CRT lambda dreamscape

- **A:** Eye-level view into a dim community computer room where stacked terminals form a rough semicircle around an anonymous operator. Phosphor green, soot black, tobacco, amber; pushed film translated to two-color halftone with scratched emulsion and uneven ink.
- **B - selected:** Floor-level three-quarter view through a canyon of stacked CRTs; smoked glass reflects repeating emerald lambdas and a branching evaluation tree while a seated programmer remains a silhouette. Deep charcoal, phosphor emerald, amber LED, smoked silver, midnight violet; cinematic 35mm, scanlines, bloom, grain, and practical haze.
- **C:** Centered frontal stack of vintage monitors treated as a sculptural monolith, jointly forming one fragmented lambda in deep negative space. Near black, acid green, soft apricot, oxidized aluminum; detailed studio photography with tactile dust, fingerprints, and controlled glow.

## 13. Photocopy math zine

- **A - selected:** Close overhead view of a chaotic paste-up table with exploded terminal diagrams, abstract algebra marks, fingerprints, coiled cables, one smiling profile, and hand-drawn arrows colliding around a large off-center lambda. Toner black, newsprint cream, fluorescent pink; repeated photocopy degradation, wax pencil, tears, staples, tape shadows, and misregistered overprint.
- **B:** Tilted crop of a desktop terminal exploded into diagrammatic fragments, with a profile portrait trapped in a halftone circle and cables cutting across a checkerboard grid. Xerox black, hot magenta, copier blue, pale gray; photocopy, abstract transfer blocks, dot screens, and jagged paste-up edges.
- **C:** Disciplined vertical collage with a dense central column and wide empty margins; archival terminal parts and human gestures overlap translucent mathematical marks. Soft black, chalk white, neon rose, sage; scanned paper, toner dust, vector annotations, blind emboss, and fluorescent risograph accents.

## 14. Recursive paper sculpture

- **A:** Slightly elevated photograph of handmade cream cardstock unfolding across a communal table into recursive stairs, tiny terminals, branching type trees, and folded lambda structures. Unbleached cream, cobalt, marigold, brick red; practical craft, screen-printed tabs, pencil guides, daylight, and film grain.
- **B:** Low macro angle through a pop-up paper city of tiny beige computers and nested lambda arches. Computer beige, ultramarine, lemon, coral, cool gray; immaculate studio photography with period airbrush retouching, crisp shadows, and print grain.
- **C - selected:** Top-down architectural photograph of one continuous sheet folded into recursive staircases, modular computers, nested lambdas, and a circular evaluation path. Warm ivory, Klein blue, citron, vermilion, lavender shadows; museum-quality paper sculpture, precise die cuts, tactile fibers, and natural studio light.

## 15. Cosmic algebra landscape

- **A:** Low horizon beneath an immense lambda constellation, with lattices, type trees, and polyhedra rising like communal monuments while transformations arc overhead. Deep indigo, burnt orange, dusty rose, turquoise, pale gold; gouache, stippled ink, screen-printed stars, and visible brush texture.
- **B - selected:** Upward view from a neon-edged geometric plain toward a glowing lambda constellation; crystalline polyhedra and wireframe type trees recede into an indigo sky. Midnight indigo, electric turquoise, dusty magenta, solar orange, starlight cream; period airbrush, gouache highlights, technical wireframes, and offset grain.
- **C:** Distant aerial view of a spare abstract terrain whose rivers trace transformations and ridges form type trees, with a restrained off-center lambda constellation. Ink blue, terracotta, faded coral, mineral teal, moonlit ivory; layered digital gouache, scanned pigment, delicate grain, and selective geometric linework.
